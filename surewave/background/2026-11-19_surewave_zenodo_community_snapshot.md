## Observations against the WP8 KPI tracker and deliverable list

- Public deliverables not in the community on this date: D5.7 modelling tools validation, D6.3 fatigue / connection performance, D6.4 long-term degradation, D7.2 technological assessment, D8.2 societal needs, and Sunlit Sea's own public WP8 deliverables D8.1, D8.5 and D8.3 Technical Brochure. D8.1 is on Zenodo outside the community (10.5281/zenodo.8278574), as are an earlier D2.1 record and the April 2024 wave-basin test dataset (10.5281/zenodo.10960431); found through OpenAIRE on the same day.
- Record 10, "SUREWAVE FPV System Design" (2024-05-31, Kegelart), is most likely D3.4 Summary of global system design. D3.4 is Sensitive (SEN) in the Grant Agreement; if this is D3.4, its open publication should be confirmed with the coordinator. CORDIS, however, lists D3.4 among the public deliverables.
- Peer-reviewed open-access papers in the KPI tracker that are not mirrored here: the three Energies papers (wave basin tests 2024, aerodynamic breakwater protection 2024, MARIN hydrodynamic simulations 2026), Sensors 2024 (eddy-current sensor) and Electronics 2024 (UWB accelerometer nodes). They remain open access at the publisher, so this matters only if the consortium wants Zenodo as the single index.
- Record 4 lists Aritz Dorronsoro and José M. Martínez-Esnaola but not Unai Eibar, who is first author in the KPI tracker, and the DOI field carries a stray "Digital" suffix.
- Name-order errors in creator fields: records 7 and 17.
- The D7.4 dataset (record 17) is the most-viewed and most-downloaded record in the community.
- The KPI tracker has no Zenodo or open-data KPI line. For the final technical report's open-science section, the community supports the claim that public deliverables and datasets were shared openly under CC BY 4.0, with the gaps listed above.
