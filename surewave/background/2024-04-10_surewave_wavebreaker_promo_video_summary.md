# "SUREWAVE - wavebreaker and floating solar" video, 10 April 2024 — summary

> Discover the future of sustainable energy at sea: floating solar systems safeguarded by pioneering wavebreakers, at the forefront of eco-friendly design. Strategically installed against the waves, these barriers protect the installation and preserve its integrity. Beyond protection, the barriers serve as anchoring points for the solar panels, giving stability and security, and double as platforms for essential equipment. These innovations enable offshore solar installations previously limited by wave conditions. The future is here, and it's brighter than ever.

Points made: the breakwater protects the floating PV against waves, wind, boats and debris; it is the anchoring point for the PV field and a platform for equipment; it opens sites previously ruled out by wave conditions.
