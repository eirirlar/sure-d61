# Horizon Results Booster — KERs profile template

Structure of a Key Exploitable Result (KER) profile published on the Horizon Results Platform (HRP) / EU Innovation Platform. Issued as part of the Horizon Results Booster support. One profile per KER.

For Sunlit Sea the SUREWAVE Key Exploitable Results currently envisaged for HRP publication are KER1 (integrated nearshore floating PV platform, owner Sunlit Sea), KER3 (concrete floating breakwater, owner Clement Germany) and KER6 (structural health monitoring platform, owner Ceit). Each needs its own filled profile.

When filling, save each copy as `YYYY-MM-DD_hrp_ker_profile_kerN.md` under `surewave/deliverables/` and keep this template in `background/` untouched.

Sections 1–4 apply to every profile. Section 5 appears only when the Section 1 target-audience selection includes one of three investor-facing audiences (see Section 5 below).

---

## Section 1 — Identification and ask

- **Result title.** Concise public name of the KER as it will appear on the platform listing.
- **Teaser to potential user.** Short hook written for a prospective user or buyer, not for an EU reviewer. What it is, what problem it solves, why it is credible.
- **Link to your video.** Short explainer video URL (YouTube or Vimeo typically).
- **Typology of KER.** Category: product, service, methodology, data, software, process, or other.
- **Target Audiences.** Platform-side taxonomy selection. Options include industrial users, SMEs, public authorities, research community, policy-makers, public or private funding institutions, other actors who can help fulfil market potential, private investors.
- **What you look for.** Type of engagement sought: co-development partners, customers, licensees, investors, standards contributors, policy input.

## Section 2 — Attribution and credentials

- **Other related projects.** Other EU or national projects the KER builds on or depends on (SuRE, SUREWAVE, Enova forprosjekt, Skattefunn, etc.).
- **Result contributors.** Consortium partners who contributed to generating the result.
- **Owners for exploitation.** Partner(s) owning the IP and taking the result to market.
- **Start-up created for further exploitation.** Spin-out name and link, if applicable.
- **Your Logo.** Organisation logo file for the owner-for-exploitation.
- **Testimonials / references.** Quotes or reference deployments from users or stakeholders.

## Section 3 — Description and framing

- **Result description.** Detailed description of the KER: what it is, how it works, what is novel.
- **Business Sector(s) / Policy Area(s).** Sectors the result applies to and relevant EU policy areas (Green Deal, REPowerEU, Industrial Strategy, Blue Economy, etc.).
- **Tags / Keywords.** Searchable terms for the HRP catalogue.
- **Your result's Contribution to Sustainable Development.** SDGs addressed; climate, circular-economy or social impact.

## Section 4 — Maturity, roadmap and value proposition

- **KER maturity (if applicable).** TRL or equivalent stage.
- **Current stage and next steps (if applicable).** Where the KER is now and near-term exploitation roadmap.
- **Customised questions about your customers.** Customer-side questions the profile owner wants to surface. Content typically drawn from the Market Definition Canvas and Value Proposition Canvas for the KER.
- **Unique Value Proposition.** One-sentence statement in the Strategyzer ad-lib pattern: *Our [product] helps [segment] who want to [job] by [reducing pain] and by [enabling gain] — unlike [competing solutions].*

## Section 5 — Investor and funding audience (conditional)

Appears when the Section 1 target-audience selection includes any of:

- Public or private funding institutions
- Other Actors who can help us fulfil our market potential
- Private Investors

The platform then surfaces a set of specialised questions relevant to those audiences — investment-readiness detail that the general public listing does not display.
