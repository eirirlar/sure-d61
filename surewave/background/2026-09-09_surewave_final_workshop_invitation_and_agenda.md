# SUREWAVE final workshop, 30 September 2026 — invitation and draft agenda

Invitation sent by Balram Panjwani (SINTEF) on 9 September 2026, with the draft agenda attached (agenda last edited 9 September 2026). No recording of the workshop has been found.

## Invitation

- Title: SUREWAVE Webinar/Workshop: Sustainable Concrete for Marine Applications.
- Date and time: Wednesday 30 September 2026, 11:00–13:00.
- Format: hybrid, in person in Trondheim or online on Microsoft Teams. In-person participants were invited to lunch at the hotel from 13:00 to 14:00.
- Organiser: Balram Panjwani (SINTEF). 27 guests invited, including the speakers, SUREWAVE partners (SINTEF, MARIN, Clement Germany, Ceit, ACCIONA, IFEU, Sunlit Sea) and external participants from Holcim and Aker Solutions.
- Open to a wider audience: recipients were asked to share the invitation with colleagues interested in sustainable concrete, marine infrastructure, offshore applications and related research.
