# SUREWAVE communication material, 2023 — descriptions

Four print and promotional items made by Sunlit Sea (WP8 communication) in June–September 2023. The source files are in the SUREWAVE Teams folder held by SINTEF; this file describes them so they can be referred to without the binaries. Used as evidence for the WP8 KPIs (factsheets, flyer, roll-up).

## 1. Roll-up artwork — 5 June 2023

Artwork for the SUREWAVE roll-up, 85 × 200 cm, made in InDesign and printed by Østfold Trykkeri for the June 2023 trade fairs. Counted under roll-ups.

Content, top to bottom: SUREWAVE logo on a blue wave background; the headline "Floating PV project utilizing Innovative Offshore Breakwater Concept"; the line "SUREWAVE aims to solve the main challenges of offshore FPV by developing a solution adapted to the most critical sea states, being able to mitigate the harsh conditions for the FPV"; an "Our Project" block with four icon panels:

- Effort to diversify the technology portfolio to take maximum advantage of the vast potential offered by EU's seas to become climate neutral by 2050.
- The main objective: an innovative FPV system with an external floating breakwater made of new circular materials, protecting the FPV against severe wave loads and allowing higher operational availability and energy output.
- Consortium: SINTEF, MARIN, Sunlit Sea, Ceit, ACCIONA, Clement Germany, IFEU.
- Project duration three years, kick-off October 2022.

Footer: "Visit www.surewave.eu", LinkedIn and Twitter icons, EU emblem and the standard EU funding disclaimer.

## 2. Project overview — 30 June 2023

Two A4 pages made in InDesign. Counted both as the flyer and as a factsheet (Eirik, 22 Nov 2026).

Page 1, Project summary: SUREWAVE develops and tests a floating PV system with an external floating breakwater that protects the PV modules against severe wave, wind and current loads, increasing operational availability and energy output. The project optimises aero- and hydrodynamic behaviour, structural integrity, connections, mooring and anchoring, and overall efficiency, to meet mechanical, electrical and cost requirements with high reliability, long component life and easy construction, installation and operation. Key objectives:

1. New circular concrete materials for the breakwater: cost-effective, easy to produce, good mechanical and durability properties, low carbon footprint.
2. A predictive computational modelling and simulation framework combining aero- and hydrodynamic, structural integrity and material-property modelling with structural health management, to reduce CAPEX and OPEX.
3. A testing and validation methodology for offshore FPV to reach TRL 5: laboratory, basin-model and marine-environment tests, including resilience to corrosion and biofouling.

Figure 1-1 shows the conceptual approach. The page closes with the partners' roles: offshore wind floating structures and modelling (SINTEF, MARIN, Ceit), FPV in calm waters (Sunlit Sea), floating solutions (Clement), circular materials (ACCIONA), and social, environmental and economic assessment (IFEU).

Page 2, System description: the breakwater solution (individual breakwater units joined by flexible mechanical connections into a long floating structure; the connections keep the distance between breakwater and PV, minimise force transfer and allow for the different dynamic behaviour of breakwater and PV), with Figures 1-2 (breakwater) and 1-3 (connection between units); and the floating PV solution (prefabricated units, each a PV panel sealed to an aluminium float, delivered by Sunlit Sea in a matrix configuration protected by the breakwaters). Footer: www.surewave.eu, LinkedIn, project duration three years, coordinator contact (Balram Panjwani, SINTEF), press/marketing contact (Christoffer Isdahl, Sunlit Sea), partner logos and the EU funding disclaimer.

## 3. BRIDGE brochure 2023 entry — 15 August 2023

SUREWAVE's two-page entry (pages 149–150) in the 2023 brochure of BRIDGE, the European Commission initiative that groups Horizon projects on smart grids, storage, islands and digitalisation. Written by Christoffer Isdahl (Sunlit Sea) in the brochure's standard project-sheet layout. Counted as a factsheet.

Page 149: call topic HORIZON-CL5-2021-D3-03-10 (innovative foundations, floating substructures and connection systems for floating PV and ocean energy devices); long title "Structural reliable offshore floating PV solution integrating circular concrete floating breakwater"; one-paragraph summary; duration 1 Oct 2022 – 30 Sep 2025 (as planned at the time); project total cost and EU contribution both given as 3.515 M€; website; technologies deployed (technologies for consumers: floating PV solution, PV array integration; generation technologies: innovative energy generation solution); map of partner countries; coordinator SINTEF and the six other partners.

Page 150: project description (objective, design conditions of wind above 25 m/s, current above 1.2 m/s and waves up to 14 m height); context (three pillars: breakwater concept, circular materials, predictive modelling); scope (a complete offshore FPV system validated at TRL 5, scalable to plants of about 50 MWp); the work plan in three blocks of work packages; and expected impacts as stated in the proposal (jobs, households supplied, installed MW, clean energy and avoided CO2 by 2032, and percentage reductions in over-engineering, fabrication, installation and O&M costs). The impact figures are proposal targets from 2022, not project results.
