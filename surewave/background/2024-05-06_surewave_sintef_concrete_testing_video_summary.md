# SUREWAVE video on concrete testing at SINTEF, May 2024 — summary

Short video (about 1 min 36 s) with a single speaker, Virgile Delhaye (senior scientist, SINTEF Industry), filmed in SINTEF's laboratory during the mechanical testing of the breakwater concrete. Published by SINTEF at about the time of recording; the video file is dated 6 May 2024. The tests described match those reported in D4.2 (laboratory testing of the breakwater prototype, July 2024): small breakwater prototypes with a high-performance or lightweight aggregate concrete shell around a cellular lightweight concrete core, tested in three-point bending with digital image correlation.

Narration:

> My name is Virgile Delhaye, I'm a senior scientist at SINTEF Industry. We are leading a European project called SUREWAVE, which develops an innovative floating solar farm surrounded by a ring of breakwaters that absorb part of the wave energy coming towards the solar modules. In the project, SINTEF works on the technical validation of the floating solar farm solution, and in particular we test the structural properties of the concrete developed in the project for the breakwater.
>
> A very important part of the project is to develop new sustainable concrete solutions with our partners, and we must make sure that they meet the structural requirements for extreme conditions. SINTEF therefore does several mechanical tests, as you can see here, to check the structural properties of the different concrete formulations. Here you see the concrete shells, which have a very high structural performance, and the concrete filling in the middle, which has a very low density and no structural role of its own.
>
> One of the tests we use to qualify the structural performance is a bending test, as you can see here. The specimens are monitored with digital image correlation (DIC), so that we can track the displacement and strain fields during the test. With this test we can see when the material fails, and we can use the results to feed the numerical models we are also developing in the project.

Points made: SINTEF coordinates SUREWAVE and does the technical validation; the breakwater combines a structural concrete shell with a low-density, non-structural core; bending tests with digital image correlation show when the material fails and give data for the numerical models.
