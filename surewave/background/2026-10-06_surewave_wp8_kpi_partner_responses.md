# SUREWAVE WP8 KPI quality check — partner responses

Email exchange, 30 September – 6 October 2026. Eirik Larsen (Sunlit Sea, WP8 lead) asked all SUREWAVE partners to list their SUREWAVE-related dissemination and communication activities so the WP8 KPI tracker could be checked. Replies came from Ceit (Aritz Dorronsoro Larbide and Ainhoa Cortés, separately), MARIN (Joep van der Zanden), ACCIONA (María Aurora Casado Barrasa) and Clement Germany (Thomas Pehlke). The exchange holds no reply from SINTEF or IFEU.

Partner answers are reproduced as sent. Email signatures, confidentiality disclaimers and the quoted copies of the request have been left out. Partners who answered inline (Aritz, ACCIONA, Clement) wrote under each question; their answers are given here under the matching category.
