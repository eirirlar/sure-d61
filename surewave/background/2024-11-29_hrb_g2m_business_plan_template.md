# Executive Summary

<span class="mark">This is the introductory and most important section of the Business Plan, as it provides a summary of the future entrepreneurial project.</span>

<span class="mark">It clearly, logically, and concisely presents the most relevant information about the company, the goals to be achieved, and the sustainability of your business idea.</span>

<span class="mark">Recommended length: 1-3 pages.</span>

<span class="mark">It is advisable to draft this section last, once each chapter has been thoroughly completed and analysed in all its parts. Provide the most important elements of each chapter.</span>

# The organisation

<span class="mark">Provide an in-depth description of your organisation, its short and long term objectives, strengths and weaknesses and success factors.</span>

<span class="mark">Describe the fundamental characteristics starting from the mission.</span>

# The product/service

<span class="mark">This section is dedicated to the description of the products/services offered by your organisation, their in-depth illustration and plans for the future.</span>

<span class="mark">It is mandatory to specify whether the products/services are already existing and marketed, ready to be placed on the market (indicating the launch date). In case the products/services are being developed, indicate the time to market.</span>

<span class="mark">Make a clear indication of the current products/services and the future products/services.</span>

# The market

<span class="mark">Describe the market and the sector your will target.</span>

<span class="mark">Specify the size, stage of development and competitive nature of the sector. Describe the barriers to entry and development in the sector, the role that innovation and technological change play in the sector, how the sector is affected by political and regulatory events.</span>

<span class="mark">Here you have to include a market analysis (if available), describing the behaviours and needs of your customers and end-users and your competitors (actual and potential).</span>

<span class="mark">Specify here also your unique value proposition.</span>

# The business model and marketing strategy

<span class="mark">Describe here how you are planning to make money out of your business, specifying all different actors involved.</span>

<span class="mark">The marketing strategy should include an in-depth analysis of the methods of distribution of products/services and the definition of the guidelines along which you would like to proceed in the medium-long term to achieve your objectives.</span>

<span class="mark">You should be able to explain why you selected a specific market segment, why you decided to target some early adopters. The marketing strategy includes also your pricing strategy, the sales and promotion channels you are going to use, the amount you intend to invest in advertising.</span>

# The Team and management structure

<span class="mark">Brief presentation of the key components of the management and ownership: qualifications, specializations, experience (including previous positions). It is important to briefly indicate the successes achieved.</span>

<span class="mark">Describe the functional responsibilities of the team (list the various functions, responsibilities, expected future changes). Specify the functions still to be assigned, or if there are important functional areas still vacant, then indicate the measures to be undertaken and the expected times.</span>

<span class="mark">Describe the management and decision-making policy of the (future) organisation.</span>

<span class="mark">Include here also any relevant partnership.</span>

# The operative plan

<span class="mark">This is a detailed program with which you will let all your stakeholders know how you intends to achieve your objectives. This plan provides details on operational methods and approaches that will transform the theoretical principles developed in the previous parts into short-term operational practice.</span>

<span class="mark">Elements that you should address are: research and development, required facilities, resource requirements, outsourced production, needed investments processes, stock control.</span>

# Financials

<span class="mark">Specify here your financial objectives using the most common indicators (turnover, break even point, ebitda, etc.)</span>

<span class="mark">Provide realistic and organic projections that confirm your expected financial results.</span>

<span class="mark">Include here the explanation of the projections you made, an estimation of your income statement for at least three years, detailed cash flow projections, an estimated balance sheet for at least three financial years.</span>

# The resources

<span class="mark">Provide here you investment plan, which describes the amount of financial resources needed to make your organisation operative on the market. Specify here where you are planning to obtain such resources (investors, credit institutions, own funds, etc.), the timing for the use of the requested financial resources in the coming years, which part of the such financing is required from external actors.</span>
