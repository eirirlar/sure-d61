# SUREWAVE poster, CINEA Solar PV clustering event, April 2024 — description

Project poster presented by Balram Panjwani (SINTEF) at the CINEA Solar PV Energy Projects Clustering Event in Brussels on 25 April 2024. The distribution version (one page, 70 × 100 cm, exported from PowerPoint on 26 April 2024) is in the SUREWAVE Teams folder held by SINTEF; this file describes it so it can be referred to without the binary. Counted in the WP8 KPIs as a poster, an infographic and a factsheet (Eirik, 22 Nov 2026).

## Content, top to bottom

- Header: SUREWAVE logo and the project title "Structural reliable offshore floating PV solution integrating circular concrete floating breakwater" over a sea background.
- Major objective: to develop and test an innovative floating PV system with an external floating breakwater made of new circular materials, protecting the floating PV against severe wave loads and allowing increased operational availability and energy output.
- Concept diagram: the breakwater ring around the floating PV, with a dominant wave direction, surrounded by the project's building blocks: floating PV design for offshore extreme conditions, coupling between system and breakwater, external protective breakwater, anchoring and mooring, new circular concrete materials, predictive computational modelling and simulation framework, offshore FPV testing and validation framework, integrated sustainability assessment, and social engagement, environmental LCA and economic feasibility.
- Render of a straight breakwater section with a floating PV field behind it.
- Major activities performed (M1–M18):
  - tested hinge loads;
  - developed cellular concrete with recycled lightweight aggregates as a substitute for expanded polystyrene (EPS);
  - created high-performance concrete with supplementary cementitious materials as partial replacement for Portland cement;
  - developed concrete modelling tools based on artificial neural networks;
  - designed breakwater and integrated breakwater–FPV concepts for 0.4 MW, 2 MW and 10 MW;
  - coupled hydro-aero simulations of the breakwater–FPV concept;
  - wave tank experiments to validate the concept;
  - sensors for corrosion and force measurement;
  - development of a structural health monitoring tool (in progress).
- Four captioned result images: circular low-carbon concrete block (with a finite-element stress plot), hinge testing and simulation, wave tank experiment of the breakwater–FPV, and the SUREWAVE breakwater–FPV concept (round plant layout).
- Footer: partner logos (SINTEF, Sunlit Sea, MARIN, Clement Germany, IFEU, ACCIONA, Ceit), project duration three years with kick-off October 2022, QR code to https://surewave.eu/, Twitter and LinkedIn icons, EU emblem and the standard EU funding disclaimer.
