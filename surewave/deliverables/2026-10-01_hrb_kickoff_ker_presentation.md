## Key Exploitable Result 1 — integrated floating-photovoltaic system

Owner: Sunlit Sea AS. Technology Readiness Level 5 at project end.

What it is:

- Pressed marine-grade aluminium float with multi-sealant edge and PU potting
- Thin-film photovoltaic modules sealed for continuous marine exposure
- Generation-3 polyurethane hinge — ultimate load raised 14 kN → 37 kN
- Mooring, anchoring, transition elements for breakwater or standalone use

Defensibility:

- Open-sea survivability demonstrated at component scale (Deliverable 6.3)
- Pressed-aluminium construction compatible with mass production
- Patent candidate: Generation-3 PU hinge design (Deliverable 1.8)
- External validation: SINTEF mechanical/fatigue testing supported Gen-3 hinge development

## Commercial case — Mediterranean 10 MW is the lead

From Deliverable 7.4 Economic Assessment (September 2025):

| Site · 10 MW scale | Levelised Cost of Energy | Internal Rate of Return | Payback | Verdict |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Mediterranean | €0.062/kWh | 10.7% | 8 years | Viable |
| Baltic | €0.096/kWh | 2.2% | 14–16 years | Marginal |
| North Sea | €0.139/kWh | negative | — | Not viable |

Route to market:

- Direct sales to Mediterranean offshore-energy developers — first segment
- Licensing component sub-systems (hinge, pressed-aluminium float)
- Hybrid floating-PV + floating-wind portfolios — secondary segment
- Existing pipeline (Vollsfjorden, Enova pilot, Haugaland Kraft) as near-term beachhead
