## Findings

### F1 major. Sections 1, 2.4, 3.1: the two internal exploitation workshops of Subtask 8.2.2 are neither reported nor flagged as a deviation

Quote (D8.4 l.452–453): "the WP8 lead proposed seven candidate KERs ... listed in a common input template sent to the partners on 29 September 2026 ... The other five partners replied ... in their own format ... No partner proposed a KER of its own."

What is wrong: the GA commits the consortium to "two internal exploitation workshops ... conducted by all partners to accurately define the definitive Key Exploitable Results (KER)" (GA l.4478–4479, Subtask 8.2.2, Task 8.2 led by Sunlit Sea). D8.4 sets the definitive KERs, and its method (Section 3.1) is a template plus desk screening by the WP8 lead. The Booster workshops of Section 2.3 involved Sunlit Sea only. No source in the repo records the two consortium workshops; TASKS.md l.3141 lists their confirmation as open, and the partner input template (surewave/deliverables/2026-09-30_surewave_partner_exploitation_input_template.md l.479) still plans them. A project officer checks the KER definition against this subtask first, because it is the one place the GA prescribes how the KERs are agreed.

Fix: if the workshops were held, add them to Section 3.1 step 3 with dates, participants and outcome. If not, add to Section 3.1 after step 3 (ready text): "Subtask 8.2.2 foresaw two internal exploitation workshops of all partners to define the definitive KERs. They were not held as separate workshops. The KERs were defined through the input template of 29 September 2026, the partners' written summaries and the WP8 lead's screening (steps 2–5), and the partners review the KER map in the review round of this deliverable." Add a row to the Section 1 map: "Definition of the definitive KERs | GA, Subtask 8.2.2 | 3.1". This is not an owner question in owner_questions.md; it needs one.

### F2 major. Cover: the due date reads as fourteen months late, and the duration hides an extension

Quote (D8.4 l.5): "Project duration: 1 October 2022 to 31 December 2026 (51 months, including a three-month extension) · ... · Due in the Grant Agreement: month 36 · ... · Version 1.0 · November 2026".

What is wrong:
- With the start date on the same line, a reader computes month 36 as 30 September 2025 (GA Data Sheet l.328–330: 36 months, end 30 September 2025). The deliverable then looks fourteen months late. The amended plan dates the month-36 deliverables to 30 September 2026: D1.8, also labelled M36, gives "Due delivery date 30.09.2026" (D1.8 l.11, l.26), and the coordinator's deliverable list gives D8.4 as due 30 September 2026 (final technical report working copy l.602; used here as evidence, not as a citation).
- 51 months is the original 36 plus a twelve-month and a three-month extension (docs/project_facts.md l.51: "Nominal duration 2022-10-01 to 2026-09-30; extended three months to 2026-12-31"). "Including a three-month extension" accounts for 3 of the 15 added months. D1 forbids an extension history, not a correct figure.
- No submission date and no reason for delivery after 30 September 2026. Owner question 1 recommends keeping "month 36" if the amendment did not move it; D1.8 shows it did.

Fix (ready text): "Project duration: 1 October 2022 to 31 December 2026 (51 months, as amended) · ... · Due date: 30 September 2026 (month 36 deliverable, as dated in the amended Grant Agreement) · Submitted: [date]". Add one sentence at the end of Section 2.3: "D8.4 was completed after its due date of 30 September 2026 so that the KER1 business plan could use the Horizon Results Booster work of October to December 2026; the coordinator informed the project officer on [date]." The owner confirms the date and whether the project officer was informed.

### F3 major. Sections 3.2, 7.1, 7.2: KER1 ownership conflicts with D1.8, and the line between SUREWAVE result, background and SuRE result is not drawn for CONNECT
