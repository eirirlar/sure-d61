# T150 round 1: cold-reader review of D8.4 (d84_merged.md)

Persona: evaluator at another agency who has never seen the project. For each claim and choice: "how do we know?" and "why this?". Line numbers refer to d84_merged.md. Repo sources were checked only where a cold reader's doubt could be settled quickly; everything else is flagged as the reader meets it.

## A. Numbers and claims the reader cannot reconcile

1. Major. 4.1 (l. 518–520), 3.4 R1 (l. 476), 6.1 (l. 926): "average tensile capacity ... rose from 3.69 kN to 8.36 kN" next to "design break load ... rises from 14 kN to 37 kN" and "50-year maximum hinge loads are 34 kN in the Baltic Sea". Problem: a cold reader compares 8.36 kN tested with 34 kN expected load and concludes that the tested hinge fails at a quarter of the Baltic load, while the text says the hinge is "below that value". Nothing says what each figure is measured on (one hinge specimen or a length of hinge, the full 1.88 m edge connection, one load direction) or why the design value is four times the tested one. Source: decisions D4; D7.6 (surewave/background/2025-11-05_surewave_d7.6_integrated_sustainability_report.md). Fix: add one sentence in 4.1 after the SINTEF bullet, worded to D7.6's basis, for example: "The tested capacities are for [the hinge test specimen of x m]; the 37 kN of D7.6 is the design break load of the complete full-edge connection, the value the simulated hinge loads are compared with." Use the same sentence in 3.4 R1 and 6.1.

2. Major. Executive summary (l. 19), 3.2 (l. 436), 3.3 (l. 452), 4.1 (l. 509): "Sunlit Sea's CONNECT platform is at TRL 4–5". Problem: no source or basis is given, and 3.4 (l. 488) says "The owners did not rate the TRL of their individual results at project end". Who rated CONNECT, and on what tests? 3. Major. 4.1 (l. 508–510), 4.10 (l. 870): integrated concept "TRL 5", CONNECT "TRL 4–5", directional configuration "starts at TRL 4: it has been tested in the laboratory and basin only". Add which float generation was in each MARIN campaign.
