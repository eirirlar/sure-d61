3. Major. Which hinge MARIN's second basin campaign tested. Section 4.1, line 383: "The hinge redesign has been tested at SINTEF (D6.3) and in MARIN's second basin campaign (D8.3)". Section 7.1, line 1082: "the Generation 2 architecture with the redesigned full-edge hinge, tested by SINTEF (D6.3) and in MARIN's second basin campaign (D8.3)". But 4.1 itself says the second campaign "used an improved connector, longer and thicker than the first generation and fitted in pairs between adjacent floats" (line 395). The same paragraph defines the second generation as "the shorter hinges fitted in pairs at the float corners" and the third generation as the full-edge hinge (line 390). MARIN's paper confirms pairs: "adjacent FPV modules are connected through a pair of connectors" (surewave/background/2026-03-25_bunnik_hydrodynamic_simulations_2mw_offshore_solar_fpv.md:70–71). D7.6 places the 37 kN full-edge v3 after the v2 corner hinges (2025-11-05_surewave_d7.6_integrated_sustainability_report.md:687–689). So a cold reader concludes that the full-edge third-generation hinge, the CONNECT hinge, was tested only as a one-fifth segment at SINTEF and never in a basin. 7.1 says it was, and 4.1 is ambiguous. That is the core of the CONNECT TRL 4–5 claim and of the Lean Canvas "unfair advantage" (line 575). D8.3 also writes only "the redesigned hinge and updated connection arrangement" (D8.3:200). Fix, line 383: "The redesigned connection has been tested as a one-fifth segment at SINTEF (D6.3). MARIN's second basin campaign tested an improved connector fitted in pairs between floats (Energies 19(7) 1609); the full-edge third-generation hinge has not yet been tested in a basin." Line 1082: "...the Generation 2 architecture with the redesigned full-edge hinge, tested as a segment by SINTEF (D6.3), ...". Line 395: add "D7.6 does not say whether this connector is the second-generation corner hinge." If the owner knows that the second-campaign connector is v2, say so in one clause at line 390 instead.
