# D8.4 cold-reader sweep: places the reader cannot follow

Source: `surewave/deliverables/2026-09-28_surewave_d8.4_exploitation_pathways_business_plan.md` (state of 2026-11-24). Reader: CINEA project officer or external evaluator with no other project document. Findings in document order. Format: section, quote, what is missing, proposed fix.

## Document-wide (placed first because they surface from the executive summary onwards)

1. Whole document, from Exec summary. "SINTEF ... IFEU ... MARIN ... ACCIONA ... Ceit". The seven beneficiaries, their countries and type (research institute, contractor, SME) are never introduced; the reader meets them as bare names. Fix: one sentence in the Exec summary or Section 1: "The consortium: SINTEF (NO, coordinator), Sunlit Sea (NO), Clement Germany (DE), ACCIONA (ES), MARIN (NL), Ceit (ES), IFEU (DE)", with one-word roles.

2. Whole document, from Exec summary. "D7.2", "D7.6", "D3.1, D3.4", "WP2", "WP3", "WP5". Deliverable numbers and work packages are used from the first paragraph; the deliverable list is in Annex A without a pointer, and the work packages are never listed. Fix: in Section 1, "Deliverables are cited by number; titles and leads are in Annex A" plus a one-line WP list (WP1 management ... WP8 exploitation, with leads).

3. Whole document. "Energies 17(19) 4873", "Preprints 202610.0011", "Sensors 24(13) 4211". Journal and preprint citations have no reference list, so the reader cannot find authors or titles. Fix: add a short references annex, or give author-year with the first citation.

4. Whole document. "Sunlit Sea requests ... as the WP8 lead proposes". Sunlit Sea and "the WP8 lead" are the same party, but the doubled naming reads as two actors agreeing. Fix: state once in Section 1 that "the WP8 lead" means Sunlit Sea acting for the consortium, and use "Sunlit Sea, as WP8 lead, proposes" where both roles apply.

5. Whole document. "global solution", "integrated system", "KER1", "protected floating PV system", "open-sea plant", "SUREWAVE system". Several names for overlapping things; the reader cannot tell when the open-sea ring plant is meant and when the CONNECT product line. Fix: define in Section 4.1 "KER1 = the product line; the D7.4 reference plant = the open-sea ring configuration; the protected system = CONNECT behind a directional breakwater" and use those three terms only.

6. Whole document. "exposed sites", "exposed nearshore sites", "exposed inland water" (Section 10), "sheltered sites", lake plants in both groups. A lake is a sheltered site in Section 4.4 (third reference plant) and an exposed site in Section 10 (EIC demonstrator). Fix: define "sheltered" and "exposed" once by wave height (e.g. by the class table of 4.1.1), independent of sea or lake.

7. Whole document. "reference plant" (D7.4 reference plant), "Norwegian reference plants", "reference design" (mooring, 6.5), "the €1,560/kWp reference" (4.10). One word, four meanings. Fix: keep "reference plants" for Norway, call the D7.4 case "the D7.4 open-sea plant", the mooring case "the baseline mooring", and name the cost benchmark (see 59).

8. Whole document. "hinge", "connector", "connection", "full-edge", "full-width". Hinges between floats, "connectors" in the basin campaign, and breakwater-array "connections" share vocabulary; "full-edge" and "full-width" both name the third generation. Fix: "hinge" for float-to-float, "connection" for breakwater-to-array only; use "full-edge" throughout.

9. Whole document. "the first plant", "the first plants", "Sunlit Sea's own first plant", "the first commercial plant with a breakwater class", "the first multi-megawatt plant". Several different "first" plants. Fix: name them once in 4.4 (Plant A port, Plant B industrial basin, Plant C lake) or use "the first Norwegian reference plant" consistently, and "first plants" only with a qualifier.
