## Part 4. Findings

### 1. Major. The shared KER1 clause says CONNECT was "developed in SuRE". This contradicts Sunlit Sea's input and the draft's own executive summary and 4.1 (3.2 l.301, 3.4 R1 l.341, 6.1 l.907, 7.1 l.1117, Section 10 l.1196)

Quotes:
- 3.2, 3.4 R1, 6.1 l.907 and 7.1 (identical in all four): "CONNECT, the Generation 2 platform with cast polyurethane floats, developed in the parallel project SuRE on the results of the Generation 1 aluminium-float SUREWAVE array".
- Section 10: "SuRE funds the development of Sunlit Sea CONNECT ... in parallel with SUREWAVE".

Against these:
- Executive summary l.24: "Sunlit Sea started the Generation 2 redesign from the SUREWAVE hinge tests and models and continues it in the parallel project SuRE".
- 4.1 l.400 says the same.
- 6.1 l.903: "Sunlit Sea continues the Generation 2 redesign in ... SuRE".
- 4.1 l.407: Sunlit Sea redesigned the connection in SUREWAVE and delivered the injection-moulded parts that SINTEF tested (D6.3).

What is wrong:
- Sunlit Sea's input says the SUREWAVE tests "led to a complete redesign of the product architecture, resulting in Generation 2", and that "This redesign process continues in the follow-up EU project SuRE" (surewave/background/2026-09-30_sunlit_sea_exploitation_summary.md:37–43).
- The de-duplication pass (dedup_log item 4) standardised the four cells on the D63 wording. That wording reverses the round-4 fix of r4_owner 3. The KER table now tells CINEA something different from the executive summary, and drops the SUREWAVE start of the redesign.
- "On the results of the ... array" also hides which results: the hinge tests and the models.
- This is an error inside the D63 wording, not a challenge to D63. D63's own order (SUREWAVE results first, then the carrier) is kept.

Fix, the same in the four places: "Commercial carrier: Sunlit Sea CONNECT, the Generation 2 platform with cast polyurethane floats. Sunlit Sea started its redesign from the SUREWAVE hinge tests and models of the Generation 1 aluminium-float array and continues it in the parallel project SuRE (Section 4.1)."

Section 10 SuRE cell: "Sunlit Sea continues in SuRE the Generation 2 redesign that it started from the SUREWAVE hinge tests (SINTEF's connection tests and MARIN's basin tests) and models: prototypes 4 and 5 of Sunlit Sea CONNECT are developed in SuRE and prepared for pilot-scale deployment (Section 4.1). SuRE and SUREWAVE fund different work (Section 4.7). ..."

### 2. Minor. The 6.1 network result points to Section 10 for a fact that is not there, and leaves out the Advisory Board link (l.904)

Quote: "Network. As WP8 lead, Sunlit Sea built a network of stakeholders, research partners and industrial contacts. Sunlit Sea initiated SuRE (Section 10)."

What is wrong:
- Section 10 (l.1196) does not say that Sunlit Sea initiated SuRE.
- The network result's evidence is that the initiative came through the SUREWAVE Stakeholder Advisory Board, and that another board member works with Sunlit Sea in joint initiatives. That evidence is in Sunlit Sea's input l.53 and in A.10 l.1426, but not in the network bullet. This undersells WP8's main exploitation impact.

Fix: "Network. As WP8 lead, Sunlit Sea built a network of stakeholders, research partners and industrial contacts. Sunlit Sea initiated SuRE in partnership with a member of the Stakeholder Advisory Board, and another board member works with Sunlit Sea in several joint initiatives (Section 10; Annex A.10)."

### 5. Minor. 4.10 and 6.5 state more than D5.1 Table 9 shows on the optimised mooring loads (l.843, l.1030)

Quotes:
- 4.10 CAPEX row: "the fairlead and anchor loads rise in the optimised designs (Section 6.5)".
- 6.5: "The lighter moorings carry higher maximum tensions at the fairlead, up to about 2.5 times the reference value (D5.1, Table 9)."

What is wrong:
- D5.1 Table 9 gives fairlead tensions only (surewave/background/2024-04-16_surewave_d5.1.md:1485–1534). Section 6.5 says nothing about anchor loads, so the 4.10 pointer leads to no such statement.
- In Table 9 the maximum tension rises for the Mediterranean (1.40 and 2.46) and for the North Sea 2 MWp ring (2.15). It is unchanged for the North Sea 10 MWp ring (1.00).
- D5.1's own wording is "for most designs on the Pareto front" (l.1543).

Fix:
- 4.10: "the lighter moorings carry higher maximum fairlead tensions in most optimised designs (Section 6.5)".
- 6.5: "Most of the lighter moorings carry higher maximum tensions at the fairlead, up to about 2.5 times the reference value; for the North Sea 10 MWp ring the maximum tension is unchanged (D5.1, Table 9)."
