## 7. Intellectual property

### 7.1 Ownership and background

Results belong to the beneficiary that generated them; joint ownership arises only from inseparable intellectual contributions, and testing, modelling or feedback by other partners do not create it by themselves (D1.8, the IPR management plan). KERs are owned as listed in Section 3. Background, including Sunlit Sea's original floating PV technology and pre-existing hinge designs, remains with its owner (D1.4, D1.8).

For KER1, D1.8 records Sunlit Sea as principal generating partner of the floating PV components and modified hinges, and the breakwater design as Clement Germany's foreground, subject to confirmation of any documented inseparable contributions (Section 7.4); integration transfers no ownership. In the split the WP8 lead proposes, Sunlit Sea's results are the floating PV array and global system design (D3.1, D3.4), the hinge redesign (Section 4.1), the nearshore wave and wind models with the wave-corrected performance ratio (Section 6.1), and the economic assessment and cost model (D7.4). Its background is the Generation 1 float and hinge, used in the SUREWAVE array, and its earlier patent. The commercial carrier is Sunlit Sea CONNECT, the Generation 2 platform with cast polyurethane floats, whose redesign Sunlit Sea started in SUREWAVE and continues in the parallel project SuRE. D1.8 records no dispute requiring escalation.

## 15. Conclusions

Seven Key Exploitable Results with named owners rest on Clement Germany's breakwater, mooring and anchoring design (WP3); ACCIONA's circular mixes and the Gijón prototype (D4.x); Sunlit Sea's floating PV and global system design (WP2, D3.1, D3.4) and hinge redesign; SINTEF's tests (D6.1, D6.3, D7.2); MARIN's 1:10 basin tests of a 3 × 5 array behind one breakwater section, with the ring only simulated, and D5.1; Ceit's monitoring (WP5); Sunlit Sea's economic assessment (D7.4); and IFEU's sustainability assessment (WP7, D7.1). The integrated system is at TRL 4–5 (Section 4.1).

Sunlit Sea commercialises KER1, the integrated floating PV system with breakwater, from sheltered Norwegian sites towards exposed sites with a breakwater. Its financial plan reaches EBITDA break-even in 2030 if the reference plants come on time (Section 4.7). Partners' results reach KER1 as services or licences under agreements still to be concluded, and are also exploited outside floating PV.
