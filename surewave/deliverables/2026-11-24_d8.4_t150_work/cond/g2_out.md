## 3. Key Exploitable Results

### 3.1 How the KERs were identified

The proposal named one global solution, P1 *Offshore FPV plant*, to be marketed by Sunlit Sea, and 11 expected results, R1–R11; R1 is the global solution itself. D8.5 (month 18, March 2024) reported the mid-term status of R2–R10 (Section 2.1). From these, the WP8 lead proposed seven candidate KERs, one anchored in each beneficiary's main contribution, in an input template sent to the partners on 29 September 2026. ACCIONA's summary (26 November 2025) and Clement Germany's (4 September 2026) predate the template; the other five partners replied between 30 September and 5 October 2026, in their own format (Section 2.2). The seven summaries report 27 exploitable results; none gives a TRL, a time to market or a revenue figure, and no partner proposed a KER of its own. The two exploitation workshops of subtask 8.2.2 are not recorded as separate events; the definitive KERs were set through the written input round and the WP8 lead's screening and mapping.

The WP8 lead checked each result against the technical deliverables. Three gains in network and visibility and Clement Germany's contribution to engineering practice are reported as impact; the other 23 results each fitted one of the seven candidates, which were kept. SINTEF's hydrodynamic modelling sits with MARIN's in KER5, since both serve the same customers, and its aerodynamic method in KER4; ownership stays separate (Section 7.1). Where sources differ, this plan uses the owner's figure. The IPR management plan D1.8 asks Sunlit Sea and the documented contributors to confirm the contributions to the hinge redesign before any registration, licence or commercial deployment (Section 7.4). The table in Section 3.2 is the basis for the results ownership list in the final periodic report (Grant Agreement, Annex 5).

### 3.3 Depth of treatment

Each KER is assessed against the criteria of Section 2.4. KER1 has the full business plan (Section 4) that the proposal and objective O8 ask for the global solution. It is the only new product, carries the commercial risk (market entry, production chain, Norwegian reference plants, an equity raise of €5.5M) and entered the Horizon Results Booster's Go-to-Market service. KERs 2–7 strengthen products and services their owners sell today, mainly outside floating PV, and no partner reported a spin-off or investment calling for a separate business plan. Each gets the individual exploitation plan the proposal foresees: a route and a dated action plan with indicators (Section 6), its role in KER1 (Section 5) and its protection position (Section 7.2). Internal commercial plans stay with their owners, since some partners are each other's suppliers or customers.

### 3.5 Non-commercial pathways

The scientific, social and political pathways of the proposal continue through the partners' publications and open records (Sections 2.5 and 6), IFEU's societal engagement (D8.2) and social life-cycle assessment (D7.5), and the public recommendations of D7.6 and cost data of D7.4.
