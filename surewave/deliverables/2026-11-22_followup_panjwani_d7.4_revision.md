PROPOSED RESPONSE FOR THE PORTAL

1. End of life. Version 1.0 did not include site clean-up; version 2.0 does (Section 4.4). In year 50 the breakwater, pontoons, most of the mooring chains and the PV modules are removed, at the same cost as installation. The anchor piles and a quarter of the chains remain on the seabed, as in the environmental assessment (D7.3), which also covers reuse and recycling. Because the cost falls after 50 years, it changes the results very little.

2. Excluded costs. Section 2.4 lists what the model includes and what lies outside it, including the offshore substation, grid connection, insurance, financing, removal of the anchor piles and disposal fees.

3. Corrections beyond the request. Extending the model, we checked every calculation and found errors in version 1.0: costs described but not calculated (structure maintenance, inverters, SCADA), PV replacement missing from the LCOE, and a non-standard discounting and IRR method. We have corrected them (Section 2.5). For the 10 MW cases:
- Mediterranean: LCOE €0.062 → €0.076/kWh, NPV €8.9M → €7.3M
- Baltic: LCOE €0.096 → €0.120/kWh, NPV €2.1M → break-even
- North Sea: LCOE €0.139 → €0.173/kWh, not viable in either
IRR is now calculated on undiscounted cash flows, so it is not comparable with version 1.0.
