Hi Balram,

Before I send you the revised D7.4, I need your decision on how far the revision should go.

To answer the Project Officer's request on end-of-life costs, I extended the cost model and reviewed it at the same time. The review found that the model left out some costs (maintenance of the floating structure, inverters and transformers, the SCADA system, and the PV replacement cost in the LCOE) and used a non-standard discounting and IRR calculation. Corrected, the headline figures for the 10 MW cases change as follows (old → new):

Mediterranean: LCOE €0.062 → €0.076/kWh, NPV €8.9M → €7.3M, IRR 10.7% → 15.6%
Baltic: LCOE €0.096 → €0.120/kWh, NPV €2.1M → €0.0M (break-even), IRR 2.2% → 8.5%
North Sea: LCOE €0.139 → €0.173/kWh, NPV negative in both, IRR negative → 3.4%

The IRR is now calculated on undiscounted cash flows, the standard definition, so the old and new IRR values are not directly comparable. Like for like, the return falls with the NPV, from 21.1% to 15.6% for the Mediterranean. The conclusions hold, except that the Baltic moves from marginally viable to break-even at the 8.5% discount rate.

The figures are also quoted in D7.6, D8.3, the preprint, D8.4 and the final technical report.

As I see it, there are two options:

1. Corrected revision. D7.4 version 2.0 adds the system boundary and end-of-life sections the Project Officer asked for and updates all figures to the corrected model. This version is ready. Documents already delivered (D7.6, D8.3) would not be reopened, and D7.4 version 2.0 would become the reference. The documents still in progress (final technical report, preprint, D8.4) would use the new figures. The updated cost workbook would go to Zenodo as a new version of the D7.4 dataset.

2. Minimal revision. D7.4 adds the system boundary and end-of-life sections but keeps the version 1 figures, and states the model's known limitations in the scope section. Nothing downstream changes. The drawback is that the report would keep results we know to be wrong, and the limitations would be visible to the Project Officer.

My recommendation is option 1, but it is your call as coordinator, also on whether to tell the Project Officer in advance. Once you have decided, I will send the files and a short response for the portal.
