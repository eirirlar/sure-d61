# SUREWAVE LinkedIn posts

Register of posts on the SUREWAVE LinkedIn company page: the publication schedule, the full text of planned posts, the archive of earlier posts, and ideas that were never posted. Sunlit Sea maintains the page as WP8 leader.

Sources: planned posts are written in this repo (task numbers in the table). The archive is the consortium's post log in Google Drive, downloaded 22 November 2026; texts are copied as they stand in the log, dates as the log records them. The log does not record dates for every post, and publication of each archived text has not been checked against the page (the public page shows only the latest posts). The video posts of November 2026 are known from the WP8 KPI verification (`surewave/background/2026-11-19_surewave_wp8_kpi_verification.md`), not from the log.

Conventions for new posts:

- One post each Tuesday during the schedule below.
- Newsletters, the brochure and the infographic are document posts: upload the PDF with "Add a document" in the post editor, with the document title given in the entry (LinkedIn allows at most 57 characters), so it shows as a swipeable document with page 1 as the cover.
- Post text is plain text (copy everything inside the text block). Hyphen lists are fine; no other Markdown.
- Every post ends with the funding line "SUREWAVE is funded by the European Union under Horizon Europe (GA 101083342) and coordinated by SINTEF, with Sunlit Sea, Ceit, MARIN, ACCIONA, Clement Germany and IFEU.", a link where relevant, and hashtags starting with #SUREWAVE.
- After publishing, change the status in the schedule to Published with the date and post link, and record it in the KPI verification (section 7, and section 8 for infographics).

```text
How do you know a floating breakwater will protect an offshore solar plant for its whole life at sea?

SUREWAVE answers it with a chain of linked models. Site wind and wave data feed coupled aero-hydrodynamic models, their connector and mooring loads feed finite-element models of the concrete breakwater, and a Structural Health Management System combines the stress fields with crack-growth algorithms and input from a new corrosion sensor. The chain can be used for site-specific design, remaining-life prediction and inspection planning for offshore floating PV.
```

### Improving hinge performance for offshore floating PV

Date: Not recorded in the log. Listed newest in the log, before the Madrid summit post (2025).

```text
As part of the Horizon Europe-funded SUREWAVE project, one key technical focus has been the optimization of the hinge used to connect floating PV modules, developed by Sunlit Sea AS.

Initial tensile and fatigue testing by SINTEF, along with wave basin testing by MARIN, showed that the first-generation hinge design was not suited to the mechanical loads and dynamic conditions expected in offshore environments. Based on these results, the hinge was re-evaluated and redesigned through an iterative process, with input from multiple project partners.

A numerical study by CEIT complemented the physical testing by providing detailed insights into stress distributions and informing decisions about connector materials. This contributed to a more robust understanding of the hinge’s structural behavior.

The updated design demonstrates improved performance and durability, reflecting the value of combining experimental and numerical approaches in the development of offshore energy components.
```

### Floating PV breakwater characterization

Date: 13 February 2024.

```text
Exciting developments in SUREWAVE, a project that's promising to deliver circular floating wavebreakers for floating solar.

Imagine a floating breakwater, a simple yet innovative solution, very well suited to protecting other floating structures such as floating solar installations. Through the EU-funded SUREWAVE project, project partner ACCIONA and others have driven extensive research towards an optimal wave breaker for shielding FPV installations from harsh marine conditions, offering a more resilient approach to energy production.

What sets SUREWAVE apart is the careful consideration given to concrete formulations. Lightweight Aggregate Concrete (LWAC) and High-Performance Concrete (HPC) is currently being explored, seeking an optimal composition in order to increase circularity and reduce the CO2 footprint - all while ensuring the structural reliability of the wavebreaker.

Addressing concerns about the cement industry's impact on CO2 emissions, SUREWAVE takes a practical approach by minimizing cement content. This involves the use of low-carbon cement paired with Supplementary Cementitious Materials (SCMs) like ground granulated blast-furnace slag (GGBS) and fly ash (FA). It's a step towards meeting the rising demand for sustainable solutions without overstating the case.

But the real excitement lies in SUREWAVE's commitment to a circular model. Leveraging non-primary available resources, recycled materials such as aggregate from concrete waste and lightweight aggregate from recycled glass breathe new life into concrete mix designs. It's not revolutionary; it's a pragmatic move towards environmental responsibility.

#SustainableInfrastructure #CircularEconomy #InnovationInConcrete #GreenTechnology
```
