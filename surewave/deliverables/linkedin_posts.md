## Newsletter V — to post at the end of December 2026

Document: `2026-11-20_surewave_newsletter_v.pdf`. Document title: "SUREWAVE Newsletter V – Cost, carbon and what comes next".

SUREWAVE Newsletter V is out: "Cost, carbon and what comes next". This is the final issue; SUREWAVE ends on 31 December 2026. Swipe through the two pages below.

- Cost of energy for a 10 MW plant: €0.062/kWh in the Mediterranean, €0.096/kWh in the Baltic and €0.139/kWh in the North Sea.
- The grid connection decides: a nearby offshore substation keeps costs down, a long submarine cable to shore does not.
- Climate and environment: a 9 MWp plant saves 160,000 to 180,000 t of CO₂-equivalent over 25 years compared with coal, even at the North Sea site.
- The people in the supply chain: almost half of the social risk sits in the solar-panel supply chain.
- What comes next: an open-sea pilot, one more hinge iteration for the North Sea, and sites that can share offshore infrastructure.

The open reports and datasets behind each result are listed at the end. Thank you to everyone who followed the project.

SUREWAVE is funded by the European Union under Horizon Europe (GA 101083342) and coordinated by SINTEF, with Sunlit Sea, Ceit, MARIN, ACCIONA, Clement Germany and IFEU.

## Newsletter IV — to post in mid-December 2026

Document: `2026-11-20_surewave_newsletter_iv.pdf`. Document title: "SUREWAVE Newsletter IV – From recycled concrete to the water".

SUREWAVE Newsletter IV is out: "From recycled concrete to the water". Swipe through the three pages below.

This issue follows the concrete for our floating breakwaters from the laboratory to nine months afloat in the port of Gijón:

- Circular concrete that meets marine specifications: high-performance concrete reached 128 MPa, with part of the cement replaced by slag and fly ash.
- Durability and strength in the laboratory, and why high-performance concrete was chosen for the shell.
- Nine months afloat at El Musel, Gijón: the prototype stayed in operation, and the inspection showed which detail of a floating concrete unit needs most attention.
- Monitoring the structure over its life: a structural health management system and a new corrosion sensor.

The last page lists the open reports, papers and data behind each result.

SUREWAVE is funded by the European Union under Horizon Europe (GA 101083342) and coordinated by SINTEF, with Sunlit Sea, Ceit, MARIN, ACCIONA, Clement Germany and IFEU.

## Newsletter III — to post the week of 23 November 2026

Document: `2026-11-20_surewave_newsletter_iii.pdf`. Document title: "SUREWAVE Newsletter III – Tested at scale".

SUREWAVE Newsletter III is out: "Tested at scale". Swipe through the three pages below.

This issue covers what the tests at scale showed for our wave-shielded offshore floating PV concept:

- Two wave-basin campaigns at MARIN. Behind the floating breakwater, FPV module motions dropped by a factor of about 2 to 3.
- A hinge that failed, and the one that replaced it. The redesigned hinge clears the Baltic and Mediterranean design loads; the North Sea needs one more iteration.
- Connection tests to breaking point at SINTEF.
- A full 2 MWp farm simulated on a supercomputer: 3,660 FPV modules inside a ring of 32 floating breakwaters.

The last page lists the open reports, datasets and papers behind each result.

SUREWAVE is funded by the European Union under Horizon Europe (GA 101083342) and coordinated by SINTEF, with Sunlit Sea, Ceit, MARIN, ACCIONA, Clement Germany and IFEU.
