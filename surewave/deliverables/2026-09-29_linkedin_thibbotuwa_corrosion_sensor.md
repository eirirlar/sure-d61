# LinkedIn post — Thibbotuwa et al., Sensors (3 November 2022)

Thibbotuwa, Cortés and Irizar (CEIT / Universidad de Navarra) deployed a small ultrasound-based corrosion sensor near the coast at Gran Canaria and tracked thickness loss on an S355 steel sample over five months. Sensors, November 2022.

The probe sits glued on the dry side of the plate and reads wall thickness via ultrasound pulse-echo, so the sensor doesn't disturb the corrosion it measures. Small, low power, built to run unattended on offshore structures. Result at the test site: 0.134 mm/year corrosion rate, resolved from repeated intraday measurements.

Aimed at offshore wind, but the same monitoring question sits on top of any offshore FPV array once past first deployment.

Ainhoa Cortés later co-authored the SUREWAVE basin-test paper on our FPV with floating-breakwater protection (Energies 2024).
