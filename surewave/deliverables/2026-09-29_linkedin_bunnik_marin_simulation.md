# LinkedIn post — Bunnik et al., Energies (25 March 2026)

The MARIN team published simulation results for a 2 MWp offshore FPV farm built on our floats, protected by a ring of 32 concrete floating breakwaters. Out in Energies in March.

Tim Bunnik, Naman Baderiya and Joep van der Zanden extended their wave-diffraction solver DIFFRAC to run all 3660 interconnected FPV modules together — hydrodynamic response, connector loads, and mooring interaction resolved for the full farm on a supercomputer, rather than for a downscaled subset.

Main findings:

- The breakwater ring lowers connector loads on average across the array.
- Vertical loads and overturning moments on connectors drop by up to 50% in steep irregular seas.
- Local hotspots remain — horizontal motions can be dynamically amplified, and standing waves inside the ring add load peaks.
- Design recommendations follow for the FPV layout and breakwater arrangement.

Part of SUREWAVE (Horizon Europe, GA 101083342), coordinated by SINTEF, with MARIN leading the hydrodynamics work.
