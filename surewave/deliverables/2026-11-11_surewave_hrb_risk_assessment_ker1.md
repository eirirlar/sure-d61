# Risk assessment and priority map — KER1 Sunlit Sea CONNECT floating photovoltaic platform

Horizon Results Booster G2M input · Sunlit Sea AS · final version, 14 November 2026, revised 23 November 2026 to match the financial plan and the value chain analysis

Ratings follow the G2M risk map: criticality and probability from 1 (low) to 10 (high); risk grade = criticality × probability; feasibility of the intervention from 1 (low) to 10 (high). The conclusion follows the template rule: grade above 50 with feasibility above 5 is *Action*, grade above 50 with feasibility below 5 is *Warning*, grade up to 50 with feasibility above 5 is *Control*, grade up to 50 with feasibility below 5 is *No action*; feasibility exactly 5 falls between categories.

## Summary

Four risks land in the *Action* zone (grade above 50): high cost outside the best sites, buyers waiting for a track record, the dependency on two key people, and the reference plants coming a year late, the largest single risk in the financial plan. The first two are handled by the same move: deliver the Norwegian reference plants in 2027–2028 and publish their measured performance. The key-person risk is handled by the cover, deputies and hires that follow the value chain analysis, and the delay risk by the decision gates that tie hiring and investment to the plants, with a bridge or a larger second round as the funding answer. All other risks sit in the *Control* zone or on the border, with interventions planned in the exploitation roadmap.
