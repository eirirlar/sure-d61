# Risk assessment and priority map — KER1 Sunlit Sea CONNECT floating photovoltaic platform

Horizon Results Booster G2M input · Sunlit Sea AS · final version, 14 November 2026

Ratings follow the G2M risk map: criticality and probability from 1 (low) to 10 (high); risk grade = criticality × probability; feasibility of the intervention from 1 (low) to 10 (high). The conclusion follows the template rule: grade above 50 with feasibility above 5 is *Action*, grade above 50 with feasibility below 5 is *Warning*, grade up to 50 with feasibility above 5 is *Control*, grade up to 50 with feasibility below 5 is *No action*; feasibility exactly 5 falls between categories.

## Summary

Two risks land in the *Action* zone (grade above 50): high cost outside the best sites, and buyers waiting for a track record. Both are handled by the same move: build and operate the Norwegian reference plants in 2027–2028 and publish their measured performance. All other risks sit in the *Control* zone or on the border, with interventions that are already planned in the exploitation roadmap.
