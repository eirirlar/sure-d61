# SUREWAVE — full WP, task and deliverable index

*Compiled 2026-09-29 from the Grant Agreement (`surewave/background/2022-09-20_surewave_grant_agreement_101083342.md`) and Annex 1 Part B v2 (`surewave/background/2022-06-24_surewave_annex1_part_b_v2.md`). No exclusions.*

Eight Work Packages. WP1 and WP8 are cross-cutting (management and dissemination/exploitation); WP2 is the framework block; WP3–WP5 are the research-and-technology development block; WP6 and WP7 are the testing / validation / sustainability block.

Sub-WPs do not exist as a formal structure in SUREWAVE. Some deliverable narratives (e.g. D5.4 §T5.3) mention informal subtasks such as "Subtask 5.3.3" or "Subtask 8.3.2"; these are not tabulated in the Grant Agreement and are omitted here to avoid inventing structure. Milestones (MS1–MS11) are also omitted — they are project checkpoints, not tasks or deliverables.

Durations are stated as `M<start>–M<end>` per the original 36-month DoA. All M36 deliverables actually shipped in 2026 following the ~12-month post-signature extension inferred in `2026-09-28_status.md`.

## Ambiguities from the source material

- The GA §3.1.2 timing table is scrambled by pandoc's docx conversion — column contents are misaligned across rows. Task titles and durations were cross-checked against the intact GA §3.1.1 narrative (`^Task N.M` lines) and against the GA "List of Deliverables" table, both of which came through cleanly.
- Task 6.2 title had to be reconstructed because pandoc dropped its line from the narrative. The reconstruction (`Critical connections' ultimate strength performances`) is derived from D6.1's title, from Task 6.3's forward-reference "critical connections defined in task 6.2", and from a corrupted table row containing "Critical connections ultimate strenght performances" (sic) in the Task 6 group. Confirm with SINTEF for the WP6 lead before citing externally.
- Filed deliverable dates and titles occasionally differ slightly from the GA's original wording (e.g. D6.3 filed as "Mechanical Performance of New FPV Connection Systems" instead of "Report on fatigue performances"). These are the same deliverables, and the original DoA title is used above for consistency.
