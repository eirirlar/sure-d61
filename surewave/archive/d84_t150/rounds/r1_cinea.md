## Findings

### F1 major. Section 2.5 and executive summary: the headline KPI claim is overstated against the GA's own wording

Quote (line 38): "SUREWAVE met or exceeded every dissemination and communication target in the table of Section 2.5 except one: five of the six infographics." Line 142: "SUREWAVE met or exceeded every target except infographics".

What is wrong:
- Publications: the GA KPI is "more than 10 peer-reviewed publications" (GA line 7389; Annex 1 Part B line 814). D8.4 lists 10, and says itself (line 192) that three are congress papers whose proceedings "do not state that the papers were peer reviewed". Verifiably peer-reviewed: 6 journal papers plus the MARINE 2025 paper = 7. D8.5 sets "at least 10 peer-reviewed articles in scientific journals" (D8.5 line ~262): 6. The target column of the table says "10", which is weaker than the GA's ">10".
- Conference workshops: GA KPI "more than 3 conference workshops" (GA line 7403). D8.4 counts 3, one of which is an online webinar (the NaturSea-PV webinar), and the table target says "3".
- Videos: the table counts 7 against a target of 2 and marks it met, while the reach table (line 186) states the GA's two videos are "an introduction and a final summary" (GA line 4510: "2 videos (intro and final) will be produced to summarise the results") and that the final summary video "has not been produced". The same target cannot be met in one table and open in the other.
- Press releases: the GA target is "press releases at the start and at milestones" (GA line 7633). D8.4 counts one press release, at the prototype milestone, and counts the start announcement as a journalistic article (line 322). On the document's own classification the start press release is missing.

Fix: correct the target column (">10" for publications, ">3" for conference workshops, "start and milestones" for press releases, "2: introduction and final summary" for videos), mark the status honestly, and replace the two headline sentences. Ready text for line 38:
"Of the targets in the table of Section 2.5, SUREWAVE met or exceeded those for presentations, conferences, posters, web events, joint events, Stakeholder Advisory Board workshops, newsletters, journalistic articles, interviews, factsheets, the flyer, the roll-up, the technical brochure, LinkedIn followers and website visits. It reached ten publications against the target of more than ten peer-reviewed publications, seven of them verifiably peer reviewed; three conference workshops against more than three; five of six infographics; one press release, at the prototype milestone; and the introduction video, with the final summary video not yet produced. Several reach targets were not met or not measured (Section 2.5)."
Same change at line 142. If the final summary video and the sixth infographic are produced before 31 December 2026, say so with their dates.

### F3 major. Section 13 (and 14, 6.8): the Horizon Results Platform duty is placed in the wrong time window

Quote (lines 1180–1182): "Up to one year after the end of the action, to 31 December 2027: use the Horizon Results Platform to find interested parties for each result not exploited within that year". Line 1227: "By 31 December: ... results not exploited entered on the Horizon Results Platform."

What is wrong: GA Annex 5 (line 8886): "If, despite a beneficiary's best efforts, the results are not exploited within one year after the end of the action, the beneficiaries must ... use the Horizon Results Platform". The duty is triggered at the one-year mark and then runs within the four-year exploitation period. It is not a duty that ends on 31 December 2027, and results cannot be "entered by 31 December" because non-exploitation is only established on that date. Decision D17 also lists this rule among the four-year obligations; the draft departs from it.

Fix: move the bullet to the four-year list. Ready text: "for each result not exploited by 31 December 2027 despite best efforts, use the Horizon Results Platform to find interested parties, unless otherwise agreed in writing with the granting authority (Section 6.8);". Leave only the access-rights requests under the one-year heading. In Section 14 change the 2027 cell to "By 31 December: access-rights requests made; results not exploited by then identified for the Horizon Results Platform" and add to 2028: "Q1: results not exploited in 2027 entered on the Horizon Results Platform by their owners." In Section 6.8 add the entry date: "is entered on the Horizon Results Platform by its owner in the first quarter of 2028."

### F8 major. Section 4.10: the LCOE comparison with the proposal target is not on the same basis, and the reason does not explain the gap

Quote (line 876): "LCOE of about €0.054/kWh for the offshore plant, from €0.161/kWh in the reference case | D7.4 version 2.0: €0.076/kWh in the Mediterranean ... | Capital cost and cost of capital (D7.4)".

What is wrong: LCOE reduction is a topic outcome the project had to justify "at project start and end, using a recognised calculation methodology" (Annex 1 Part B line 560). The proposal's €0.054/kWh is for a 10 MWp plant off Portugal (1,645 kWh/m²), a 20-year life and a 4 % equity discount rate; its reference case uses 15 years and 8 % (Annex 1 Part B lines 585–591). D7.4 uses a 50-year structure life, PV replacement after 25 years, 8.5 % and other sites. The reader cannot reconstruct the gap from "capital cost and cost of capital".

Fix: add below the table: "The proposal's LCOE figures and D7.4's are calculated on different bases. The proposal's target of €0.054/kWh assumed an Atlantic site off Portugal with 1,645 kWh/m² of irradiation, a 20-year life, a 4 % equity discount rate for a mature, low-risk technology and €781/kWp; its reference case of €0.161/kWh assumed 15 years, 8 % and €1,560/kWp. D7.4 assumes a 50-year structure life with the PV modules replaced after 25 years and an 8.5 % discount rate, at Mediterranean, Baltic and North Sea sites. The main differences are the discount rate, 8.5 % against 4 %, and the investment, €940/kWp, about 20 % above the target. At 4 % D7.4 gives the Mediterranean plant an NPV of €20.8 million (D7.4, Section 7.4)." Change the reason cell to "Discount rate of 8.5 % against the proposal's 4 %, and an investment about 20 % above the target (D7.4)".
