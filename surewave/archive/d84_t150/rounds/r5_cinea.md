## Findings

### F1 minor. 3.2 KER2: D6.4 cited as a source again, which reverses the round-4 fix

Quote (l.302, maturity cell): "Validated in the laboratory and in the reduced-scale breakwater prototype exposed at Gijón (D4.1, D4.2, D4.3, D6.4, all ACCIONA; Preprints 202611.1592; Section 6.2)".

What is wrong: Annex B (l.1578) says the project records hold no copy of D6.4 and that the results are cited from Preprints 202611.1592. Round 4 (r4disp r4_coord 8) changed 6.2, KER2, R3 and R4 to "the subject of D6.4" for this reason. The one-home pass rewrote the KER2 cell (pre-dedup text: "(D4.3; Task 6.5, ACCIONA, the subject of D6.4; Preprints 202611.1592)") and put D6.4 back as evidence, next to deliverables that were read. A reviewer who looks up D6.4 to check the claim will not find what the cell implies. R3 and R4 (l.343–344) are correct.

Fix (ready text): "Validated in the laboratory (D4.1, D4.2, both ACCIONA) and in the reduced-scale breakwater prototype (D4.3, ACCIONA), exposed at Gijón in Task 6.5, the subject of D6.4 (Preprints 202611.1592; Section 6.2)".

### F2 minor. 4.10 CAPEX row: "fairlead and anchor loads rise" is not in D5.1; 6.5 overstates the tension rise

Quotes: l.843 "the fairlead and anchor loads rise in the optimised designs (Section 6.5)"; l.1030 "The lighter moorings carry higher maximum tensions at the fairlead, up to about 2.5 times the reference value (D5.1, Table 9)".

What is wrong:
- D5.1 reports maximum line tensions at the fairlead only. It gives no anchor loads (D5.1 Table 9 and the paragraph after it, 2024-04-16_surewave_d5.1.md l.1530–1536: "a mooring mass reduction comes with the cost of increased maximum tensions at the fairlead"). "Anchor" came in during round 4 from a reviewer's wording (r4_partners.md l.81), not from D5.1. The pointer to Section 6.5 does not support it either, because 6.5 mentions only the fairlead.
- Per Table 9, the optimised 10 MWp North Sea mooring is 26 % lighter at an unchanged maximum tension (Opt/Ref 1.00). D5.1 itself says "for most designs". The other lighter designs show 1.40, 2.46 and 2.15.

Fix: l.843: "the maximum fairlead tensions rise in most of the optimised designs, up to about 2.5 times (Section 6.5)". l.1030: "Most of the lighter moorings carry higher maximum tensions at the fairlead, up to about 2.5 times the reference value; the 10 MWp North Sea mooring is lighter at an unchanged maximum tension (D5.1, Table 9)."

### F3 minor. 3.4: R8 and R9 are rated below their target TRL with no reason

Quotes: l.348 R8 "target TRL 5 ... WP8 lead's rating: TRL 4–5"; l.349 R9 "target TRL 5 ... WP8 lead's rating: TRL 4–5".

What is wrong: the proposal sets TRL 5 at project end for the SHM approach and the SIF and crack-propagation work, "with comparative analysis with basin-model testing" (GA l.6121–6127). R4 (encapsulated EPS), R5 (ReFRESCO work not reported) and R10 (field validation is further work) state why they fall short. R8 and R9 do not. An external expert compares the rating column with the target column and asks why. D5.7 gives the reason: validation against long-term field data is further work, as 6.6 says (l.1061).

Fix (ready text, R8 and R9 maturity cells, at the end): "WP8 lead's rating: TRL 4–5, below the target TRL 5, since validation with long-term field data from the prototype is further work (D5.7)". Or add one sentence after the table, in l.353: "Results rated below their proposal target are R1, R4, R5 and R8–R10; the reason is given in each row."

### F4 minor. 3.4 R6: the proposal's target TRL is missing

Quote (l.346): "R6 Coupling of an optimisation toolkit with linearised simulation methods for wave-induced response; software tool, MARIN; calculation services for moored multi-body structures".

What is wrong: the intro (l.337) says the proposal column gives the target TRL "where the proposal set them". The proposal sets TRL 5 at project end for the coupled modelling challenge, which explicitly includes the DAKOTA optimisation toolkit combined with linearised calculations (GA l.5970–5997: "AT THE END OF PROJECT (TRL5) ... also including an extended optimization toolkit to allow the simulation of the linearized offshore FPV wave responses"). R5 gives "target TRL 5" from the same passage. R6 does not.

Fix: add "; target TRL 5" to the R6 proposal cell. The rating (TRL 5) then reads as meeting the target.

### F5 minor. Section 15: EBITDA break-even stated without its condition

Quote (l.1309): "Sunlit Sea's financial plan reaches EBITDA break-even in 2030 and a positive net result in 2031, with €5.5M of equity raised in two rounds, in 2027 and 2029. It is most sensitive to the timing of the Norwegian reference plants".

What is wrong: the one-home pass cut this paragraph (dedup_log item 21). The executive summary (l.32) and Section 12 (l.1230) attach the condition "provided / if the Norwegian reference plants come on time", and 4.7 (l.768) shows 2031 EBITDA slightly negative after a one-year delay. Read alone, the conclusion states break-even as unconditional. The next sentence's "most sensitive to the timing" does not tell the reader that the result changes sign.

Fix (ready text): "Sunlit Sea's financial plan reaches EBITDA break-even in 2030 and a positive net result in 2031 if the Norwegian reference plants come on time, with €5.5M of equity raised in two rounds, in 2027 and 2029. A one-year delay of those plants turns 2031 EBITDA slightly negative; the first plants need investment support of about a quarter of their cost (Section 4.7)."

### F6 minor. 2.5 counting: the DeepWind poster is not counted as a presentation, while the DCIS and CINEA posters are

Quotes: l.204 "an event attended counts as a conference, and the talk or poster given there as a presentation or poster"; l.209–210 the DCIS poster counts as "conference, presentation and poster", the CINEA poster as "presentation, poster"; l.221 the EERA DeepWind poster counts only as "conference and poster". A.5 (l.1370–1386) has no DeepWind row.

What is wrong: the rule treats a poster the same way at all three events, but the count does not. The presentation target is met either way (15 against more than 10), so no status changes. The inconsistency is still visible to a reviewer who reconciles A.4–A.6.

Fix: either add DeepWind to A.5 and the 2.5 table (16 presentations) and to the l.221 list ("conference, presentation and poster"), or state the reason in l.204, for example: "A poster counts as a presentation where the record names the presenter; SINTEF's DeepWind poster has no presenter or year in its report."
