## Findings

### F1 major. Annex A.23 and Section 13: five of the seven peer-reviewed papers are not shown in a trusted repository, and the MARINE 2025 paper's licence does not meet the GA (round 2 F6, not fixed)

Quotes: l.1492 "The five papers in *Energies*, *Sensors* and *Electronics* are on the publisher MDPI's site under CC BY 4.0, and the *Sensors* paper is also in PubMed Central ... The MARINE 2025 paper is on Scipedia ... under a Creative Commons Attribution-NonCommercial-ShareAlike licence (CC BY-NC-SA 3.0)"; l.1218 "ensure immediate open access to peer-reviewed publications, with a copy deposited in a trusted repository at the latest at the time of publication, under a CC BY licence or equivalent".

What is wrong:
- Annex 5 requires deposit of the published version or accepted manuscript "in a trusted repository for scientific publications" and open access "via the repository" under CC BY or equivalent (GA l.9216–9224). The AGA states it outright: "Publishing in an open access venue without depositing in a repository, does NOT comply with the open access requirements" (AGA l.18662–18663). Of the seven papers, two are shown in a repository: the *Sensors* paper (PubMed Central) and the FFEMS article (Zenodo community). Energies 17(9) 2059, Energies 17(19) 4873, Energies 19(7) 1609 and Electronics 13(22) 4485 are shown only on the publisher's site.
- The MARINE 2025 paper is under CC BY-NC-SA 3.0. The GA allows non-commercial licences only "for monographs and other long-text formats" (GA l.9221–9225). A conference paper counted as peer-reviewed (l.1279) needs CC BY or equivalent.
- The text lists the obligation as binding without a time limit (l.1218) and, in A.23, shows facts that do not meet it, with no remedy. A project officer reads this as an unreported non-compliance.
- Owner question 16 (owner_questions.md l.88–91) holds the remedy, but the text does not reflect it.

Fix: before submission, deposit the four MDPI papers in the SUREWAVE Zenodo community (CC BY 4.0 allows it), and ask MARIN to deposit the accepted manuscript of the MARINE 2025 paper in Zenodo under CC BY. Then replace the A.23 bullet's first three sentences with (ready text): "All seven peer-reviewed papers are deposited in trusted repositories with open access under CC BY 4.0: the *Sensors* paper in PubMed Central (PMC11244420), the *Fatigue & Fracture of Engineering Materials & Structures* article and the four other MDPI papers in the SUREWAVE community on Zenodo, and the accepted manuscript of the MARINE 2025 paper in Zenodo [doi]; the published MARINE 2025 version is on Scipedia under CC BY-NC-SA 3.0." If the deposits cannot be made before submission, state it (ready text): "The four MDPI papers in *Energies* and *Electronics* are open on the publisher's site and are deposited in the SUREWAVE community on Zenodo by [date]; MARIN deposits the accepted manuscript of the MARINE 2025 paper under CC BY by the same date."

### F2 major. Executive summary and Section 2.5: the headline is silent on the second table, where most reach targets are not met or not measured

Quotes: l.36 "SUREWAVE met or exceeded most of the dissemination and communication targets in the table of Section 2.5 ... Section 2.5 also gives the status of further reach targets of the Grant Agreement and of the dissemination plan D8.5"; l.173 "the second table lists them with their status".

What is wrong: the second table (l.220–243) has 22 targets. By its own status column: 3 met (four R&D lines, published impact assessments, partnerships), 1 met for 2024 only (web visits a year), 3 partly met, 5 not met (citations 41 against 140, webinars and workshops 8 against 10, media mentions 13 against 20, media publications 13 against 25, replication guide), 7 not measured (academics reached, workshop experts, digital-campaign stakeholders, quarterly trainings, industry stakeholders reached, new contacts, final-conference stakeholders), 1 not shown (PhD), 2 open. The executive summary names the exceptions of the first table one by one and gives none of this. A reviewer who reaches the second table after reading "met or exceeded most" sees an overstatement by omission (brief requirement 4). Seven "not measured" also tell the reviewer that the monitoring of Subtask 8.2.3 did not cover reach; the plan should say so once.

Fix: add after the D8.5 partnership sentence in the executive summary (l.36), ready text: "Of the 22 further reach targets in the second table of Section 2.5, three are met and one is met for 2024 only; three are partly met, five are not met (among them citations, 41 against more than 140, and media publications, 13 against more than 25), seven were not measured, a new PhD is not shown, and two are open or apply after the project." Put the same sentence, without the examples, after "the second table lists them with their status" in l.173, and add there: "The project measured counts of items, not their reach; the attendance, readership and contact targets were not monitored."

### F5 minor. Sections 6.8, 11, 13 and executive summary: two interpretations of the Horizon Results Platform rule are written as Grant Agreement text

Quotes: l.1063 "The Grant Agreement duty applies to each result, not to a KER as a whole ... The duty then applies for the rest of the four-year exploitation period"; l.34 and l.1171 "goes on the Horizon Results Platform from then on, for the rest of the four-year period".

What is wrong: Annex 5 says only that if "the results are not exploited within one year after the end of the action, the beneficiaries must ... use the Horizon Results Platform" (GA l.8886–8888). It sets no duration for the platform use. The AGA frames the rule around key exploitable results: "The use of the Horizon Results Platform becomes mandatory, if one year after the end of the action, key exploitable results are not exploited", and notes that deliverables are not necessarily KERs (AGA l.17826–17844). The per-result procedure and the four-year duration are a sound, stricter proposal (D17, D23), but "The Grant Agreement duty applies to each result, not to a KER as a whole" contradicts the Commission's own guidance and a project officer will notice.

Fix: l.1063, ready text: "The Grant Agreement requires that, if despite a beneficiary's best efforts its results are not exploited within one year after the end of the action, by 31 December 2027, the beneficiaries use the Horizon Results Platform to find interested parties, unless otherwise agreed in writing with the granting authority. The WP8 lead proposes to apply this to each result, not only to each KER, since a KER can be exploited while one of its results is not, and to keep a result on the platform until it is exploited or the four-year exploitation period ends on 31 December 2030." In l.34, l.1171 and l.1202 write "... goes on the Horizon Results Platform; this plan keeps it there for the rest of the four-year period".
