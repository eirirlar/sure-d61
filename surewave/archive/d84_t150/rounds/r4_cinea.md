## Findings

### F1 major. 2.5 and A.23: the open-access deposits are late, the text does not say so, and the MARINE 2025 CC BY deposit is stated as certain

Quotes: l.252 "The partners deposit the other five before this plan is submitted, MARIN's paper at MARINE 2025 ... as its accepted manuscript under CC BY"; l.1549 "Two of the seven papers with stated peer review meet this today. The partners deposit the papers not yet in a trusted repository ... before this plan is submitted. For the MARINE 2025 paper ... MARIN deposits the accepted manuscript under CC BY."

What is wrong:
- Annex 5 requires the deposit "at the latest at the time of publication" (GA l.9216–9224; the D8.4 text itself says so at l.1263 and l.1549). Four of the five papers were published in 2024–2026 (A.1), so any deposit now is late by up to two and a half years. The text reports the status honestly but presents the deposit as routine. A project officer reads the silence as a non-compliance that the beneficiaries have not recognised.
- Whether MARIN may deposit the accepted manuscript of the MARINE 2025 paper under CC BY depends on the rights it kept when it published with CIMNE under CC BY-NC-SA 3.0. Annex 5 makes the beneficiary responsible for retaining the rights needed (GA open-science provisions, l.9216–9225). If the rights were transferred, the paper cannot be brought into compliance, and the text then states something untrue.
- The deposit is owner action 1, so its timing is not a finding; the missing acknowledgement and the unconditional licence claim are text errors.

Fix (l.1549, ready text): "Two of the seven papers with stated peer review meet this requirement. The other five were not deposited at publication, as the Grant Agreement requires; the partners deposit them in the SUREWAVE community on Zenodo before this plan is submitted. For the MARINE 2025 paper, whose published licence excludes commercial use, MARIN deposits the accepted manuscript under CC BY if its publishing agreement with CIMNE allows it; if not, the paper stays open under CC BY-NC-SA 3.0, which does not meet the Grant Agreement licence requirement, and MARIN reports this in the final periodic report." In l.252 keep one sentence and a pointer (see F15).

### F2 minor. Section 3.4: no achieved TRL per result against the proposal's target TRLs

Quotes: l.331 "The proposal column gives the owner, type, business strategy and target TRL where the proposal set them"; l.347 "The owners did not rate the TRL of their individual results at project end, apart from Sunlit Sea's rating of the CONNECT platform at TRL 4–5".

A KER analysis without a TRL per result is the gap an external expert would name first.

### F3 minor. R3 (3.4, 4.10): the switch from UHPFRC to HPC and LWAC is not argued, though D4.1 gives the reason, and "as the proposal set" overstates

Quotes: l.337 "Neither D4.1 nor ACCIONA's final summary discusses UHPFRC"; l.337 maturity "Validated in the laboratory and in the reduced-scale prototype exposed for nine months at Gijón, as the proposal set"; l.856 "HPC and LWAC shell mixes in place of UHPFRC".

What is wrong: a change of a GA result needs a reason. D4.1 states why the shell mixes are HPC and LWAC: the cellular core absorbs water, so the shell must also cover the bottom and be the waterproof layer, "very dense and as light or thin as possible ... That is why lightweight aggregate concrete (LWAC) and high-performance concrete (HPC) were investigated for the structural shell" (D4.1 l.215). "As the proposal set" attaches the proposal's validation to a different material.

Fix (l.337, delivered column, replace the sentence "Neither D4.1 nor ...", ready text): "D4.1 explains the choice of shell concretes: because the cellular core absorbs water, the shell must also close the bottom and act as the waterproof layer, so it must be very dense and as light or thin as possible, and LWAC and HPC were investigated for it. Neither D4.1 nor ACCIONA's final summary discusses UHPFRC; ACCIONA's mid-term entry in D8.5 still named it." Maturity column: "Validated in the laboratory and in the reduced-scale prototype exposed for nine months at Gijón: the validation route the proposal set, applied to HPC and LWAC in place of UHPFRC (Section 6.2)". In l.856, Reason column, add "the shell must be waterproof and as light or thin as possible (D4.1)".

### F9 minor. 4.2, 4.5, 4.9: TRL 6 for a plant in commercial operation is not explained

Quotes: l.452 "reach TRL 6 for sheltered sites through the first Norwegian reference plants"; l.637 "TRL 6 for sheltered sites after the first Norwegian reference plant's first winter (Q2 2028)"; M7 (l.838).

What is wrong: in the Horizon Europe TRL scale (General Annexes of the work programme, annex B), TRL 6 is "technology demonstrated in relevant environment" and TRL 7 "system prototype demonstration in operational environment". A grid-connected plant selling power at its host's port is the operational environment. An external expert will ask why a year of commercial operation gives TRL 6 and not 7, and what TRL 7 then requires.

Fix (l.637, ready text): "TRL 6 for sheltered sites after the first Norwegian reference plant's first winter (Q2 2028): the platform demonstrated in its environment through one winter; TRL 7 follows when the first multi-megawatt plant has operated for a year with loads and yield within design values (2029, decision gate 4)." If Sunlit Sea prefers to keep TRL 6 only, give the reason in one clause (for example: a single small plant, one winter).
