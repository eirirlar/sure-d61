### 1. Major. The full-edge (third-generation) hinge is said to have been tested in MARIN's second basin campaign; MARIN's own paper and Section 4.1 show a paired connector (Sections 4.1, 6.1, 7.1)

Quotes:
- Line 383: "The hinge redesign has been tested at SINTEF (D6.3) and in MARIN's second basin campaign (D8.3)".
- Line 1082: "Sunlit Sea's SUREWAVE results are the Generation 2 architecture with the redesigned full-edge hinge, tested by SINTEF (D6.3) and in MARIN's second basin campaign (D8.3)".
- Line 893: "Several prototypes were CAD-modelled, 3D-printed and cast in polyurethane, and tested mechanically at SINTEF and in MARIN's wave basin. Hinges distributed along the full edge of the floats improved load capacity and durability."

What is wrong:
- MARIN's paper describes the second campaign's connector as "a new design of the polyurethane connector, longer and thicker than the first generation" and states that "adjacent FPV modules are connected through a pair of connectors" (surewave/background/2026-03-25_bunnik_hydrodynamic_simulations_2mw_offshore_solar_fpv.md:70–71). Its line 122 says the second test series used "an improved connector design with higher compressive stiffness".
- D8.4 itself says this at line 395: "an improved connector, longer and thicker than the first generation and fitted in pairs between adjacent floats".
- On D8.4's own numbering (line 390), the paired corner hinge is the second generation and the full-edge hinge the third. The full-edge hinge was therefore tested only as a one-fifth segment at SINTEF (D6.3). It was not tested in the basin.
- D8.3:202 ("The second campaign evaluated the redesigned hinge") is Sunlit Sea's own loose wording, and lines 383 and 1082 inherit it.
- MARIN reads D8.4. Lines 383 and 1082 credit its campaign with testing a design it did not test. Line 1082 then uses that test to define Sunlit Sea's results in the IP section.
- Line 893 follows Sunlit Sea's input ("extensive mechanical testing at SINTEF and MARIN", 2026-09-30_sunlit_sea_exploitation_summary.md:41) but adds "in MARIN's wave basin" next to the cast polyurethane prototypes. The basin models were 1:10 models with scaled hinges (rejection r1disp_C, r1_cold#3), not Generation 2 casts.

Fix:
- Line 383: "The redesigned full-edge connection has been tested as a one-fifth segment at SINTEF (D6.3). MARIN's second basin campaign tested an improved connector, longer and thicker and fitted in pairs, with no measurable buckling or snap loading (Energies 19(7) 1609; D8.3). The full-edge hinge has not been tested in a basin."
- Line 1082: "Sunlit Sea's SUREWAVE results are the redesigned hinge, from the paired connector tested in MARIN's second basin campaign to the full-edge connection tested as a segment by SINTEF (D6.3), the nearshore wave and wind models, the wave-corrected performance ratio and the wave-loss module (Section 6.1)." (See also finding 4 on "Generation 2 architecture".)
- Line 893: "Several prototypes were CAD-modelled, 3D-printed and cast in polyurethane. SINTEF tested segments of the new connection (D6.3), and MARIN's second basin campaign tested an improved connector at 1:10 scale (Section 4.1)."
- Owner question 53 (mapping of the generations) then needs only a confirmation that the second-campaign connector is the paired v2 corner hinge of D7.6.
