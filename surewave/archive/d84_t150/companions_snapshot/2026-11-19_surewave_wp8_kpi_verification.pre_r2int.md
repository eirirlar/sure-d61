# SUREWAVE WP8 KPIs — verification of actuals against primary evidence

Checked 19–23 November 2026. Covers every KPI line in the WP8 KPI tracker of 29 September 2026 (19 lines) and the three D8.5 KPIs the tracker does not carry (conference workshops, SAB workshops, newsletters). For each KPI: every item found, its source, and the strength of that source. Targets are listed for reference only.

Method: a first pass with curl (Crossref, OpenAIRE, Zenodo, LinkedIn, surewave.eu, YouTube, media links), then a second pass by six parallel research agents (Ceit publications; IFEU conferences; MARIN events and articles; Sunlit Sea, SINTEF and CINEA events; ACCIONA and Clement media; videos, interviews and print material). Every agent finding used below was re-checked against its source, except where marked "agent only".

## Evidence levels

- V — proven. The item itself, or an independent public record of it, was found and checked: Crossref/OpenAIRE record, conference proceedings or programme, an organiser's page or file, another project's page, a media article, a YouTube or Vimeo video, a partner's public LinkedIn post or web page, a newsletter PDF, a file in the repo.
- O — own public record. A dated item published by the consortium itself (surewave.eu news, Sunlit Sea's YouTube channel titles, a registry entry submitted by the partner). Counts as proof when it documents a concrete, countable thing (rule set by Eirik on 19 Nov 2026).
- L — listed only. A search index or Google News record confirms headline and date, or archive.org holds a copy of the URL, but the content was not read (site blocks automated access).
- P — partner statement. Stated in a partner's written KPI reply of 30 Sep – 6 Oct 2026. Not proof.
- S — self-asserted. Appears only in the tracker or in the consortium's own report text. Not evidence.
- X — contradicted or not found after search.
- E — can only come from Sunlit Sea's records.

"Weak" marks a proven item whose link to SUREWAVE is indirect (SUREWAVE not named in the record).

The second reporting period runs to the end of the project, 31 December 2026 (D8.4 §2.5). This record marks items dated after 30 September 2026, the cut-off of the tracker and of the partners' KPI replies, as "after RP2"; the label means after 30 September 2026, and these items fall within the second reporting period.
