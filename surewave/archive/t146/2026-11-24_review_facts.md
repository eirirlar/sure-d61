## Major

M1. C9 (SME subsection), Sunlit Sea bullet: "redesigned the hinge between the floats, which raised the average tensile capacity from about 3.7 kN to 8.4 kN".
Source: D84 §4.1, lines 186–193, sets out why these two figures do not compare. 3.69 kN is the mean for complete first-generation hinges (D6.1). 8.36 kN is a one-fifth segment of the third-generation full-edge connection, tested with a steel rod in place of the rope. With the polypropylene rope that the design uses, the segment reached 2.04 kN. D6.3 scales the full connection to about 10 kN with the rope and about 42 kN with a steel rod, and the design value (D7.2) is 37 kN against 14 kN. D84 line 176 also lists the D8.3 "three times as strong" comparison, made the same way, as superseded. WC line 361 (WP6, SINTEF) already carries "3.69 kN to 8.36 kN, about 127%". The paste-in repeats this comparison in Sunlit Sea's own text, where D8.4 has already disowned it.
Fix: give the like-for-like statement, for example "the redesigned full-edge hinge has a design break load of 37 kN against 14 kN for the paired second-generation hinges (D7.2)", or "scaled from segment tests to about 10 kN for the full connection with the rope (D6.3), against 3.69 kN for the first-generation hinge". Optionally flag WC line 361 to the coordinator.

- WC line 135, the Section 1.1 objectives summary table, row O7: "Mediterranean LCOE 0.062 €/kWh, positive NPV and IRR | ✅ Achieved But calculated LCoE is slightly higher than the DoA LCOE target". No entry replaces it. The status text "slightly higher" also stops being true at 41 % above the target.
- WC line 4561 (Section 1.3, Topic Outcome 3, the paragraph after the one that A13 replaces): "The difference between the €62/MWh result and the €54/MWh target reflects…". A13 does not reach it.

A13 treats the formulas as conditional ("If the report shows the percentage as a formula"), but they are there. The surrounding prose ("approximately 14.8% above", "approximately 61.5%") also has to change, and A13 gives the replacement formulas only.
The count is therefore at least 19 places, not 17.

M3. B3, Task 2.4 paragraph: "the SAB took part in an online workshop on 19 November 2024, where project results were presented and the members asked about cost, mismatch losses, environmental monitoring, the LCOE target and equipment on the breakwater".
Source: `2024-11-19_surewave_lunch_webinar_summary.md` describes the event as a public lunch webinar announced on LinkedIn, with 31 sign-ups and about 23 participants. The questions came from "attendees": two attendees asked about mismatch, the chair of a small-wind-turbine company asked about turbines on the breakwater, and a University of Malta PhD student asked about models. The summary never names a questioner as an SAB member. KPI §5 and §21 count the event as the SAB workshop because of Clement's reply. A reviewer can check the recording on YouTube.
Fix: "the SAB was invited to an online workshop on 19 November 2024, a public webinar where project results were presented and participants asked about…", or drop "the members".

## Minor

m1. Part A intro and D7.4 date. "D7.4 version 2.0 (20 November 2026)" matches D74 version table (2.0, 20.11.2026). C8 asks `[CONFIRM: 31 May 2025?]` for the due date. D74's own header (lines 30–36) gives "Due date: 30.05.2025, Delivery date: 30.05.2025", and version 1.0 is dated 28.04.2025. Use these instead of guessing 31 May.

m2. A0: "corrects errors in version 1.0 with a rebuilt and independently tested cost model". D74 §2.5 (line 486) says "every calculation was checked against a separate implementation of the model with automated tests". "Independently tested" suggests a third party. Use "rebuilt and tested against a separate implementation".

m3. A2: "…within the €62–106/MWh SET Plan range for floating offshore wind cited in the DoA, which gives a viable business case for the Mediterranean." The viability follows from NPV €7.3M and IRR 15.6 % (D74 §7.1.1), not from the SET Plan range. The logic is loose.

m4. A11 Baltic assessment: "viable with favourable financing or nearshore sites". D74 (lines 345, 1293) supports "viable at a lower cost of capital (8 % or lower) or with support". "Nearshore sites" comes from D84 (exec summary, "assessed site by site"), not D7.4. Acceptable, but the table is labelled D7.4.

m5. Part A table, Baltic NPV "about €0 (break-even)". D74 gives −€0.03M. Fine. The Baltic payback "not reached" is the base case; D74 §7.3 gives 17 years in the optimistic scenario. No change needed.

m6. C3, Task 8.4: "Following the proposal's five steps (KER and IPR analysis, value innovation and market analysis, value-proposition testing, business models and plans)". The parenthesis lists four items, which is the GA task text (WC Task 8.4: 1)–4)). D84 §2.4 names a different five-step list: identification and IPR management, evaluation of business potential, business models, IPR analysis, business and exploitation plans. Either say "four steps" or use D84 §2.4's list.

m7. C3 O4, C10 Action 1, C3 Task 8.4: "maps the 27 results reported by the partners to seven KERs". D84 §3.1 (line 125) says four of the 27 (three network and visibility gains, and Clement's contribution to engineering practice) are reported as impact, and the other 23 fit the KERs. The D84 executive summary says "mapped to them the 27 results", so D84 is inconsistent with itself. "27 results, 23 of them mapped to seven KERs" is the accurate form.

m8. C3 O4 and C4: "Results are already carried forward in SuRE (Sunlit Sea), CIRCWIND, SFI BLUES and FlexiShape (SINTEF)…".
- FlexiShape is led by MIKO Marine; SINTEF is a partner (project_facts line 148). SFI BLUES is hosted by SINTEF Ocean.
- D84 §10 lists FlexiShape (2021–2025) and SFI BLUES as "earlier and parallel" projects, and FlexiShape is completed, so "carried forward into" a finished project reads oddly.
- MariSol (Sunlit Sea, RCN 317808) is omitted, although D84 counts five projects that used SUREWAVE results, MariSol among them.
"(SINTEF's contributions)" would be safer.

m9. C9: "Three of the seven beneficiaries are SMEs: Sunlit Sea, Clement Germany and IFEU." The GA supports this (lines 7997, 8004 "IFEU is a non-profit SME", 8114). Noted as verified; no change.

m10. C9: "the public deliverables, the design basis, the cost model and the economic assessment are open on Zenodo". The cost model is on Zenodo (10.5281/zenodo.17534609; D84 §6.1) and so is D2.1 (WC §4.1). KPI "Open science" lists D2.1 and D2.4 among the public deliverables, but not D2.2 or D2.3. "The design basis" is supported only for D2.1.

m11. C9: "the modular 2 MW architecture". D74 assesses 2 MW, 5 × 2 MW and full-scale 10 MW. The base case is the full-scale 10 MW ring, and D74 calls 2 MW and 5 × 2 MW "significantly less economically viable" (line 91). Presenting the 2 MW module as the architecture overstates its role.

m12. B3: "Its seven members represent project development, FPV technology, regulation, finance, insurance, energy utilities, installation, O&M and policy-making". D2.4 (lines 211–262) also has an environmental-agencies category, filled by Wageningen University and Research. The list omits it, and SAB members reviewing D8.2 include "representatives of environmental agencies" (D8.2 line 223). Add "environmental research". Four of the seven member organisations are companies (Greenlight, Scandinavian Solar Parks, Innovex, BayWa r.e.), which is verified.

m13. B3: "The SAB's points on grid access, permitting, certification and insurability were used in the exploitation plan (D8.4)." D84 does not attribute these points to the SAB. WC line 4838 attributes them to "stakeholder and partner feedback" (coordinator text). D2.4 (line 277) says the SAB gave feedback on "project development requirements, technological advancements, regulatory challenges, and market acceptance". Not supported as written.

m14. C2 and C3 Task 8.3: "Technical brochure (D8.3), written by Sunlit Sea and SINTEF and reviewed by the partners." KPI §18 records reviews by Clement Germany (18 Nov) and MARIN (19 Nov) only, so "reviewed by Clement Germany and MARIN" is what the record shows. D84 §6.1 says Sunlit Sea "led D8.3 and D8.5 with SINTEF". "Written by" is consistent with that.

m15. C3 Task 8.3: the list of conferences includes "EU PVSEC 2026". KPI §1 and §2 mark EU PVSEC as S/X: not found in Crossref or by title search, and counted in the D8.4 totals only as partner-reported. Naming it as an example invites a check. Use a proven one instead (Floating Solar in Europe 2025, or the 88th and 91st LCA Discussion Forums).

m16. C2 and C3 Task 8.5: "the LinkedIn page reached 694 followers and 40 posts". 40 includes 6 posts scheduled for 24 Nov – 29 Dec 2026; 34 were published by 18 Nov (KPI §7). The C5 note explains this, but C2 and C3 state 40 without the qualifier, and "reached" is in the past tense.

m17. C3 Task 8.2: "checked every indicator against publisher records, Crossref, OpenAIRE, Zenodo, the website and the LinkedIn page". Several indicators rest on Sunlit Sea's own records (website visits from the discontinued Matomo account, the SAB March 2025 meeting, newsletters III–V, three 2024 interviews; KPI "Counting rules"), and the partner-reported items (P) are included. "Checked every indicator against … or the project's own records" is accurate.

m18. C3 Task 8.5: "two press releases (project start, 1 November 2022; Port of Gijón prototype, 11 October 2024)". The ACCIONA item of 11 Oct 2024 is titled "ACCIONA participates in european project to address the challenges of floating photovoltaics" (KPI §14 item 5). KPI and project_facts call it the announcement "at the prototype milestone". "Port of Gijón prototype" as its subject is plausible but not confirmed from the text. "ACCIONA's announcement at the prototype milestone" is safer.

m19. C4 Significant Results and C2: the publication topics ("breakwater aerodynamics, wave-basin validation, farm-scale hydrodynamics, corrosion sensing, wireless monitoring and fatigue-crack modelling") leave out the concrete-interface and reinforcement modelling papers (GEF 2024; MZT 2024; KPI §1 items 8–9). Add "concrete modelling".

m20. C10 Action 2 item 3: "qualification and certification of the critical components". D84 §4.5 describes qualification against DNV-ST-C108, DNV-ST-E309 and DNV-RP-0584, and a DNV design-basis assessment from Q4 2027. It does not describe certification. Use "qualification against the DNV floating-solar standards". Also, "replaces the proposal's direct step from TRL 5 to a 25 MW offshore pilot" goes beyond D84 §4.10, which says "25 MW offshore pilot in 2025–2027; … 1,240 MW by 2032" and names no TRL. Minor.

m21. Reporting-period definition. The draft uses "M19–M48", following the WC header (01.04.2024 – 30.09.2026), and at the same time counts items "before the project end on 31 December 2026" (C5 note). D84 gives the project as 51 months to 31 Dec 2026, with D8.4 due at M48. KPI line 19 says RP2 runs to 31 Dec 2026. The draft should not pair "M19–M48" with post-September items without saying which applies. A reviewer can see M51 in the amended GA.
