## 17. Section 1.3 Impact — new SME subsection

Location: end of Section 1.3, after the last "Overall Assessment" paragraph (ending "…the exploitation framework required to pursue them."), before "1.4 Update of the plan for exploitation and dissemination of results".

Action: insert

Impact on SMEs

Does (or will) the work carried out have an impact on SMEs?

Yes. Three of the seven beneficiaries are SMEs: Sunlit Sea, Clement Germany and IFEU.

- Sunlit Sea, the FPV technology provider, has used SUREWAVE to take its FPV system from concept to a tested design for exposed water. The redesigned polyurethane hinge, validated through the connection, fatigue and basin tests, raised average tensile capacity by about 127 % and removed the buckling and snap loading of the original design. The wave and wind models and the validated design basis now support Sunlit Sea's site screening and energy-yield planning. The results continue in the Horizon Europe project SuRE and in an EIC Transition application, and the integrated system is Sunlit Sea's lead commercial result.
- Clement Germany has extended its floating breakwater design, mooring and anchoring know-how to the protection of FPV arrays. This opens FPV as a market alongside its marinas, ports and other floating infrastructure.
- IFEU has applied its integrated life-cycle sustainability assessment to offshore FPV and reuses the method in assessment services and publicly funded studies.

For the wider SME sector, offshore and nearshore FPV is still an early market where much of the supply chain consists of small technology providers, engineering consultancies, marine contractors and local installers. SUREWAVE lowers their entry barriers in three ways. First, the public design basis, metocean dataset, cost model and economic assessment are openly available on Zenodo, so SMEs can screen sites and test business cases without commissioning their own studies. Second, the modular 2 MW architecture splits assembly, installation and maintenance into work packages of a size that regional marine contractors can deliver. Third, the validated testing and modelling methods give SME suppliers a route to qualify their components before certification.
