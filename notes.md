>  The customer requirement in these two segments is not hypothetical — it is already under contract negotiation.
A statement that smells desperation to disprove our irrelevance.

Generally, I notice that you often come with statements such as:
"This is not only a bla bla bla - it is the bla bla of the bla bla bla".
The key her being "not".
In general, don't say what you don't want the reader to focus on. Say what he SHOULd focus on instead. Say it in multiple ways. Make THAT piece interresting. If you absolutely have to say "not", put it behind the core message, so the core message is the first thing he reads. If he reads the "not" piece first that's what sticks in his mind.
