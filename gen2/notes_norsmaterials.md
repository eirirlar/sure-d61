# Norsmaterials — lookup notes

Working notes from the pre-brief research on **Norsmaterials AS**. Used to calibrate the level of detail in `norsmaterials_brief.md` (skip what they already know; emphasise what is Sunlit-Sea-specific).

## Company at a glance

- **Location:** Industrivegen 20, 6823 Sandane, Norway.
- **Heritage:** Originated from **Strukturplast AS**; decades of polymer engineering experience.
- **Positioning:** Custom polymer solutions for extreme environments, designed around casting and moulding at their own facility.
- **Contact:** contact@norsmaterials.com, +47 57 86 61 33.

## Product families

- **NORSelast®** — "the strongest elastic polymer in the world" (their tagline). Variants listed on the site: **01, 02, S4, EL, PIR, Spray, AF**. Key differentiators mentioned:
  - **01** — high tensile strength and abrasion resistance.
  - **02** — hybrid composites with enhanced mechanical properties.
  - **PIR** — stiffer components, higher-temperature use.
- **NORSfoam®** — complementary foam material.
- **Hybrid solutions** — combinations of the two families.
- Chemistry is **not stated on the public page**. Strukturplast lineage + "cast elastic polymer" strongly suggests a polyurethane elastomer family, but this must be confirmed with Norsmaterials in the first meeting — do not assume in the brief.

## Industries served

Maritime; energy (oil & gas, subsea, renewables); **aquaculture**; defence. Marine-specific selling points on their site include:

- Impact- and wear-resistant solutions for vessels.
- High-performance seals.
- Weight reduction and corrosion protection for offshore structures.
- **Biofouling-resistant materials for fish-farming environments.**

The aquaculture + biofouling angle is the most direct overlap with Sunlit Sea's operating environment.

## Capabilities

- Component casting (in-house at Sandane).
- Fast-track development cycles.
- Multi-client products.
- Circular-economy solutions (they market this explicitly).
- R&D team that "collaborates with clients to develop next-generation solutions tailored to industry needs."
- They invite customers to "experience our production process firsthand" — open to on-site visits.

## What is *not* on the public page

- No hardness (Shore A/D) numbers.
- No operating temperature window.
- No UV-resistance data.
- No cure chemistry or pot-life data.
- No 25-year marine durability data (though they claim NORSelast® has "50+ years of durability in maritime settings").
- No adhesion data to aluminium alloys or laminated glass.

All of the above are things we need to ask them directly — they should be on the meeting agenda, not guessed in the brief.

## Calibration for the brief

- **Skip:** PU-101 explanations, generic marine PU background, generic UV degradation background — they know this better than we do.
- **Emphasise:** our specific geometry (pressed 0.8 mm Al 5083-H111 cup), our specific interfaces (PU-to-Al, PU-to-laminated glass), our specific durability target (25 years aquatic), our specific optical target (low absorptivity off-white top), and our development pipeline (SuRE model chain + risk-based P3→P4→P5 iteration).
- **Ask:** which NORSelast variant fits, whether they can help design test casts iteratively, and whether they have prior work on similar problems.
