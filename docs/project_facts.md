- Hærøya Industripark (HIP) / Hærøya Nett — host and grid operator at Gunneklevfjorden. Hydro's remediation of the industrial basin has capped the formerly polluted seabed, which complicates mooring; subsurface anchoring in the capped seabed accepted by Miljødirektoratet as treating authority.

- Innovasjon Norge — Norwegian state enterprise. Two outstanding loans to Sunlit Sea: ~3 MNOK and ~0.5 MNOK remaining. Loans will not convert to grants (confirmed 2026).
- Tongge (Weihai, China) — current PU casting vendor; cast Gen 2 Prototype 3 in a multi-part aluminium mould.
- Norsmaterials — Norwegian PU specialist in Sandane. Partner candidate for Gen 2 PU formulation, mould design, cure behaviour. Not yet contracted.
- SINTEF AS (PIC 910945140) — coordinator of both SUREWAVE and the EIC Transition SUREWAVE and EUPI-PV applications. Based in Trondheim.

- Miljöbalken 11 kap. — Vattenverksamhet. Main tillstånd for the FPV demonstrator. Jurisdiction: Mark- och miljödomstolen (Nacka tingsrätt for Gotland).

- Miljöbalken 7 kap. 28 a § — Natura 2000 appropriate assessment, if screening triggers it.

- Ellagen — Swedish electricity act; concession regime for generation and distribution.
- EIFS 2018:2 — Energimarknadsinspektionen föreskrifter on technical connection requirements (grid code).
