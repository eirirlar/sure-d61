# Case: Prinsippendring / Årsregnskap 2025 / Fortsatt drift

Case index — pointers only. Files live in their original folders.

## Status

Open. 2025 annual accounts in auditor review; principle-change bilag (383/2025) booked; going-concern dialogue with auditor active (nummerert brev nr. 2 answered, emphasis-of-matter path agreed).

## Main concept

For the 2025 annual accounts, Sunlit Sea has reclassified development costs (SuRE pressverktøy work) from P&L expense to capitalised intangible asset per `regnskapsloven § 5-6` and `NRS(F) Nedskrivning av anleggsmidler`. The change triggers:

1. Retrospective principle-change treatment with 2024 equity-side adjustment per `NRS 8` and the auditor's 2026-08-26 dialogue.
2. Impairment test at both 31.12.2024 and 31.12.2025 per `NRS(F) Nedskrivning`.
3. Separate going-concern assessment (auditor's nummerert brev nr. 2 / 2025 flagged uncertainty; board responded with a documented 12-month liquidity view; emphasis-of-matter route agreed with auditor).
