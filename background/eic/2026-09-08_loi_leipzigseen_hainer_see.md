## Letter of Intent

This letter of intent (the "Agreement") is entered into between:

Clement Germany, registered with the German Register of Business Enterprises with registration number HRB 11497; and LeipzigSeen registration number HRB 19741 (with its affiliates, the "Project Partner").

The Technology Provider, Project Partner are jointly referred to as the "Parties", and each as a "Party".

The parties intend to develop 1 floating solar project of total capacity approximately 50 kWp near Leipzig, Hainer See. The project is intended to be a part of a joint research project that the Technology Provider and other academic and industrial entities are involved in. The projects have an expected lifetime of approximately 12 months.

After the research projects are finished (or, alternatively, if the research projects do not accomplish the required funding), the parties intend to either decommission the projects, or, formalize an agreement that specifies a commercial strategy that should include price targets, bankable options, manufacture guaranties, maintenance procedures, and other relevant legal obligations an formalities.

This letter of intent does not govern ownership of intellectual property rights. Neither does it govern any non-disclosure aspects of the collaboration.

This letter of intent is valid from the signing date, below, until purchase orders, or other contractual agreements have been signed. However, this letter of intent is automatically cancelled if no collaboration agreements are signed before [28th Feb 2027].

## Notes on the document

Observations that matter for the proposal, kept separate from the transcription above.

**Company name.** The document spells the site owner **LeipzigSeen GmbH** (with a final *n*) in the letterhead, the body and the signature block. The PDF filename says "LeipzigSee". HRB 19741 is the registration number given. Use LeipzigSeen GmbH in the proposal unless the register says otherwise.

**Capacity.** The letter names **approximately 50 kWp**, which is the bottom of the 50–300 kWp band described in §1.2 of the proposal. Site access is agreed for that figure, not for the top of the band.

**Duration.** Approximately 12 months expected lifetime, which matches the operational campaign in WP5.

**End of project.** The letter gives two paths once the research project ends: decommission, or formalise a commercial agreement covering price targets, bankable options, manufacture guarantees and maintenance procedures. It does **not** state that the site owner will retain the breakwater pontoons.

**Expiry.** The letter is automatically cancelled if no collaboration agreement is signed before **28 February 2027**. Grant agreement signature for this call is indicatively within six months of the 16 September 2026 deadline, which lands close to or after that date.

**Scope limits.** The letter expressly does not govern IP ownership and does not govern non-disclosure.

**Drafting slips in the original**, transcribed as written: "manufacture guaranties" (guarantees), "an formalities" (and formalities), and "The Technology Provider, Project Partner are jointly referred to" (missing conjunction). "Technology Provider" is used without being defined; from the structure it is Clement Germany.
