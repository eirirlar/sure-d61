Under this aid scheme aid to undertakings will be awarded as de minimis aid. EEA regulations set out strict requirements for de minimis aid, including limiting the amount of aid that may be awarded to a single undertaking to maximum EUR 300 000 over a period of three fiscal years (i.e. the current fiscal year and the two preceding years).
