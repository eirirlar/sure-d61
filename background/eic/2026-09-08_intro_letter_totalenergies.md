Subject: EIC Transition proposal — invitation to explore a role for TotalEnergies

Hi,

We were in touch back in 2023 regarding the application of Sunlit Sea's floating solar technology in collaboration with TotalEnergies.

I am contacting you regarding a new EIC Transition proposal that we are currently preparing as a follow-up to our SUREWAVE project, in which Sunlit Sea, SINTEF and CLEMENT are key partners.

The objective is to move the technology from component and subsystem validation towards an integrated pilot-scale demonstration and prepare it for future commercial deployment.

The current plan is to design, manufacture, deploy, and operate a pilot FPV system in the range of 100–300 kWp at a demonstration site in Germany, where we will validate:

- The floating breakwater concept.
- FPV performance under real operating conditions.
- Structural behaviour and reliability.
- Monitoring and predictive maintenance frameworks.
- Energy yield and commercial viability.

Given TotalEnergies' scale in floating solar deployment (notably the Obourg project) and your broader position as a global renewable-energy developer, we believe you could bring significant value to the proposal as a future technology adopter and commercialisation partner. SUREWAVE would extend the deployment envelope of proven FPV concepts like Obourg into locations with rougher waters that are currently inaccessible to conventional inland FPV designs. In particular, your expertise in:

- Utility-scale floating and ground-mount PV project development,
- Techno-economic assessment and PPA structuring,
- Market uptake and industrial-customer commercialisation,

would considerably strengthen the pathway-to-market and scale-up strategy of the project.

We would therefore like to explore whether TotalEnergies would be interested in joining the consortium and contributing to the demonstration, business development, and future deployment aspects of the project.

If this could be of interest, I would be happy to organise a short meeting to present the project concept and discuss possible roles.

The time until the application deadline is unfortunately a bit short, so I would appreciate a quick reply on this matter.

Thank you in advance, and I look forward to hearing from you.

Best regards,
Eirik Larsen
Sunlit Sea AS
