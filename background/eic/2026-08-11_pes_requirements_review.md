# PES requirements review

Review of what Sunlit Sea needs to submit a PES application to RCN (Research Council of Norway) to fund the effort of preparing the EIC Transition SUREWAVE-based proposal (see MoM 2026-07-31 and WP structure 2026-08-10).

## 1. What PES is, briefly

- **PES** = Prosjektetableringsstøtte (Project Establishment Support). RCN-administered coordination and support activity that funds Norwegian applicants' *preparation* of Horizon Europe applications.
- Aid form: **de minimis aid** — subject to the EUR 300 000 / 3 fiscal years ceiling per "single undertaking" (Commission Regulation (EU) 2023/2831). Must be declared before disbursement.
- Fixed PES rates apply for budget calculation; RCN publishes these annually.
- Delivered when the EU project proposal is submitted by the stated deadline. If the proposal is not submitted, the PES grant is lost.

## 2. Eligibility check for Sunlit Sea

- Sunlit Sea is a Norwegian AS — **eligible** as PES applicant.
- Sunlit Sea will participate in an EU project proposal (EIC Transition) — **eligible activity**.
- Sunlit Sea has previously participated in EU project proposals (SuRE, Surewave) — answer "Yes" to question 2 in the template's Background section.
- Sunlit Sea must not exceed the EUR 300 000 de minimis ceiling across the current + two preceding fiscal years — see section 6 below for the check.
