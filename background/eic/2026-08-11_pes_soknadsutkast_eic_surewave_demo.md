### Primary and secondary objectives (max 1000 characters)

> Draft (~750 chars):

```
Primary objective: Deliver, by the 2026-09-16 deadline, a competitive EIC Transition proposal for a SUREWAVE-based offshore floating photovoltaic (FPV) pilot demonstrator at approximately 100-300 kWp, to be deployed at Aguçadoura (Portugal). The proposal will be submitted to the Horizon Europe EIC Transition call HORIZON-EIC-2026-TRANSITIONOPEN.

Secondary objectives:
- Consolidate the five-partner consortium (SINTEF, Sunlit Sea, CLEMENT, EDP, WavEC) around a shared work-package structure that reflects each partner's core expertise.
- Strengthen Sunlit Sea's position in the Norwegian floating PV cluster by contributing offshore-scale capability to the SUREWAVE technology track.
- Prepare a technology roadmap that leaves the pilot demonstrator "certification-ready" against DNV-RP-0584, DNV-ST-C108 and DNV-ST-E309 without requiring DNV as a consortium partner.
```

### Project summary (max 2000 characters)

> Draft (~1300 chars):

```
This PES application funds Sunlit Sea's work to prepare an EIC Transition proposal building directly on the SUREWAVE project (Horizon Europe, 2022-2026). SUREWAVE developed an integrated offshore concept combining a Sunlit Sea floating photovoltaic (FPV) unit with a floating breakwater from CLEMENT. The EIC Transition project will take that concept from TRL 4-5 to TRL 6 through a pilot-scale demonstrator (approximately 100-300 kWp) deployed at Aguçadoura (Portugal).

The consortium is complete and confirmed at five partners:
- SINTEF (Norway) — coordinator, numerical modelling and data;
- Sunlit Sea (Norway) — FPV unit design, manufacturing and factory acceptance;
- CLEMENT (Germany) — floating breakwater design and integration;
- EDP (Portugal) — grid interface, techno-economic analysis and business model;
- WavEC (Portugal) — site, permitting, offshore operations.

Sunlit Sea's PES effort covers our share of proposal drafting: work-package structure, FPV design and manufacturing sections, budget input, participant-portal setup, review coordination with the consortium, and preparation of DNV-alignment documentation as a proposal deliverable. Estimated effort: approximately 2-4 person-weeks. The PES period ends on the EU application deadline.
```

### Outcomes and impacts (max 1000 characters)

> Draft (~700 chars):

```
Outcome: a complete, submitted EIC Transition proposal that reflects Sunlit Sea's core technology contribution (integrated FPV unit) inside a broader offshore demonstrator concept. If awarded, the resulting project will validate our gen 2 floating photovoltaic platform under real offshore conditions and generate performance data that feeds directly into the Norwegian commercial pipeline (Skien Havn, Storavatnet, Gunneklevfjorden).

Impact of PES for Sunlit Sea specifically: PES makes the proposal-writing effort financially sustainable for a small company with limited administrative capacity. Without PES, our share of the writing effort would compete for hours against revenue-generating work on gen 2. With PES, we can commit dedicated time to a proposal we assess as strategically important.
```
