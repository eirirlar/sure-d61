# JA Solar JAM66D45 LB — n-type Double Glass Bifacial Modules

Product datasheet, version Global-EN-20240326A.

Series covered: JAM66D45 595/LB, 600/LB, 605/LB, 610/LB, 615/LB, 620/LB
(rated power 595 W to 620 W). Highlight variant: 620 W.

## Cell / module highlights

- n-Bycium+ cells, 16BB MBB half-cell technology
- Cell conversion efficiency up to 26 %
- n-type with very low LID
- Better temperature coefficient
- Better low-irradiance response
- Higher power generation, better LCOE
- Deep Blue 4.0 Pro platform

## Warranty

- 12-year product warranty
- 30-year linear power output warranty
- 1 % first-year degradation
- 0.4 % annual degradation over 30 years
- End-of-warranty output vs. standard module linear performance warranty:
  87.4 % (this product) vs. 83.1 % (standard), a +6.3 percentage-point
  advantage at year 30 (n-type bifacial double glass linear performance
  warranty curve).
