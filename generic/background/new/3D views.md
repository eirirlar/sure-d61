# 3D views — floating PV installation on basins

Three SketchUp-style 3D renderings of a small floating-PV installation over
rectangular concrete basins (appearance suggests water-treatment / effluent
basins). Labels are in French. Palm trees in the background.

## Page 1 — overview of the two-array PV field

Two rectangular PV arrays side by side, mounted low over separate basin
compartments. Each array is annotated:

- 8 Pnx (8 panneaux — 8 panels per array)
- Incl: 12°
- Orientation: 50.4° Sud Est

A separate orange building sits to the right of the arrays, connected by a
run of pipe/conduit along the basin wall. Callouts on the building side
indicate:

- A onduler (to be inverted / inverter side)
- Report de terre (earth conductor return)

Behind the PV field is another rectangular basin containing green liquid
(two long compartments visible), and the whole assembly sits on a dark
water/pond ground plane.

## Page 2 — close-up of the two arrays from the opposite corner

Same two 8-panel arrays viewed from the front / basin-water side. The tilt
(12°) is visible; the panels are supported on light metal frames sitting on
the basin walls. Labels on the arrays repeat "8 Pnx / Incl: 12° /
Orientation: 50.4° Sud Est".

The orange equipment cabin visible in the background has three callouts:

- Onduleur (inverter)
- Variateur (variable-frequency drive / controller)
- Batterie (battery)

Two dark battery-shaped objects and a lighter cylinder (likely inverter
/ VFD) are visible inside the open cabin.

## Page 3 — single-array view over a larger basin

A single, longer array is shown spanning across a wider basin. The array
sits on a steel frame with support legs standing on the basin floor
(dry-appearing basin) and running the length of the compartment.

Callouts on the equipment cabin (left side) and along the array:

- Onduleur
- Batterie
- Pompe (pump, mounted at the basin edge)

A second small white cabinet is visible mid-basin near a pump callout on
the right side of the array. In the far background: trees and open ground.

## Notes

The three views appear to belong to the same site layout, showing (1) the
two-array configuration on the smaller twin basins, and (3) a longer
single-array configuration on a larger basin. All arrays share the same
tilt (12°) and azimuth (50.4° SE) parameters. The system architecture
implied by the callouts is: PV → onduleur → variateur → batterie, with a
pump on the DC/AC side (unclear from the drawings).
