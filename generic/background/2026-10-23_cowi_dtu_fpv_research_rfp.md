## 1 Invitation and Purpose

DTU and COWI invite you to submit a detailed proposal for the engineering, supply, installation, testing and commissioning of an approximately 30–40 kWp floating solar photovoltaic (FPV) installation in Denmark. The intended contracting model is a complete turnkey delivery, with the supplier responsible for all works, equipment and coordination required to provide a safe, compliant and fully operational plant, except for items expressly identified as supplied by DTU/COWI or excluded in the proposal.

The offer should be sufficiently transparent to allow technical and commercial comparison between the proposed solutions. In particular, please provide separate prices for the principal work packages and equipment categories identified in Section 4.4 Financial proposal and price breakdown.

An optional quotation may be submitted for an additional floating system, provided that it presents sufficiently different technical characteristics and specifications.

## 2 Project Overview

This innovative research project seeks to establish and operate an experimental floating solar PV test facility at the Photonic Lab at DTU in Risø, Roskilde.

The project will investigate, analyse, and discuss the technical challenges associated with offshore floating PV system design and evaluate the market and performance potential of floating solar energy in offshore environments in Denmark and elsewhere. Furthermore, the project will evaluate the impact of floating photovoltaic systems on the flora and fauna in the surrounding area.

A series of configurations will be tested to investigate its individual profitability, reduce the risks and uncertainties regarding floating solar projects for public and private investors, and generate reliable data to assist in developing standards, regulations, and certifications for the floating solar market.

After the project period has ended, the results, certain data and reports will be publicly available.

This pilot project consisting of different floating PV systems will be setup, tested and compared based on a series of parameters including performance, LCOE, O&M, marine life impact, and more.

### 2.1 The research facility

The different floating solar systems will be selected and installed west of the Risø campus in Roskilde Fjord, which is located close to DTU Electro's Photovoltaic research facility, where there is a small harbour place as shown in the following figures.

Permission for these areas has been seeded. The water is shallow <4 m in the harbour area but rises to >5 m in the light blue area where a floating bridge will be extended, and the testbed placed.

## 3 Tender Basis

### 3.1 Scope of Supply and Services

The suppliers will deliver the floating systems, mooring and anchoring, PV modules, complete DC installations and inverters mounted on the pontoons/floating bridge, as illustrated in Figure 3, including earthing and bonding.

AC cabling, AC switchgear, any transformer and the associated shore-side electrical works, as well as additional research sensors are handled separately on the DTU/COWI side.

The proposed scope is to include the following (but not limited to) activities unless expressly excluded and priced as an option.

#### 3.1.1 Engineering and Design

- Project-specific design basis and design criteria, including applicable loads, environmental conditions, water-level range and operating conditions.
- PV layout and electrical design, string configuration, inverter sizing, cable sizing, voltage-drop calculations and electrical single-line diagrams.
- Floating platform layout and structural verification, including module-to-float interfaces, walkways/access provisions and freeboard as applicable.
- Mooring and anchoring design appropriate to the specific water body, including design loads, anchor type, line configuration, bank/bottom interfaces and allowance for water-level variation.
- Protection, earthing/bonding, surge/lightning protection, metering interfaces and connection up to the agreed limit of the scope of work.
- Cable routing and management for floating sections, including dynamic/flexible cable provisions where required.
- Monitoring, plant controller / communications and SCADA interface design, where applicable.
- Safe isolation, electrical safety and O&M practices.
- Design calculations, drawings, equipment schedules, datasheets, and method statements.

#### 3.1.2 Equipment Supply

- PV modules, including product/performance warranties.
- Floating system, pontoons/floats, structural elements, walkways, connection hardware and module mounting accessories.
- Mooring and anchoring system, including anchors, ropes/chains/lines, shackles, connectors, tensioning components and shore interfaces.
- Inverters and associated mounting/support arrangements.
- DC cables, connectors, string/combiner equipment where required, labels and cable management components and all auxiliary electrical DC equipment within the supplier scope.
- Monitoring, communications and SCADA equipment included in the proposed solution, if any.
- All consumables, fastening systems, fixings, glands, conduits, trays and ancillary materials needed for a complete installation.
- Spare parts and special tools proposed for initial operation and maintenance.

#### 3.1.3 Installation and Construction

- Mobilisation, site establishment, temporary works, and demobilisation.
- Assembly and installation of the floating array and PV modules.
- Installation and proof/verification of anchors and mooring system in accordance with the accepted design.
- Installation of DC, earthing/bonding, communications and shore-side interfaces.
- All civil works within the tendered scope.
- Quality control, inspection and construction records.
- Housekeeping and reinstatement of areas affected by the Works.

#### 3.1.4 Testing, Commissioning and Handover

- Pre-commissioning inspections and electrical tests.
- Functional testing of inverters, protection, monitoring, communications and safety systems.
- Commissioning of the complete FPV plant and demonstration of correct operation at the agreed point of connection.
- PV string/array testing and test records appropriate to the supplied system.
- Mooring/anchoring completion records and final inspection.
- Punch-list.
- O&M manuals if any, warranties, certificates, test reports and commissioning records.

### 3.2 Floating PV Specific Design Requirements

- The floating solar system shall be designed and constructed to a high standard.
- The proposed floating technology should be a proven commercial solution suitable for the project water body and project climatic and environmental conditions (wind, wave, current, temperature, etc).
- The design life of the floats, structural components, mooring lines and anchoring system should be stated together with any associated warranties.
- Materials exposed to water, humidity or splash shall be suitable for the expected environment and selected to control corrosion, degradation and galvanic interaction.
- Safe access to the array for inspection, maintenance and emergency response should be described.
- Any restrictions or considerations related to the Natura 2000 location should be identified relevant to the technology/materials.

### 3.3 Codes, Standards and Regulatory Compliance

The works shall comply with all applicable Danish legislation and Standards, authority requirements, grid-connection requirements and the latest applicable editions of relevant European and international standards.
