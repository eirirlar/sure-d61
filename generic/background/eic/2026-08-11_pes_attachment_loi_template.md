# Letter of Intent

**Project:** SUREWAVE-based offshore floating photovoltaic demonstrator at Aguçadoura, Portugal.

**Call:** HORIZON-EIC-2026-TRANSITIONOPEN, EIC Transition Open (2026), deadline 16 September 2026.

**Date:** 2026-08-11

## Purpose

The undersigned organisations declare their intent to participate as a consortium in the preparation and, if awarded, execution of an EIC Transition proposal for a SUREWAVE-based offshore floating photovoltaic (FPV) demonstrator. The project builds directly on the outcomes of the Horizon Europe SUREWAVE project (grant 101083342, 2022-2026) and aims to advance the integrated concept — combining a floating photovoltaic unit with a floating breakwater — from TRL 4-5 to TRL 6 through a pilot-scale demonstrator of approximately 100-300 kWp deployed at Aguçadoura, Portugal.

## Consortium and roles

The consortium is complete and consists of five partners, each contributing complementary expertise:

- **SINTEF** (Norway) serves as project coordinator with overall responsibility for project management, financial reporting to the European Innovation Council, risk and quality management, and coordination of the technical work packages. SINTEF leads Work Package 3 (Design and Performance Assessment), including numerical modelling (Task 3.1) and structural validation (Task 7.1), and contributes numerical modelling and standards expertise across the project, building on its lead role in the parent SUREWAVE project.

- **Sunlit Sea AS** (Norway) is responsible for the floating photovoltaic side of the demonstrator. Sunlit Sea leads Task 2.1 (Requirement Definition), Task 2.3 (FPV Platform Design), Task 4.1 (Monitoring Requirements), Task 5.2 (Prototype Manufacturing — PV module), Task 5.5 (Acceptance and Factory Testing), Task 7.2 (Reliability Assessment) and Task 8.4 (Booster Grant Activities), and contributes to system integration, model development and business model tasks.

- **CLEMENT Systems** (Germany) contributes the floating breakwater technology developed and refined in SUREWAVE. CLEMENT leads Task 2.2 (Floating Breakwater Optimization), Work Package 5 (Demonstrator Construction and Integration) including Task 5.1 (Prototype Manufacturing — breakwater and mooring components), and Task 6.3 (Offshore Installation and Commissioning), and co-authors the integrated FPV+breakwater interface design in Task 2.4.

- **EDP** (Portugal) contributes utility-side expertise, grid interface competence, and Portuguese market and regulatory knowledge. EDP leads Task 8.1 (Techno-Economic Analysis) and contributes to Task 3.2 (Energy Yield Assessment), Task 3.3 (Model Development), Task 4.1 (Monitoring Requirements), Task 7.3 (Energy Performance Assessment), Task 7.4 (Model Validation) and Task 8.2 (Business Model Development). EDP also leads engagement with Portuguese authorities and infrastructure providers required for the pilot's connection and operation.

- **WavEC** (Portugal) contributes offshore engineering and operations expertise. WavEC leads Work Package 4 (Instrumentation and Monitoring System Development) including Task 4.2 (Sensor System Integration) and Task 4.3 (Data Acquisition Platform), and Work Package 6 (Demonstration Site Preparation, Permitting and Deployment) including Task 6.1 (Site Selection and Metocean Feasibility) and Task 6.2 (Regulatory Compliance and Environmental Assessment). WavEC also supports Task 5.1 (mooring components) and Task 7.2 (Reliability Assessment).

## Commitments

By signing this Letter of Intent, each undersigned organisation confirms that it:

- Is aware of the proposed project scope, work-package structure and consortium composition as described above.
- Will actively contribute to the preparation of the EIC Transition proposal for submission to call HORIZON-EIC-2026-TRANSITIONOPEN by the 16 September 2026 deadline.
- Will, if the project is awarded, sign the Consortium Agreement and the Grant Agreement, and deliver the tasks, resources and expertise attributed to it in the awarded proposal.
- Has no known conflicts of interest that would prevent participation.

This Letter of Intent is provided in support of Sunlit Sea AS's Project Establishment Support (PES) application ES765081 to the Research Council of Norway, and to accompany the consortium's EIC Transition proposal.
