## 1. Project-management and cross-regulatory problems and risks

**1.1 Lump-sum milestone exposure and Gotland slip risk.**
EIC Transition grants are paid against milestone achievement verified by CINEA, not against actual costs incurred. If the Gotland installation slips beyond the agreed window — due to permit delays, marine survey iterations or grid connection procurement — the milestone trigger may not fire, and Sunlit Sea loses that tranche. Because the lump-sum model prevents recovery of incurred costs once a period closes without milestone confirmation, the exposure is asymmetric: spend continues (site prep, sub-contracted surveys, Olle's time) while revenue waits. The consortium agreement must specify a risk-sharing mechanism between Sunlit Sea and its WP partners so that a Gotland slip does not cascade into a dispute with SINTEF over deliverable dependencies.

**1.2 Twenty-four-month sequencing is fully back-loaded by permit lead times.**
The Swedish Vattenverksamhet permit (Miljöbalken 11 kap.) for marine structures takes 9–18 months from complete application submission. MKB and samråd preparation upstream of that application typically consumes 6–12 months. Marine archaeological survey required before application by Riksantikvarieämbetet typically adds 2–4 months plus possible iteration if a finding triggers further investigation. Stacking these serially against a H2 2027 deployment-preparation start and a 2029 operative installation window leaves essentially zero float. Starting permit preparation pre-grant (at risk) is not optional — it is a structural requirement of the schedule.

**1.3 Parallel-site decoupling risk: Hainer See moves faster.**
Hainer See (Germany) sits in an operating mining lake with an existing landowner consortium and established grid connection infrastructure. German Wasserhaushaltsgesetz procedures for converted mining lakes are well-charted. Hainer See will almost certainly reach installation-ready status before Gotland North. CINEA milestone structure will reflect both sites; if CINEA frames Gotland as a precondition for the "full demonstrator" milestone, Hainer See progress does not substitute. The project office must negotiate milestone definitions that allow partial payment upon Hainer See completion and protect against total non-payment if Gotland is delayed but technically sound.

**1.4 Trondheim–Gotland remote management and reporting cadence.**
The WP leader for Gotland sits in Trondheim. Olle operates on Gotland without direct day-to-day oversight from anyone else in the consortium. The risk is divergence: Olle advances an interpretation of the scope or makes a judgment call on a regulatory contact that the WP leader would have handled differently, and neither party realises until a monthly report surfaces it three weeks later. A bi-weekly written status note (not just a call) with explicit flag items — new contacts initiated, authority responses received, any change to planned activity — is the minimum discipline to keep remote management functional.

**1.5 The Vattenverksamhet application is the long pole; it cannot start without completed MKB.**
Länsstyrelsen Gotland is the prövningsmyndighet for Vattenverksamhet at this scale. MKB (miljökonsekvensbeskrivning) requires samråd with a defined set of remissinstanser — including Länsstyrelsen itself, Transportstyrelsen Sjöfartsavdelningen, Sjöfartsverket, Kustbevakningen, Lantmäteriet, Naturvårdsverket (potentially, given Natura 2000 proximity), Riksantikvarieämbetet and adjacent municipalities. Each samrådsrunda takes 4–8 weeks; a second round after response is common. The MKB cannot be finalised until all samrådsvar are processed. The application cannot be submitted until MKB is complete. This is the sequence-critical path and must be resourced immediately.

**1.6 Navigation marking and VTS coordination via Transportstyrelsen.**
An FPV installation at 57.84°N in the Baltic approaches constitutes a sjöfartsanordning that requires navigation-marking assessment under SjöLAG (1983:293) and Transportstyrelsens föreskrifter. Transportstyrelsen Sjöfartsavdelningen must be contacted early — both as a samrådspart in the MKB process and separately to agree on the marking scheme (light buoys, AIS transponder requirement, NAVTEX notice if warranted). Failing to get this agreed before Vattenverksamhet application submission creates a risk of conditional approval with marking requirements that have not been costed.

**1.7 Sjöfartsverket sjösäkerhetsanordningar and construction-phase vessel management.**
Sjöfartsverket is responsible for sjösäkerhetsanordningar in Swedish waters and will need to be informed of the construction-phase vessel operations — anchor handling, crane barges, supply vessels. This is distinct from the Transportstyrelsen marking question; it relates to operational safety during the installation campaign. A project-level consultation with Sjöfartsverket's operational arm should occur no later than T+18 to allow lead time before the installation window.

**1.8 Marine archaeological survey and Riksantikvarieämbetet iteration risk.**
Northern Gotland is archaeologically sensitive — Viking-age and medieval maritime activity is well documented in the area. Riksantikvarieämbetet (RAÄ) via the regional antikvarisk konsult will review the MKB and may require a dedicated underwater archaeological survey before Länsstyrelsen can issue the Vattenverksamhet permit. If the survey turns up a finding, RAÄ has the authority to require a full arkeologisk undersökning before construction. This adds an unpredictable 3–12-month delay. The survey must be commissioned early to allow time for the iteration cycle before permit submission.

**1.9 Grid connection (nätanslutning) is a parallel critical path, not a downstream task.**
The grid operator for northern Gotland is Vattenfall Eldistribution (formerly Gotlands Energi). Anmälan om nätanslutning at 1 MWp will require a detailed technical investigation by the DSO; queue times plus technical design time mean 6–18 months from formal application to grid agreement. This process must start in parallel with the MKB and permit process — not after the Vattenverksamhet permit is issued. A nätanslutningsavtal must be in place before final investment commitment; it is also a milestone evidence document for CINEA.

**1.10 Natura 2000 and species-specific environmental constraints.**
Gotland's coastal waters include Natura 2000 areas; the site at 57.84°N must be checked against the Natura 2000 register and the relevant bevarandeplan. If the site or its electromagnetic/shading footprint intersects a designated area, an Article 6(3) Habitats Directive appropriate assessment is required, which substantially extends the MKB and can result in conditional or negative decision from Länsstyrelsen. This must be assessed before the samråd process is designed.

**1.11 Seasonal forcing functions constrain installation window.**
The Baltic at 57.84°N is typically navigable year-round but winter ice conditions (typically December–March, variable) restrict marine construction operations. Spring bird-nesting surveys (April–June) constrain offshore survey vessel operations and would delay marine archaeological fieldwork if scheduled in that window. The practical installation campaign window is May–September. Against a 2029 target, this means H2 2028 must be used for final pre-installation checks and mobilisation — and that means all permits must be in hand by Q1 2028 at latest.

**1.12 Kustbevakningen operational awareness and emergency response.**
Kustbevakningen (Swedish Coast Guard) is responsible for operational maritime safety in Swedish waters. They should be consulted at project level before construction commences — both for awareness of the installation structure as a new navigational obstacle in their patrol area and for coordination of the emergency response plan during construction. This consultation is typically not legally required as a pre-condition for permits, but absence of it creates operational risk during installation.

**1.13 SMHI meteorological and oceanographic data procurement.**
The structural and mooring design of the FPV installation (concrete breakwater, catenary mooring) requires site-specific wind, wave and current data as design inputs. SMHI holds historical observational records and can provide hindcast data products for northern Gotland. Procuring SMHI data is a pre-requisite for the MARIN hydrodynamic modelling that feeds into the structural design. Lead time for a formal SMHI data delivery contract is typically 4–8 weeks. This should be procured at project start (or pre-grant, via a low-cost data purchase).

**1.14 Procurement rules under HE lump-sum for Olle's sub-contracted work.**
Under Horizon Europe grant rules, a beneficiary's sub-contractors must be selected via competitive procurement when the value exceeds EU threshold rules (approximately €215 000 for services under Swedish national procurement law as a default, but HE rules apply the beneficiary's own procurement procedures). Sunlit Sea AS is a Norwegian SME; its procurement procedures must be documented and applied consistently. Olle's contract must be structured as either a sub-contractor (separate legal entity; proportionality principles apply) or via an employment/secondment arrangement. The consortium agreement should specify which model is used and the flow-down IP and audit-access clauses. Getting this wrong is an audit risk at final reporting.

**1.15 IP flow-down for Gotland site-specific deliverables.**
The SUREWAVE consortium agreement governs IP generated during the project. Gotland site-specific deliverables — site surveys, permit applications, grid connection studies — are primarily background and sideground, not foreground IP. However, the permit files and survey reports will contain third-party data (RAÄ, Lantmäteriet, DSO) with usage restrictions. The consortium agreement and sub-contractor terms for Olle must clarify ownership and access rights to these documents for the purpose of EIC Transition grant reporting and CINEA audit access.

**1.16 CINEA audit trail and document retention.**
CINEA audits under EIC Transition lump-sum grants focus on evidence of milestone achievement, not on cost documentation — but they do require proof that activities were performed as described (survey reports, permit submissions, nätanslutningsavtal, meeting minutes with authorities). All correspondence with Swedish authorities must be archived in a project document management system accessible to Sunlit Sea's finance function and to the SINTEF consortium coordinator. Olle must understand from day one that originals of all authority correspondence (email, formal decision letters, samråd responses) must be forwarded to the central document management system within five working days of receipt.

## 3. Suggested sequencing

### Pre-grant (now — until T0, nominally H2 2027)

The permit critical path cannot afford to wait for grant confirmation. The following preparatory activities should run at the consortium's pre-grant risk:

- Procure SMHI hindcast data (low cost, no regulatory trigger). This feeds MARIN's initial modelling and can be used to validate the site selection for the proposal itself.
- Commission a Fornsök desktop search (RAÄ register) and a preliminary Natura 2000 overlap check (Naturvårdsverket NVDB). Both are desk studies, low cost, no formal process required.
- Establish an initial informal dialogue with Länsstyrelsen Gotland's environmental unit — a project introduction meeting (not a formal samråd). This is standard practice for large marine permit processes; it aligns Länsstyrelsen with the project timeline and avoids surprises in the formal process.
- Identify and pre-qualify the marine archaeological survey firm; do not commission until T0 funds are available but have the procurement process ready to execute.
- Map the grid connection route and identify the Vattenfall Eldistribution contact; make an informal enquiry about connection feasibility and queue status.
- Draft the Olle sub-contractor terms of reference and contract template for execution at T0.

## 5. Open questions and things to verify

**Q1. Nominal T0 date.**
The proposal targets a December 2026 submission. EIC Transition evaluation and grant agreement negotiation typically takes 6–12 months after submission. A realistic T0 is H1 2028 rather than H2 2027. If T0 slips to H1 2028, the operative installation window shifts from 2029 to 2030, which is still within the normal 36–48 month EIC Transition project duration. Verify with the EIC programme office what the expected timeline from submission to grant start is, and build the milestone plan accordingly.

**Q2. Exact installation window and campaign duration.**
Is 2029 the hard installation year, or is the grant structured to allow 2030? A summer 2030 installation window changes the entire sequencing: pre-grant work is less critical, and the permit critical path has more float. This must be resolved in the proposal's work plan before submission.

**Q3. MSA civilian consultation — procedure and lead time.**
The formal civilian consultation with Försvarsmaktens Marksamordningsområde for marine developments on Gotland is a standard procedural step, but the exact submission format, lead time and contact routing for an FPV installation at this scale and location needs to be confirmed with Länsstyrelsen or a Swedish marine-permit specialist. Specifically: does the MSA consultation happen as part of the samrådsprocess (i.e., Försvarsmakten is listed as a remissinstans alongside Transportstyrelsen) or is it a separate standalone submission before the MKB can be finalised? This distinction affects sequencing.

**Q4. Is Sunlit Sea's project office set up to run this cross-regulatory sweep?**
Sunlit Sea is a Norwegian SME with no prior Swedish permitting experience. The cross-regulatory sweep described above (Länsstyrelsen, Transportstyrelsen, Sjöfartsverket, RAÄ, Naturvårdsverket, Lantmäteriet, Kustbevakningen) is a medium-complexity Swedish marine permit process. The options are: (a) Sunlit Sea engages a Swedish environmental law firm to own the permit process and use Olle as local logistics and liaison; (b) SINTEF (with Swedish partners in the consortium) takes a larger coordination role for Swedish-language authority contacts; (c) Sunlit Sea hires or contracts a Swedish project engineer to support the permit process in Trondheim. Option (a) is the most robust but adds cost. This decision must be made before T0 since the samråd clock starts at T0.

**Q5. Olle's contractual status under the Grant Agreement.**
Is Olle contracted as an individual sub-contractor (enskild firma or AB), or is he contracted through an employer-of-record arrangement? HE lump-sum rules require that sub-contracting is competitive and auditable. If Olle's engagement is framed as a service contract to an individual (not a legal entity), Swedish tax and labour law implications (F-skattsedel requirement) must be addressed. Verify with Sunlit Sea's finance function and the consortium agreement before contract execution.

**Q6. SINTEF coordination of Gotland vs Hainer See milestone dependencies.**
Does the consortium agreement allow partial payment upon Hainer See completion if Gotland is delayed, or are the two sites treated as a joint milestone? This is the highest-value financial risk in the project and must be resolved in the milestone plan negotiation with CINEA at grant agreement stage.

**Q7. Natura 2000 overlap — confirmed or unconfirmed?**
The site at 57.84°N, 18.80°E near Storugns must be checked against the Naturvårdsverket NVDB Natura 2000 layer. Northern Gotland has designated areas (e.g., SE0330135 Gotlandskusten). If the site intersects a designated area or its buffer zone, the MKB must include an Article 6(3) appropriate assessment. This may or may not already be known from the SUREWAVE site selection process.

**Q8. Nordkalk's Storugns operation — stakeholder consultation requirement.**
Nordkalk operates the limestone port at Storugns. Vessel traffic to and from Storugns may intersect with the FPV site or its navigation marking zone. Nordkalk is a material stakeholder in the samrådsprocess and likely in the Transportstyrelsen navigation marking consultation. Early dialogue with Nordkalk's harbour master is advisable to avoid a formal objection during the permit process.
