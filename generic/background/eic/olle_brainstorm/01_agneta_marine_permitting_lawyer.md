## 1. Problems and risks from the permitting-law angle

**1.1 Vattenverksamhet — gating permit, long lead time**
Placing a floating structure anchored to the seabed with catenary moorings and possibly running a cable to shore constitutes vattenverksamhet under miljöbalken 11 kap. 3 § (anläggande i vattenområde, muddring, kabelledning). Tillstånd is required under 11 kap. 9 § and must be sought from Mark- och miljödomstolen (Nacka tingsrätt for Gotland) since the installation exceeds the thresholds for anmälan. Lead time from complete application to dom is typically 12-24 months. This is the single longest gating permit; everything else fits inside or alongside this window if started promptly. Do not underestimate MKB requirements (see 1.7).

**1.2 Strandskydd — 7 kap. 13-18 §§ MB — may require dispens or lagligförklaring**
The Gotland shoreline carries strandskydd 300 m from strandlinjen (7 kap. 14 § MB) extending seaward as well as landward. A floating installation at 57.84°N near Storugns is almost certainly within this zone. Dispens can be granted by Länsstyrelsen under 7 kap. 18 b § only if one of the grounds in 7 kap. 18 c § applies; the strongest ground here is likely att området är ianspråktaget på ett sådant sätt att dispens inte motverkar strandskyddets syften. Access to the industrial Nordkalk terminal next door may support this argument. This is not automatic. Länsstyrelsen Gotland has historically applied strandskydd rigorously even inside established industrial areas.

**1.3 Natura 2000 — screening and potentially appropriate assessment**
Northern Gotland falls within or adjacent to several Natura 2000-areas under Habitatdirektivet (92/43/EEG) and Fågeldirektivet (2009/147/EG). The Gotland coast has eight or more designated sites; the Storugns–Klinthagen area is near sensitive limestone reef habitats (reefcode EU: 1170) and coastal heath. Under miljöbalken 7 kap. 28 a § a prövning is required if the project can antas påverka ett Natura 2000-område. This triggers a full appropriate assessment (artikel 6.3 habitatdirektivet). If Länsstyrelsen or MMD concludes significant negative effect cannot be ruled out, a governmental dispensation from Naturvårdsverket is required under 7 kap. 29 § MB — this alone could add 12-24 months. Verify exact Natura 2000 boundaries against the official NVDB layer before starting.

**1.4 Fornminnen and maritime archaeology — seabed**
Gotland has exceptional density of both land and maritime fornminnen (ancient monuments). Havsbottnen in northern Gotland has documented prehistoric coastal sites submerged by isostatic rebound, and limestone reefs can contain medieval wreck deposits. Under kulturmiljölagen (1988:950) 2 kap. 1 § all seabed fornminnen are automatically protected; touching them is förbjudet without tillstånd from Länsstyrelsen. Mooring anchor placements require archaeological survey (bottenkartering). Riksantikvarieämbetet must be consulted. A bottenkartering typically takes 2-4 months fieldwork plus analysis and adds cost; scheduling it early avoids blocking the anchor-design phase.

**1.5 Sjöfartsskydd and farledsrätt — vessel traffic**
Storugns port has regular bulk-carrier traffic (limestone to Oxelösund, Raahe, Luleå). The proposed footprint at 57.84°N, 18.797°E must be checked against Sjöfartsverket's chart for farleder, ankarplatser and sjötrafikzoner. If the footprint intersects a farled or hinders navigationssäkerhet, Sjöfartsverket must approve an adjusted layout or a sjöfartsavstängning. Under Lagen om Sveriges sjöterritorium och maritima zoner (2017:1272) and Sjötrafiklagen the installation must not constitute hinder. Sjöfartsverket also issues nautisk warning (NOTAM/EfS) for construction phases. This is a parallel permit but Sjöfartsverket review must be initiated early to avoid last-minute layout changes.

**1.6 Tillstånd for kabelledning — seabed cable**
The cable from the FPV field to the Nordkalk grid connection constitutes en kabelledning i vattenområde, which is itself vattenverksamhet under 11 kap. 3 § MB. If Nordkalk's grid connection is within existing industrial infrastructure (likely), the cable length is short. However the cable crossing any part of privately or publicly held seabed requires a separate consideration or can be bundled into the 11 kap. vattenverksamhet-ansökan. Verify whether any cable route crosses seabed owned by the state (allmänt vatten) or Nordkalk's fastighet — this affects the avtalsside (see 1.9) and the permits.

**1.7 MKB — obligatorisk eller frivillig**
En specifik miljöbedömning (MKB) under miljöbalken 6 kap. 20 § is mandatory if the installation is of a category listed in 6 kap. 23 § or the annexes (from MKB-direktivet 2011/92/EU, Annex II). A 1 MWp floating solar installation with mooring and cable is likely Annex II (installations for harnessing solar energy for maritime use). A behovsbedömning by Länsstyrelsen under 6 kap. 22 § is required: the authority decides if full MKB is mandatory. Given Natura 2000 proximity and sensitive sea floor, Länsstyrelsen will almost certainly require full MKB. Budget 6-12 months for MKB preparation, samråd and compilation. MKB adds significant cost: budget 600k-1,2 MSEK for consultants.

**1.8 Grundvatten och dricksvattenskydd — Gotland-specific**
Gotland has unique freshwater vulnerability. The island depends entirely on groundwater and Gotland has delineated numerous vattenskyddsområden under 7 kap. 21 § MB protecting karst limestone aquifers (kalkstensvatten). The Storugns area sits above the northern Gotland karst. Any land-based foundation work, cable trenching or change to coastal hydrology requires consultation with Länsstyrelsen's VA-enhet and possibly a påverkansanalys on the skyddszon. This is not a gating permit but failure to address it in the MKB will cause Länsstyrelsen to remit the application for supplementation.

**1.9 Fastighetsrätt — vattenområdet tillhörighet**
Under Fastighetsbildningslagen and JB may the seabed be privat fastighet (Nordkalk's property through strandlinjen) or statens vatten (allemansrätten applies differently). In Sweden, by default the seabed to 300 m from the shoreline or to the 5-m depth contour (depending on cadastral circumstances) may belong to the adjacent fastighet. If Nordkalk owns the vattenområde, a nyttjanderättsavtal or arrende is required before application; if it is statens vatten, Lantmäteriet may need to confirm. Confusion here is a common gotcha: applicants assume statens vatten, but Gotland historical delimitation means much coastal seabed belongs to adjacent fastigheter. An early Lantmäteriet utredning (fastighetsutredning) is therefore essential.

**1.10 Ellag och elnätsanslutning — nätkoncessionsplikt**
The cable from FPV to shore and the grid connection point are subject to Ellagen (1997:857) and kräver nätkoncession from Energimarknadsinspektionen (Ei) if the cable constitutes en ledning för överföring av elektrisk kraft. For a short industrial connection to Nordkalk's internal grid, the question is whether Nordkalk is willing to take the power directly (bilateral avtal) or whether connection to Svenska kraftnäts or Vattenfall Eldistribution's nät is required. If the latter, Ei-tillstånd is needed — typically 3-6 months for short low-voltage cables, but longer if kabelledningen is new high-voltage. Clarify the grid connection model in 2027 planning.

**1.11 Planförhållanden — kommunal plan**
Region Gotland's översiktsplan 2025 designates northern Gotland; the Storugns area has industrial mark designation for Nordkalk's operations, and the sea area may lack specific maritime spatial planning designation at this scale. Under Plan- och bygglagen (PBL) a FPV installation offshore does not normally require bygglov if it is fully marine, but a strandnära land installation (transformer station, grid connection building) may trigger lovplikt. Olle must check whether Region Gotland's översiktsplan contains any utpekat riksintresse for naturvård, kulturmiljö or friluftsliv that would require coordination. Riksintressen for naturvård and friluftsliv in 3 kap. MB are likely present and will be weighed in the vattenverksamhet-prövning.

**1.12 Iskraft och vinterförhållanden — designkrav i MKB**
Northern Gotland has an ice probability of roughly 30-50 % in harsh winters (based on SMHI Baltic ice records). The MKB must address the risk of ice loads on the floating structure and mooring — not a legal risk per se, but Länsstyrelsen and MMD will require it as a teknisk underlag before approving. If ice risk assessment is missing, the court will remit the application. Commission an SMHI-based ice load study early; it takes 2-3 months.

**1.13 SO₃ — Helcom marine protection zones**
The Baltic is an MARPOL Annex VI designated Emission Control Area. This affects construction vessels and supply ships but not the installed FPV. Note that Sweden reports to Helcom under the Helsinki Convention; any significant project in Swedish coastal waters typically requires an informal Helcom check for consistency with Helcom's Baltic Sea Action Plan objectives — including nutrient reduction and biodiversity goals. This is not a formal permit but Länsstyrelsen will want to see the BSAP compatibility discussed in the MKB.

**1.14 Arrendeavtal med Nordkalk — risk för motstående intressen**
Nordkalk's Klinthagen operation is a major industrial actor with its own expansion plans and potential conflicting interests in the sea area (vessel traffic, dredging, potential future quay extensions). An FPV installation 130 m in diameter near their port could interfere with their operational footprint. Nordkalk may have existing tillstånd for the water area that includes exclusive use rights. Due diligence on Nordkalk's existing vattenverksamhet-tillstånd and any arrendekontrakt for the seabed is a prerequisite for any application.

**1.15 Säkerhetsavstånd och ISPS**
Storugns port handles limestone for steel mills; if it has ISPS-status (International Ship and Port Facility Security) or is designated under Skyddslagen (2010:305), there may be säkerhetsavstånd that the FPV installation must respect. Olle should query Länsstyrelsen samhällsskydd and Sjöfartsverket on this — a civilian FPV project near an ISPS-classified port could require additional coordination with port management. This is not a dealbreaker but must be documented.

## 2. Tasks for Olle

### Workstream A — Samråd och kommunikation

**A1. Initiate formellt tidigt samråd med Länsstyrelsen Gotland**
Contact Länsstyrelsen Gotland, Enheten för vatten- och naturvård (also known internally as vattenenheten or naturprövningsenheten), and request an early consultation meeting under MB 6 kap. 29 §. Present project concept, coordinates, and a preliminary layout sketch. Objective: get Länsstyrelsen's view on permitting scope, likely Natura 2000 screening obligation, and MKB requirement before investing in full application. Output: written meeting notes, informal guidance letter. Time: 1-2 days preparation + 1-day meeting; 2-4 weeks for Länsstyrelsen response. Dependency: needs project concept presentation (PPT or fact sheet).

**A2. Hold möte med Region Gotland plan- och bygg**
Contact Region Gotland's Samhällsbyggnadsförvaltningen, plan- och byggavdelningen, to discuss whether the project triggers any PBL considerations (strandnära markåtgärder, transformer/grid buildings requiring bygglov). Request a pre-application discussion. Output: written position on lovplikt. Time: half-day meeting. Dependency: none; can run in parallel with A1.

**A3. Genomför informationsmöte med Nordkalk Storugns**
Nordkalk is both a potential host/partner and a potential objector in the vattenverksamhet process. Meet with Nordkalk Storugns site management to explain the project, discuss cable route through their area, and explore a markupplåtelse or arrendeavtal for the seabed (if within their fastighet). Time: 1 day including preparation. Output: Nordkalk's initial position (supportive/neutral/opposed). This must happen before vattenverksamhet-ansökan is filed; an objecting Nordkalk at the MKB samråd is a serious risk.

**A4. Informera fiskenäringen och lokala intressenter**
Under MB 6 kap. 29 § the samråd must cover those likely to be particularly affected. Contact Gotlands fiskareförbund and any local recreational fishing associations for northern Gotland; identify any yrkesfiskelicenser active in the water area via Havs- och vattenmyndigheten's (HaV's) register. Output: documented outreach and responses. Time: 2-3 days. Dependency: coordinates confirmed.

### Workstream B — Tillståndsansökan vattenverksamhet (MB 11 kap.)

**B1. Genomför fastighetsutredning — vattenområdets tillhörighet**
Commission Lantmäteriet (fastighetsavdelningen) or a certified lantmätare to determine whether the proposed footprint lies within Nordkalk's registered fastighet, a communal samfällighet, or statens vatten. This is non-negotiable before filing. Output: lantmäteriteknisk rapport med fastighetsbeteckning och gränser. Time: 2-4 weeks fieldwork + desk analysis. Dependency: final coordinates confirmed. Cost: ~50-100k SEK.

**B2. Beställ marin bottenkartering och arkeologisk utredning**
Engage a maritime archaeology consultant (e.g. Arkeologerna at Riksantikvarieämbetet, or Bohusläns museum marine archaeology unit) to perform a bottenkartering (sidescan sonar, sub-bottom profiler) covering the footprint plus a 200 m buffer. Coordinate with RAÄ kulturmiljöenhet (Riksantikvarieämbetet). Output: bottenkarteringsrapport + fornminnesutlåtande. Time: 2-4 months from commission to final report. Dependency: final coordinates and approximate anchor point layout.

**B3. Beställ marinbiologisk inventering**
Engage a marine biologist to survey benthic habitats, reef communities (potential 1170 reef habitats for Natura 2000), and fish spawning areas in the footprint. This feeds both the MKB and the Natura 2000-screening. Output: marinbiologisk inventering med artlista. Time: seasonal dependency — survey ideally spring-summer; allow 4-6 months total. Start this no later than early 2027.

**B4. Genomför MKB-samråd under MB 6 kap. 29-32 §§**
Hold formal samråd with Länsstyrelsen, Region Gotland, Naturvårdsverket, Riksantikvarieämbetet, Sjöfartsverket, HaV, and allmänheten. Produce a samrådsredogörelse. Output: documented samrådsunderlag and samrådsredogörelse. Time: 3-6 months including notice period and response compilation. Dependency: basic project description, preliminary layout, preliminary environmental baseline (B2, B3 in progress).

**B5. Skriv och lämna in vattenverksamhet-ansökan till MMD (Nacka tingsrätt)**
Compile full ansökan under MB 11 kap. 9 § including MKB, samrådsredogörelse, marinbiologisk inventering, bottenkartering, teknisk beskrivning, islaststudie. File with Mark- och miljödomstolen at Nacka tingsrätt. Output: complete filed application. Time: 3-4 months compilation after baseline studies are in hand. Dependency: B1-B4 complete, A3 complete (Nordkalk position known). Expected MMD lead time after filing: 9-18 months to dom.

### Workstream C — Strandskyddsdispens

**C1. Identifiera strandskyddsgränserna för platsen**
Request Länsstyrelsen Gotland's GIS layer for strandskyddsgränser to confirm whether the offshore footprint and any land-based infrastructure fall within 300 m-zonen (7 kap. 14 § MB). If land-based transformer station or cable landing point is within the zone, strandskyddsdispens is required. Output: map confirming zone status. Time: 1-2 weeks. Dependency: coordinates confirmed.

**C2. Ansök om strandskyddsdispens hos Länsstyrelsen Gotland**
If required: file a dispensansökan under 7 kap. 18 b § MB with Länsstyrelsen Gotland. Motivate under 7 kap. 18 c § (most likely ground: industrial area ianspråktaget, or särskilda skäl för förnybar energi). Output: dispensbeslut. Lead time: 3-9 months depending on complexity and whether överklagande follows. Länsstyrelsen's naturprövningsenhet handles this.

### Workstream D — Natura 2000-screening

**D1. Genomför Natura 2000-screening**
Using the marine biology survey (B3) and the official Natura 2000-areas GIS layer from Naturvårdsverket, produce a formal screening document (behovsbedömning) assessing whether the project can antas påverka ett Natura 2000-område under MB 7 kap. 28 a §. Engage an environmental consultant for this. Output: screening report with conclusion. Time: 1-2 months. Dependency: B3 (marinbiologisk inventering).

**D2. Om prövning krävs — genomför lämplighetsprövning (art. 6.3)**
If screening concludes prövning is required, commission an appropriate assessment under MB 7 kap. 28 b §. This must demonstrate no significant negative effect on protected habitats and species, or propose measures. Output: appropriate assessment annexed to MKB and vattenverksamhet-ansökan. Time: adds 3-6 months to MKB process. If negative conclusion, escalation to Naturvårdsverket and government is needed — plan for 24+ additional months in that scenario.

### Workstream E — Elnät och nätkoncessionsrätt

**E1. Klargör nätanslutningsmodell med Nordkalk och Svenska kraftnät**
Determine whether the FPV power is sold directly to Nordkalk under a PPA (avoiding nätkoncession) or fed into the public grid. Contact Vattenfall Eldistribution (regional DSO for Gotland) to obtain a preliminary nätanslutningsbesked. If nätkoncession is required, file application with Ei. Time: 2-3 months for nätbesked. Dependency: commercial agreement with Nordkalk or grid operator.

### Workstream F — Fastighetsrätt och nyttjanderättsavtal

**F1. Förhandla och teckna nyttjanderättsavtal med fastighetens ägare**
Once B1 (fastighetsutredning) identifies the seabed owner (Nordkalk or state), negotiate and sign a nyttjanderättsavtal or arrende for the water area including cable route. For state-owned seabed: contact Lantmäteriet for statlig nyttjanderätt. For Nordkalk-owned: direct förhandling. Output: signed agreement. Time: 2-6 months negotiations. Dependency: B1.

**F2. Säkra markrättigheter för kabellandning och teknisk utrustning på land**
If transformer station or grid connection infrastructure is on Nordkalk's land, a markavtal is required. Coordinate with Nordkalk Fastigheter. Output: signed markavtal. Time: 1-3 months. Dependency: cable route confirmed.

### Workstream G — Löpande myndighetskontakter och samordning

**G1. Etablera regelbundna check-in med Länsstyrelsen Gotland under processens gång**
Maintain quarterly contact with the handläggare at Länsstyrelsen vatten- och naturvård and kulturmiljöenheten to flag supplementation needs early and avoid surprises. Time: 1-2 days per quarter. No formal output; builds relationship.

**G2. Följ upp bottenkarteringsresultaten med RAÄ**
After B2 is complete, hold a follow-up meeting with Riksantikvarieämbetet kulturmiljöenhet (or Länsstyrelsen Gotland kulturmiljöenhet as delegated authority) to review findings and agree on any fornminnesåtgärder. Time: 1 day. Dependency: B2 complete.

**G3. Bevaka och svara på kungörelser och remisser under MMD-processen**
Once vattenverksamhet-ansökan is filed, MMD will kungöra the application and collect remissvar. Olle must monitor kungörelserna, coordinate responses to supplementation requests from MMD, and ensure Sunlit Sea's technical experts in Trondheim can respond within deadlines (typically 3-6 weeks per remissrunda). Time: ongoing, approximately 1-2 days per remissrunda, 5-8 rounds expected.

## 3. Suggested sequencing

**Must start before grant confirmation (2026-2027) — because lead times exceed the preparation window:**

- Workstream A (samråd with Länsstyrelsen, Region Gotland, Nordkalk) — the informal early consultation under A1-A3 is a prerequisite for the MKB-samråd and for scoping the application correctly. Starting this after grant award in late 2027 would compress the entire timeline dangerously. Early consultation does not commit the project financially and is proportionate to the risk of losing 12-18 months if the scope is wrong.
- Workstream B1 (fastighetsutredning) — a 4-week investigation that must precede everything; can start as soon as coordinates are confirmed.
- Workstream B2 (bottenkartering) — 4-6 month exercise; must start in 2027 at the latest, ideally Q1 2027 to have results ready for formal MKB-samråd. Seasonal fieldwork window makes this time-critical.
- Workstream B3 (marinbiologisk inventering) — strongly seasonal (spring-summer survey window). If the first available survey window is missed, the entire schedule slips one year.
- Workstream D1 (Natura 2000-screening) — can start on the basis of desk study before marine surveys are complete, to identify whether full appropriate assessment (D2) is in scope. Early screening scopes the MKB.

**Cannot start before grant confirmation:**

- Workstream B5 (formal vattenverksamhet-ansökan filing) — filing implies commitment to the application fee, external consultants for MKB compilation, and legal representation at MMD. This is the point of no return financially; hold until grant award or a firm investment decision.
- Workstream E (nätanslutning/nätkoncession) — commercial terms and power volume need to be confirmed.
- Workstream F (nyttjanderättsavtal signing) — can be negotiated in parallel with grant process but final signing typically follows investment confirmation.

**Critical path blockers:**

1. Bottenkartering (B2) blocks the vattenverksamhet-ansökan (B5) because MMD will reject an application without archaeological clearance.
2. Natura 2000-appropriate assessment (D2, if triggered) blocks the MKB finalisation and therefore the vattenverksamhet-ansökan (B5).
3. Fastighetsutredning (B1) blocks nyttjanderättsavtal (F1) and is needed for the ansökan.
4. MMD dom (output of B5) is the gating event for the entire installation; until it is legally binding (after eventuell överklagning), construction cannot begin.
5. Nordkalk's position (A3) is a soft blocker — an objecting Nordkalk in the MMD process can extend the process by 12-18 months through överklaganden.

## 5. Open questions and things to verify

**5.1 Vattenområdets tillhörighet — Nordkalk eller statens vatten**
It is assumed but not confirmed that the proposed footprint lies in water adjacent to Nordkalk's fastighet. In Sweden, the cadastral boundary of a coastal fastighet often extends to the 5-m depth contour or to a specified gräns, after which it is statens vatten. The northern Gotland shoreline was last cadastrally surveyed in the 19th and early 20th century; historical styckning may have defined boundaries differently. Lantmäteriet must confirm this before any application is filed.

**5.2 Natura 2000-gränser — platsen kan ligga direkt angränsande**
Assumed the site is near but possibly outside designated Natura 2000-areas. The northern Gotland coast has several Natura 2000-areas related to limestone reefs, alvar, and seabird habitats. Whether the 57.84°N, 18.797°E coordinates lie within a designated area's marine boundary must be verified against Naturvårdsverket's official NVDB layer and Länsstyrelsen Gotland's GIS. If inside a boundary, an appropriate assessment is mandatory — a significant schedule and cost consequence.

**5.3 Varaktigheten för vattenverksamhet-tillståndet — 1 MWp-skala**
It is assumed that a 1 MWp FPV installation on a 130-m footprint exceeds the anmälningsgränsen in vattenverksamhetsförordningen (2013:14) and requires full tillstånd from MMD (not just anmälan to Länsstyrelsen). This should be confirmed with Länsstyrelsen at the A1 early samråd. If it falls under anmälningsgränsen, the process is faster (3-6 months at Länsstyrelsen rather than 9-18 months at MMD) — a favorable outcome but not to be assumed.

**5.4 Om strandskyddet sträcker sig ut i det marina fotavtrycket**
Strandskydd under MB 7 kap. 13 § applies both landward and seaward from strandlinjen. The seaward extent is 300 m unless Länsstyrelsen has extended or reduced it. A 130-m installation footprint at the proposed coordinates is almost certainly within the 300-m zone. What is unclear is whether the offshore water-surface installation itself — as opposed to any land-based infrastructure — constitutes an åtgärd that requires dispens. Case law on this specific question for FPV is still developing; Länsstyrelsen Gotland's position should be obtained explicitly at the A1 samråd.

**5.5 MKB-gränsvärden för FPV vid 1 MWp**
There is no explicit MKB threshold in Swedish regulation specifically for floating solar. The obligation derives from the EIA Directive (2011/92/EU Annex II) as transposed in MB 6 kap. and förordning (1998:905) om miljökonsekvensbeskrivningar (Annex 2 lists energy installations). At 1 MWp, full MKB is likely but not certain — Länsstyrelsen's behovsbedömning under 6 kap. 22 § decides. The risk is that Länsstyrelsen imposes a full MKB when the project team has budgeted for a simplified review; this should be scoped explicitly at the A1 meeting.

**5.6 Islastregler och konstruktionskrav**
It is assumed that Swedish regulations and the relevant harmonised European standards will require documentation of ice load capacity for the mooring and structure. The specific standard applicable (ISO 19906 for arctic/sub-arctic marine structures or a Baltic-specific HaV guidance) should be confirmed with Länsstyrelsen as part of the A1 scoping. SMHI has Baltic ice climatology data; the question is whether a formal third-party structural verification is required by the prövningsmyndighet.

**5.7 Militärfastighetsavstånd och Försvarsmakten**
Northern Gotland has increased Försvarsmakten presence following NATO accession. It is assumed the project has no interaction with Försvarsmakten clearances since the site is civilian and not near declared military facilities, but this should be verified with Länsstyrelsen samhällsskyddsenhet. Note: Olle Roman's role is civilian; his employer affiliation is irrelevant to the permit process. However, if the project coordination role creates a perception of Försvarsmakten involvement (even informally), it could attract additional scrutiny from remissinstanser. This is a communication risk, not a legal one.

**5.8 Elproduktionslicens och tillståndsplikt under ellagen**
Whether the FPV installation at 1 MWp requires a separate elproduktionslicens under ellagen (1997:857) should be confirmed with Ei. For installations above a certain threshold (historically around 1 MW for grid-connected generation in Sweden), Ei registration may be required. This is a parallel and typically fast administrative step but should not be overlooked.
