grants.

## 2. EDP — end user and Impact partner

### Group scale

**Sources:** <https://edp.com/en/media/edp-stories/new-business-plan-12-questions> ·
<https://edp.com/sites/default/files/document/2026-07/1H26%20-%20Operating%20Data%20Preview%20-%20EDP.pdf> ·
<https://www.edp.com/en/media/news/edpr-scales-global-ambition-growth-plan-more-4gwyear>

- Total installed capacity **32.7 GW**; wind and solar **21.2 GW** as of Q1 2026
- Wind and solar generation **11.5 TWh** in Q1 2026, +4 % year on year
- Around **12,000 employees**; Europe, North America, South America, Asia Pacific
- Target **>50 GW renewables by 2030**; **100 % renewable production by 2030** (74 % today)
- **>€12 bn investment through 2028**, roughly **70 % on renewables and storage**
- Utility-scale solar **4.8 GW**; stated intention to grow solar presence tenfold

### Alqueva — reservoir floating solar at scale

**Sources:** <https://www.edp.com/en/europe/portugal/media/news/edps-pioneer-floating-solar-power-plant-alqueva-ready-start-producing> ·
<https://edp.com/en/media/edp-stories/alquevas-floating-solar-panels> ·
<https://www.offshore-energy.biz/edp-gets-approval-for-4mw-floating-solar-farm-in-portugal/>

- Largest floating solar platform on a dam reservoir in Europe (2022)
- ~**12,000 PV panels**, **4 hectares**, **5 MWp**, ~**7.5 GWh/year**
- Hybridised with the Alqueva hydroelectric plant, plus a **1 MW battery**
- EDP won a **70 MW lot at Alqueva** in the April 2022 Portuguese floating-solar auction — the
  largest capacity awarded in that auction

### Floating PV Lab, Alto Rabagão — the strongest single Impact argument

**Source:** <https://edp.com/en/innovation/innovation-projects/floating-pv-lab>

- Location: Alto Rabagão dam reservoir, Montalegre, Portugal
- **220 kW** floating platform, **2,500 m²**, ~**300 MWh/year**; office and meeting facilities on site
- Hybrid: floating PV + hydropower + storage
- Stated purpose: *"reduce technological risk, promote applied innovation, and support the
  industrialisation and commercialisation of competitive solutions"*
- **Open-access**: third parties apply to test and validate under real operating conditions
- Site conditions as described by EDP: **strong winds, significant wave action**, temperatures from
  below 0 °C to above 30 °C, plus ice and snow
- Current third-party testers: **Fred. Olsen 1848** (NO, adaptive mooring) and **Sperra** (US,
  3D-printed concrete gravity anchors)
- **Target: support commercialisation of at least five new floating solar technologies by end-2027**

**Why this reframed the proposal:** EDP's own problem sites are windy inland reservoirs, not
offshore. That makes Hainer See a *directly relevant* environment rather than a compromise, and it
gives the project a named, funded, operating route to the first commercial installation.

## 3. Market sizing

**Sources:** <https://www.gminsights.com/industry-analysis/floating-solar-pv-market> ·
<https://www.openpr.com/news/4604213/floating-solar-market-study-highlights-reservoir-based-pv> ·
<https://www.marknteladvisors.com/research-library/global-floating-solar-market.html>

- Floating solar market growth around **26 % CAGR to 2030**; market value forecasts vary widely by
  source (USD ~19 bn to ~145 bn by 2030) — **use the CAGR, not the absolute value, and cite one
  source explicitly**
- **1 % coverage of global reservoirs ≈ 404 GW** of generating potential — roughly twice total
  world solar additions in 2024
- **25 % coverage of existing reservoirs ≈ 4,400 GW** of incremental renewable capacity
- Hybridising floating solar onto hydropower reservoirs reuses existing transmission corridors and
  cuts capital cost; calm water simplifies mooring and reduces inspection frequency — i.e. the
  industry itself identifies wave conditions as the constraint

## 4. Sunlit Sea — public profile

**Source:** <https://www.sunlitsea.no/>

- Founded 2019; Fjordgata 30, 7010 Trondheim, Norway
- Floating solar for water bodies; water-cooled for efficiency; modular for fast installation
- Public references listed: RIXEN Cableway European tour (2022); a floating solar industry
  partnership (2023)
- Site states floating solar growth of ~29 % per year over five years
- Contacts listed publicly: Per Lindberg (CEO/co-founder), Eirik Larsen (CFO/co-founder)

**Note:** the public site carries no deployed-capacity or manufacturing figures. Any capacity or
reference claim in the proposal must come from Sunlit Sea's own records, not from the website.
