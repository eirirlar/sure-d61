# Project description — PES support for the EIC Transition SUREWAVE-based proposal

Sunlit Sea AS applies for Project Establishment Support (PES) to fund preparation of a Horizon Europe EIC Transition proposal building on the SUREWAVE project. The proposal will be submitted to call **HORIZON-EIC-2026-TRANSITIONOPEN** by the **2026-09-16 deadline**.

## 1. Background

### 1.1 Contact with the National Contact Point (NCP)

No. Sunlit Sea has not been in contact with the NCP about this specific EU project proposal. If contact is initiated before proposal submission, this will be reflected in the final application.

### 1.2 Previous EU project participation

Yes. Sunlit Sea AS has previously participated in Horizon Europe project proposals and is currently a partner in two active Horizon Europe projects:

- **SUREWAVE** (Horizon Europe grant 101083342, HORIZON-CL5-2021-D3-03), 2022-09-01 to 2026-12-31. Partner 2 of 7 in a consortium led by SINTEF. Sunlit Sea leads WP2 (Global framework & specifications) and WP8 (Dissemination, communication & exploitation). The EIC Transition proposal now being prepared builds directly on the SUREWAVE outputs — an integrated concept combining a floating photovoltaic (FPV) unit with a floating breakwater.
- **SuRE** (Horizon Europe), 2024-10-01 to 2027-08-31. Partner in a consortium led by Institutt for Energiteknikk (IFE). Sunlit Sea leads WP6 ("Sunlit's integrated FPV technology") which develops the numerical model chain (pressing simulation, structural FEM, thermal CFD, LCA) used to design and screen next-generation FPV prototypes.

## 2. Information about the EU project proposal

### 2.1 Project title

- **Full name:** SUREWAVE-based offshore floating photovoltaic demonstrator at Aguçadoura
- **Acronym:** SUREWAVE_DEMO

### 2.2 Sunlit Sea's role(s) in the planned EU proposal

Sunlit Sea will be a partner in a 5-partner consortium. Our specific role covers the floating photovoltaic (FPV) side of the integrated demonstrator: design of the FPV units, factory manufacturing, integration of standard PV modules into cast PU float-structures, factory acceptance testing, reliability assessment of the FPV during operations, and lead of the EIC Booster Grant activity.

Consortium (per meeting 2026-07-31):

- **SINTEF** (Norway) — coordinator, numerical modelling
- **Sunlit Sea AS** (Norway) — FPV unit design, manufacturing and factory acceptance
- **CLEMENT Systems** (Germany) — floating breakwater design and integration
- **EDP** (Portugal) — grid interface, techno-economic analysis, business model
- **WavEC** (Portugal) — site, permitting, offshore operations

Per the preliminary work-package structure agreed in the consortium, Sunlit Sea leads the following tasks:

- T2.1 Requirement Definition
- T2.3 FPV Platform Design
- T4.1 Monitoring Requirements (FPV-side instrumentation spec)
- T5.2 Prototype Manufacturing (PV module)
- T5.5 Acceptance and Factory Testing
- T7.2 Reliability Assessment
- T8.4 Booster Grant Activities

## 4. Horizon Europe Project Outline

| Field | Content |
|-------|---------|
| **Basic idea** | Pilot-scale (100-300 kWp) offshore floating photovoltaic demonstrator deployed at Aguçadoura (Portugal), integrated with a floating breakwater developed in the SUREWAVE project, to advance the technology from TRL 4-5 to TRL 6 through real offshore operation. |
| **Objectives** | Validate the integrated FPV + breakwater concept structurally, hydrodynamically and in terms of energy production under real offshore conditions. Generate the design-validation dataset needed to move from pilot to commercial rollout. Prepare the technology so the design documentation is aligned with DNV FPV standards (RP-0584, ST-C108, ST-E309), leaving the pilot "certification-ready" without requiring DNV to be a consortium partner. |
| **Key results** | (i) Deployed pilot at Aguçadoura with ~100-300 kWp installed capacity; (ii) 12+ months of offshore operational data covering structural loads, energy production, water ingress, corrosion, mooring loads; (iii) validated numerical design tools; (iv) DNV-alignment documentation package; (v) commercialization plan per partner geography. |
| **Impact** | Enables commercial deployment of offshore FPV. For Sunlit Sea specifically, the pilot delivers offshore-scale validation of the CONNECT gen 2 platform that supports the Norwegian nearshore project pipeline (Skien Havn, Storavatnet, Gunneklevfjorden) and opens the offshore market segment. For EDP: a technology option for Portugal's coastal renewable energy portfolio. For CLEMENT: proven integration of their breakwater in a productive-use context. For SINTEF and WavEC: contribution to European offshore FPV standard-setting and operational know-how. Contributes to EU renewable-energy targets and to the diversification of offshore ocean uses. |
| **European dimension** | Consortium spans three countries: Norway (Sunlit Sea, SINTEF), Portugal (EDP, WavEC) and Germany (CLEMENT). Combines complementary expertise that none of the partners could deliver alone — FPV design and manufacturing (Norway), breakwater design (Germany), offshore operations and permitting (Portugal), grid and utility integration (Portugal), numerical modelling and standards (Norway). The pilot site (Aguçadoura) provides sea conditions and grid access that Norwegian sites cannot replicate at this stage of development. |
| **Consortium / partners** | Coordinator: **SINTEF** (Norway). Partners: **Sunlit Sea AS** (Norway), **CLEMENT Systems** (Germany), **EDP** (Portugal), **WavEC** (Portugal). Consortium confirmed complete as of the meeting on 2026-07-31. |

## 5. Impact of PES for Sunlit Sea

Sunlit Sea operates with a lean cost structure that keeps our resources focused on gen 2 product development and near-term commercial rollout. PES support enables us to allocate the concentrated ~2-4 person-weeks of proposal-writing effort required for a competitive EIC Transition submission without diverting capacity from that critical path. We assess this proposal as strategically important both for Sunlit Sea's own technology development trajectory — offshore validation at TRL 6 that our Norwegian nearshore pipeline cannot itself provide — and for the Norwegian FPV cluster's positioning in the European offshore market.
