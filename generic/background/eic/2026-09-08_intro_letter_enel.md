Subject: EIC Transition proposal — invitation to explore a role for Enel

Hi,

I am contacting you regarding a new EIC Transition proposal that Sunlit Sea is preparing as a follow-up to our SUREWAVE project, together with SINTEF and CLEMENT.

The objective is to move the technology from component and subsystem validation to an integrated pilot-scale demonstration and prepare it for commercial deployment. The plan is to design, manufacture, deploy and operate a pilot FPV system in the 100–300 kWp range at a demonstration site on a German lake, validating the floating breakwater concept, FPV performance under real operating conditions, structural behaviour and reliability, monitoring frameworks, and energy yield.

Your Venaus FPV pilot and the Catania Innovation Lab work show a clear appetite for extending solar into new deployment formats. SUREWAVE would take that further — from sheltered hydropower reservoirs into larger and more exposed inland waters with significant wind-driven wave loading, opening deployment locations that are inaccessible to conventional FPV designs today. As a project developer with reach across European markets, Enel could bring significant value as a future adopter and commercialisation partner.

We would like to explore whether Enel would be interested in joining the consortium, contributing to demonstration, business development and future deployment.

If this could be of interest, I would be happy to organise a short meeting. The time until the application deadline is unfortunately a bit short, so I would appreciate a quick reply.

Best regards,
