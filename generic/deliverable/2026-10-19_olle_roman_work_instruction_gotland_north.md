# Work instruction — Gotland North site coordinator

## 1. At a glance

Olle Roman's working brief for the Nearshore floating solar Gotland demonstrator — a Horizon Europe EUPI-PV application (HORIZON-CL5-2026-11-D3-14, submission deadline 1 December 2026). A starting point for iteration between Olle and Sunlit Sea; expect revisions as the proposal is refined, the grant decision outcome becomes clearer, and Olle's own on-the-ground knowledge enters the picture.

**The project.** A 1 MWp civilian floating-solar demonstrator in Kappelshamnsviken bay off northern Gotland, approximately 20 m offshore. Floating PV panels packed in a matrix, moored at a fixed location, with an external concrete breakwater intercepting incoming waves. Part of a Horizon Europe application being submitted December 2026 for two in-water demonstrators (~1 MW Gotland + ~4 MW Germany). Project start preliminarily second half of 2027 if funded; installation window May-September 2029; design life approximately 25 years. Technology combines Sunlit Sea's second-generation CONNECT floater and a concrete breakwater developed in the predecessor SUREWAVE research project.

**Olle's role.** Civilian site coordinator for the Gotland end — on-the-ground liaison with Länsstyrelsen Gotland, Nordkalk, local community, surveyors. Not legal applicant for permits, not commercial negotiator, not technical designer; Sunlit Sea owns those, Olle supports. 10-30 % time averaged over 24 months, bursty rather than steady, with peaks around permit submissions, seabed surveys and installation preparation. Carried out entirely on Gotland, no travel required.

**Phase 0 (now until grant signing, likely late 2027) is unpaid.** Hours log into Phase 1-2 billing after the Grant Agreement is signed. If the application is not funded, Phase 0 hours are not compensated at all. Realistic planning assumption: roughly a 1-in-4 to 1-in-3 chance that this installation reaches grant signing on something close to the current schedule — more likely than not that Phase 0 hours remain uncompensated. Olle prices this into the hourly rate he negotiates (section 7.5, task 0.20).

**Three principles that order everything.** (1) Relationship before announcement, announcement before application, application with allies already briefed. (2) Nordkalk must never feel surprised. (3) The permit stack and the grid question are both critical path and must start pre-grant. Full discussion in section 4.

**Civilian framing is strict.** No "strategic", "critical infrastructure", "dual-use", "energy security" or similar language in any medium. Olle's formal-correspondence affiliation is "site coordinator, Sunlit Sea" — not his Forsvarsmakten role. Full do/don't list in section 5.

**How to use this document.** Section 7 is the actionable core — 21 Phase 0 tasks Olle starts on now. Sections 8-11 are reference material: workstream shape, stakeholder map, open questions, iteration path.

## 2. Project context

Key reference points beyond the overview:

- Site coordinates: 57.84413°N, 18.79715°E. Water depth roughly 10-30 m. Baltic limestone seabed.
- Floating PV footprint approximately 130 m diameter equivalent; cable to shore follows the shortest route into Nordkalk's Storugns industrial area.
- Technology stack: pressed marine-aluminium PV modules by Sunlit Sea, polyurethane hinges, concrete floating breakwater perimeter by Clement Germany, catenary mooring, structural health management by Ceit. Reached TRL 4-5 in SUREWAVE (2022-2026) via wave-basin campaigns (Stadt, Norway; MARIN, Netherlands) and a reduced-scale breakwater prototype at El Musel Port, Gijón.
- Project duration approximately 48 months; grant evaluation and agreement negotiation typically take 6-12 months post-submission; installation window May-September 2029.
- A paired demonstrator at a second EU site brings the proposal above the call's minimum 5 MW threshold. The Gotland workstream is regulatorily and logistically independent of the second site.

Verifying the site is Task 0.1 (section 7.1).

## 4. The three principles that order everything else

Three principles dominate the pacing of this work. Each is drawn from an area where getting it wrong is expensive and hard to reverse. They are listed first because every detailed task in sections 7-10 is subordinate to them.

**Principle 1 — Relationship before announcement, announcement before application, application with allies already briefed.** On Gotland after the Klinthagen decade, any new coastal development is scanned the moment it surfaces for who benefits and who pays. If the project becomes public before Olle has established informal relationships with the fishing community, the hembygdsförening, Länsstyrelsen handläggare, Region Gotland planning staff, Gotlands Ornitologiska Förening and at least one local journalist, each of those parties will react to the project cold — and their first impression will shape every subsequent interaction.

**Principle 2 — Nordkalk must never feel surprised.** Nordkalk (owned by UK-listed SigmaRoc since 2021) has just finished a decade-long permit battle for the Klinthagen limestone extraction, confirmed by Mark- och miljööverdomstolen in June 2026 but still under appeal to Högsta domstolen by Naturskyddsföreningen and Urbergsgruppen. Their facility management and corporate communications are on high alert for anything that re-opens local opposition. Any FPV demonstration adjacent to Storugns can either be a reputational asset (clean energy at an industrial site that has been cast as destructive) or a reputational threat (a new objection vector that opponents will attach to Nordkalk). Which one depends entirely on how Nordkalk is engaged in the first six months. The rule is: Nordkalk reads about the project from Olle before they read about it from a journalist, an NGO or a permit application.

**Principle 3 — The permit stack and grid question are both critical path, both long-lead, both must start pre-grant.** The Vattenverksamhet permit (Miljöbalken 11 kap.) takes 9-18 months from a complete application. The MKB that precedes it takes 6-12 months. Marine archaeological survey must be complete before the application. On the grid side, the preferred route is behind-the-meter via Nordkalk's existing industrial grid connection — if feasible, this avoids a separate Vattenfall Eldistribution process entirely. If behind-the-meter proves infeasible (substation incompatible, Nordkalk declines, regulatory barrier), the fallback is a separate DSO connection that takes 18-36 months from informal inquiry to energisation and becomes a hard critical-path item. Confirming behind-the-meter feasibility is a Phase 0 investigation (0.15). Either way, the project has to run the permit stack and the grid question in parallel, and the opening moves on all of them must happen before the EUPI-PV grant is confirmed.

## 5. The civilian framing (do-not-do list for the Forsvarsmakten boundary)

Olle holds a 100 % position at Forsvarsmakten and is taking tjänstledigt utan lön to work this project. The leave is approved on the understanding that the engagement is a civilian clean-energy demonstration with no overlap with Forsvarsmakten's remit. Keeping that clean separation is not a formality — Länsstyrelsen, local community actors, journalists and Nordkalk's own communications team will all read any framing that touches defence, dual-use or critical-infrastructure as a signal, and a wrong signal costs the project credibility it cannot afford to rebuild.

**Do not** frame or describe the project in any of the following terms, in any medium (meetings, email, slides, correspondence, informal remarks, social media, press interviews):

- "Strategic", "strategic autonomy", "energy security", "critical infrastructure", "infrastructure protection", "dual-use", "resilience" in a security sense, "sovereign", "Baltic security".
- Any connection between the FPV demonstrator and Forsvarsmakten activity, planning, exercises, installations or interests.
- Any mention of Olle's Forsvarsmakten role beyond the strictly necessary — in formal permit correspondence Olle's affiliation is "site coordinator, Sunlit Sea", not his day job.

**Do** frame the project consistently as:

- A civilian clean-energy demonstration under Horizon Europe funding.
- A Baltic solar demonstration taking advantage of Gotland's irradiation and the sheltered water off Storugns.
- A small-scale pilot (comparable in installed capacity to a few hundred rooftop systems) with a research purpose.
- An opportunity for local industrial co-location (Nordkalk Storugns) and local grid integration.
- A European collaboration with Norwegian, Swedish, German, Spanish and Dutch partners.

**The one procedural step that touches Försvarsmakten.** Every marine development on Gotland goes through a standard civilian consultation with Försvarsmaktens Marksamordningsområde (MSA) at project level. This consultation is routed through Sunlit Sea's project office in coordination with SINTEF — Olle neither initiates nor conducts it. Olle's only involvement is (a) providing accurate site data for the submission and (b) keeping a status trace of this consultation in the risk register. Treat MSA like Transportstyrelsen or Sjöfartsverket: a civilian regulatory step owned by Sunlit Sea's project office.

**Phase 2 — MKB, surveys, early-permit workstreams (Q1 2028 — Q3 2028).**

If grant decision slips, the whole sequence shifts proportionally but the internal order does not. If the paired demonstrator moves faster, the WP leader and Sunlit Sea's project office manage the CINEA milestone structure so Gotland is not strand-cost.

## 7. Phase 0 — pre-grant site preparation (do this now)

This phase is almost entirely desk work and quiet relationship-building. It costs almost nothing in out-of-pocket expense. It cannot be skipped. Every item below should be in motion before the December 2026 EUPI-PV proposal is submitted or very shortly after.

**Compensation and risk.** Phase 0 is unpaid. Olle logs hours against each task; those hours are rolled into Phase 1-2 billing after the Grant Agreement is signed. If the EUPI-PV application is not funded, Phase 0 hours are not compensated at all — the time is at Olle's own risk. This is a deliberate commercial arrangement: Sunlit Sea cannot carry Phase 0 cost before the grant is confirmed, and Olle cannot be paid from EU funds that do not yet exist. For Olle to accept the arrangement knowingly, two things need to happen before Phase 0 work starts: (a) Olle explicitly accepts the unpaid-until-grant structure in writing, and (b) the hourly rate he quotes for the full engagement incorporates the Phase 0 risk — i.e. the paid Phase 1-5 hours carry the loading needed to make the overall engagement economically acceptable at a realistic grant-award probability. See task 0.20 for the contractual mechanics.

**Perspective on the grant-award probability.** The probability that this specific installation reaches grant signing is substantially less than one; Olle's commitment should be sized to that base rate, not to the assumption that funding arrives.

- Call structure. The HORIZON-CL5-2026-11-D3-14 EUPI-PV call has a €30M envelope and funds four projects: two offshore/nearshore floating PV + two agriPV. The Gotland proposal competes for one of the two FPV slots.
- Baseline hit rate. HORIZON-CL5 Innovation Actions historically run 10-20 % overall success. A call with a defined two-award slot and a specialised technical scope (large-scale open-water FPV) narrows the eligible pool and raises that somewhat — a rough frame is 15-30 % at the FPV topic level, with real variance between calls.
- Parallel dependency. Sunlit Sea's position in the consortium is reinforced by a separate EIC Transition SUREWAVE application already under way, which — if awarded and executed on schedule — lifts the second-generation CONNECT floater to TRL 6 by end 2027. Without that award, Sunlit Sea's EUPI-PV contribution is reduced to a lower-TRL reference and the proposal's competitiveness weakens.
- Timing risk. Even if awarded, grant evaluation + agreement negotiation can slip 6-12 months beyond the nominal 2027 start, meaning Phase 0 extends longer than planned before any Phase 1 compensation kicks in.

A reasonable planning assumption for Olle is roughly a 1-in-4 to 1-in-3 chance that this installation reaches grant signing on something close to the current schedule. The right read is that Phase 0 hours are more likely than not to remain uncompensated at the end — plan savings runway, Forsvarsmakten tjänstledighet shape, and the quoted hourly rate on that base rate, not on the assumption of funding.

### 7.1 Verify the site

**0.1 Verify the cement/limestone works and its operator.** The site is adjacent to the Nordkalk limestone operation at Storugns on northern Gotland — a quarry (Klinthagen), limestone handling terminal and port shipping limestone to Swedish and Finnish steel mills (approximately 400 vessel calls, 2.7 million tonnes/year). Nordkalk is owned by UK-listed SigmaRoc Group (acquired 2021). Confirm current corporate structure and the Storugns platschef and miljöchef by name via Nordkalk's website and SigmaRoc annual report. (Not Cementa — Cementa / Heidelberg Materials is in Slite, further south.)

**0.2 Confirm which socken.** The site is almost certainly in Lärbro socken (which contains Storugns), not in Hangvar which lies to the northwest. Verify against Lantmäteriet's socken map before any communication naming the socken. Hangvar views matter for sightlines and coastal access but Lärbro is the primary affected community.

**0.3 Fastighetsutredning — who owns the water surface.** Order a fastighetsutdrag from Lantmäteriet for the parcels adjacent to the coordinates. Map the fastighet boundaries into the water. Swedish seabed ownership defaults vary: shoreline fastigheter may extend to 300 m from strandlinjen or to a specific depth contour, or the sea may be allmänt vattenområde. If the water is Nordkalk's registered fastighet, the project needs their consent as landowner regardless of permits. If it is allmänt vattenområde, state tenure applies. This is the single piece of information that most changes downstream planning; do it first. Cost: ~50-100 kSEK if a lantmätare is engaged for a proper utredning; much less for an initial fastighetsutdrag.

How to start: order basic fastighetsutdrag via Lantmäteriet's self-service portal (lantmateriet.se, "Beställ fastighetsutdrag") for the shoreline fastighet(er) at the coordinates and the two neighbours on each side — a few hundred SEK per fastighet, delivered as PDF within days. Read the "Fastighetens omfattning" section to see whether vattenbrev or vattenområde is listed; a shoreline fastighet with vattenbrev typically extends to the 300 m line or to a historical fiskevatten boundary. If the utdrag is ambiguous (common for older fastigheter on Gotland), escalate to a lantmätare — Lantmäteriet's Gotland regional office (Visby) handles vattenutredningar directly; alternatively engage a Swedish property lawyer with fastighetsrätt experience to interpret. Cross-check with the historisk-fastighet layer in Lantmäteriet's Min karta viewer, which sometimes shows older boundaries not reflected in the current register.

**0.4 Nautical chart overlay against Storugns port approach.** Obtain Sjöfartsverket's nautical chart for Storugns harbour approaches (chart 611 or 525 region). Plot the 130 m diameter FPV footprint at the project coordinates against declared farleder, turning basins, anchorage areas and navigation marks. If the footprint intersects the port approach line for Nordkalk's bulk carriers (up to ~23,000 DWT, 9 m draught), the layout needs to shift and the entire site may need re-evaluation. Do this before the second meeting with anyone, because the whole project geometry depends on it.

How to start: Sjöfartsverket's digital charts are sold through their e-handel (sjofartsverket.se → "Sjökort") in ENC or raster format; a single chart cell for Gotland east is a one-off purchase. For free lower-detail overlays, EMODnet Bathymetry and OpenSeaMap work for first-pass orientation but should not be the authoritative source for the actual regulatory overlay. The practical workflow: import the Sjöfartsverket raster into QGIS (free), add a layer with the project coordinates, draw the 130 m-diameter circle representing the FPV footprint, then measure clearance to the nearest farledgräns and to any declared ankarplats. Flag any clearance below 100 m as a planning problem; below 50 m is a planning blocker. Capture the overlay as a PDF with scale bar and north arrow — this becomes the first visual in every subsequent conversation about site geometry. If GIS work is outside Olle's comfort zone, a two-day engagement with a Gotland-based GIS consultant (Region Gotland lantmäteri contacts can recommend one) handles the first pass cheaply.

**0.5 Natura 2000 overlap check.** Download Naturvårdsverket's Natura 2000-skiktet (NVDB) and overlay against the site plus a 5-10 km buffer and the probable cable landing route. Candidate overlaps include Gotlands nordkust (coastal SCI/SPA for limestone coast, seabirds, seals) and other designations. If the footprint overlaps even partially with a Natura 2000 area, a full Article 6(3) appropriate assessment is required under Miljöbalken 7 kap. 28 a §, adding 12-24 months to the regulatory timeline.

How to start: the fastest first-pass check is Naturvårdsverket's Skyddad natur map viewer at skyddadnatur.naturvardsverket.se — toggle the "Natura 2000, habitat" and "Natura 2000, fågel" layers, zoom to the coordinates, read off any designation that overlaps or borders the site. Gotland Natura 2000 sites carry codes in the SE0340xxxx range (SCI) and SE0341xxxx range (SPA); note any code that appears within 5 km and query it in the EU's Natura 2000 Standard Data Form search (natura2000.eea.europa.eu) for conservation objectives, habitat types and listed species. For the actual regulatory evidence pack, download the authoritative shapefile from Naturvårdsverket's open-data portal (geodata.naturvardsverket.se) and run the overlay in QGIS against the FPV footprint + cable route. Also check Länsstyrelsen Gotland's own naturreservat and biotopskyddsområde layers — these are separate from Natura 2000 but can trigger parallel permit requirements. Document the result as a one-page memo even if no overlap is found: a clean no-overlap finding is itself evidence to attach to the samråd request in 0.8.

**0.6 Fornsök desk search.** Query RAÄ Fornsök (app.raa.se/fornsok) for all registered fornlämningar within a 5 km radius — both marine (vattenbaserade) and coastal. Flag any marine entries. Marine registration in Fornsök significantly lags terrestrial coverage, so a clean result does not mean the site is archaeologically clear; it means no survey has confirmed remains yet. The archaeological risk is on the mooring footprint, not the floating platform.

How to start: in Fornsök, use the "Avancerad sökning" and filter by lämningstyp = "fartygslämning", "vrak", "hamn/ankarplats" and "fyndplats"; map-pan to the coordinates and use the drawing tool to query a 5 km radius polygon; export the result as PDF or Excel for the project archive. Fornsök's marine coverage is thin — supplement with (a) Sjöhistoriska / SMTM-Vrak's wreck database (vrak.se) which covers 1850-1945 Baltic naval losses that Fornsök does not; (b) Länsmuseet Gotland's marinarkeologiska arkiv in Visby, which holds unpublished survey reports and local informant records that never made it into the national register — contact the museum's marinarkeolog by phone; (c) Nordkalk's own Klinthagen miljökonsekvensbeskrivning from the 2010s may include coastal archaeological clearance for the Storugns port area, obtainable via Länsstyrelsen diarium under offentlighetsprincipen. Build one combined anomaly list in a shared spreadsheet from the start — this becomes the base layer for the Phase 1 geophysical survey (section 8.6).

**0.7 Vattenfall Eldistribution kapacitetskarta check.** Vattenfall Eldistribution publishes a hosting-capacity map on their website. Check the Storugns / northern Gotland area. If hosting capacity at the nearest network node is below 1 MW, a nätförstärkningsanalys is essentially certain and the connection conversation starts from a deficit position.

### 7.3 Grid pre-inquiry

**0.15 Behind-the-meter feasibility (primary) + Vattenfall Eldistribution informal föranmälan (fallback).** The preferred connection route is behind-the-meter via Nordkalk's Storugns industrial grid connection — this would avoid a separate DSO process entirely, simplify metering, and align naturally with the PPA conversation already in motion (section 8.4). The primary task is to confirm whether this is technically and commercially feasible: the substation's voltage level, transformer capacity, busbar spare capacity, Nordkalk's willingness to amend their nätanslutningsavtal, and any EI/regulatory barrier all have to line up. This runs as a Phase 0 technical question routed through the Nordkalk engagement workstream (8.4), with input from Vattenfall Eldistribution on the regulatory side.

The feasibility question cannot be answered by any single data point — the whole picture has to come together before Sunlit Sea can commit to the behind-the-meter route or firmly fall back to the DSO one. Olle maps the Nordkalk Storugns electrical setup across five linked areas; the information comes from Nordkalk directly (via workstream 8.4) and from Vattenfall Eldistribution on the DSO side:

- **Electrical infrastructure mapping.** Which meters Storugns operates and where they sit — the main Vattenfall debiteringsmätare at the DSO-industry interface (point of common coupling), plus any Nordkalk-internal sub-meters for process areas (crusher plant, kiln if any, port cranes and conveyors, lighting, pumping). A single-line diagram of the site electrical distribution — incoming feeders, main transformer(s) with rating and spare capacity, MV busbars with current rating, LV distribution, process loads, protection — is the end product. Voltage level at the PCC (likely 10 kV or 20 kV from Vattenfall's distribution network). Physical location of the main substation and the practical cable route from the proposed FPV coordinates (approximately 20 m offshore east shore) to that substation. Nordkalk's elektriker or facility management owns this information.

- **Consumption profile data.** Hourly meter data from the main debiteringsmätare for the last 24 months, longer if available. Obtainable from Vattenfall Eldistribution as "mätvärden" against the anläggnings-ID, or from Nordkalk's own facility energy monitoring if they operate one. The daytime load profile determines whether FPV generation is mostly self-consumed (which makes behind-the-meter PPA economics compelling) or whether significant export to the DSO grid is unavoidable (which requires DSO coordination even inside a behind-the-meter arrangement). Decompose into: night-time baseload (continuous processes, port equipment standby, lighting) vs daytime peak (crushing, loading, ship handling); seasonal pattern (summer vs winter, shipping season vs maintenance season); day-of-week pattern (24/7 vs weekday-only operation).

- **Reactive power, power quality and storage.** Any large motors, crushers, conveyors or compressors on the site — these are typically inductive loads producing reactive power that the installation already manages (via capacitor banks or synchronous compensation). Adding an FPV inverter park changes the reactive-power balance at the PCC; the existing compensation strategy has to be known so the inverter specification integrates rather than conflicts. Any variable-frequency drives, large rectifiers, blasting-related impulse loads — these contribute harmonic distortion and affect the inverter's grid-code compliance (IEC 61000-3-12, EIFS 2018:2) at the PCC. Any existing battery storage, UPS, or planned additions — batteries materially change the self-consumption calculation. Nordkalk's historical measured power factor at the PCC — the Vattenfall nätavtal likely includes a reactive-energy charge that FPV generation can help offset (or worsen if the inverter spec is wrong).

- **Commercial and tariff setup.** Copies of Nordkalk's recent Vattenfall Eldistribution invoices (nätavgifter / nätfakturor) — give the actual tariff structure, the effekttariff (demand charge), the reactive-energy charge if any, the anläggnings-ID. Copies of Nordkalk's elhandelsfakturor — give the actual elleveransavtal structure (fixed, spot, PPA already in place, hedging), the elhandelsbolag identity, and whether there is any clause that affects on-site self-generation. Any special arrangements Nordkalk has with Vattenfall Eldistribution (flexibility contract, demand-response participation, time-of-use tariff, interruptible supply) or with their elhandelsbolag (long-term supply contract, corporate PPA elsewhere, green certificate obligations) that would be affected by adding on-site FPV generation. SigmaRoc Group-level power procurement arrangements that may constrain or enable Storugns specifically — a group-wide corporate PPA that already covers Storugns consumption is a plausible blocker.

- **Regulatory confirmation.** Confirm with Energimarknadsinspektionen (EI) that a behind-the-meter PPA between Sunlit Sea (as generator) and Nordkalk (as consumer) does not trigger separate elleveranskoncession or elproduktionskoncession obligations under Ellagen. The common structure — on-site generation feeding an industrial facility under the facility's existing nätanslutning — is permitted, but the regulatory details depend on the generator-consumer legal relationship and the metering topology. Confirm the metering arrangement Vattenfall Eldistribution will accept (gross FPV output + Nordkalk net offtake + residual export, with Nordkalk as anläggningsägare and Sunlit Sea as self-generation producer under a lease or PPA structure).

In parallel — and only as a fallback — file a quiet informal inquiry to Vattenfall Eldistribution's regional office in Visby about a separate 1 MWp solar PV connection at the Storugns coordinates: indication of connection possibility at the nearest suitable voltage level (10 kV or 20 kV), approximate reinforcement scope and indicative cost. The Vattenfall inquiry serves two purposes: a credible fallback timeline (18-36 months) if behind-the-meter is confirmed infeasible, and a connection timeline to cite in Section 3 of the EUPI-PV proposal. If behind-the-meter is confirmed early and firmly, the Vattenfall track can be de-prioritised; while there is doubt, both tracks stay live.

### 8.6 Marine archaeology workstream

Legal framework: Kulturmiljölagen (SFS 1988:950) 2 kap. All shipwrecks pre-1850 are automatically protected as fornlämningar — Fornsök listing is not required. Any seabed disturbance affecting a fornlämning without prior tillstånd from Länsstyrelsen is a criminal offence under KML 2 kap. 12 §.

Risk realism: the Baltic preserves wooden wrecks extraordinarily well (the "Vasa effect" — no Teredo navalis in low-salinity water, near-anaerobic sediments, cold temperatures). Gotland sits astride a major Hanseatic-to-20th-century shipping corridor; wreck density off northern Gotland is well above the Baltic baseline. The project site sits between the Kappelshamn (30 km northwest) and Fårö Sund (northeast) wreck-dense areas. The archaeological risk is entirely on the mooring system — anchor piles, drilled rock anchors, drag anchors, chain scour. The floating platform itself does not touch the seabed. Mooring layout must be developed in close coordination with the archaeological anomaly map — not designed first and then checked.

Timeline realism: 9-18 months from project decision to go-ahead for mooring installation. 6 months is optimistic only if no significant anomalies are found and weather cooperates. A mid-season halt to mid-installation after a chance find (KML 2 kap. 10 §: stop immediately, report, 4-8 week minimum assessment lead time) is a serious programme risk.

**Phase 0 desk assessment (section 7.1 — tasks 0.6):** Fornsök search + Kulturmiljöregister riksintresse search + historical nautical chart review from Sjöhistoriska / Krigsarkivet + Länsmuseet Gotland records request + Nordkalk Klinthagen archaeological clearance request from Länsstyrelsen diarium.

**Phase 0/1 consultation:** written samrådsunderlag to Länsstyrelsen Gotland kulturmiljöenheten and a pre-application meeting with RAÄ Kulturmiljöavdelningen / SMTM-Vrak. Objective: formal scoping opinion specifying required survey standard before tillståndsansökan can be accepted.

**Phase 1 fishermen consultation:** meetings with 3-5 active commercial and long-term recreational fishermen. Net snags, anchor problems, sonar anomalies. Not formal inventory — survey-targeting input. Kappelshamns Fiskareförening is the natural focal point.

**Phase 1 survey programme (procured by Sunlit Sea):** combined geophysical survey (side-scan + magnetometer + sub-bottom profiler + multibeam) over the full mooring footprint plus 200 m buffer; anomaly classification submitted to Länsstyrelsen Gotland; licensed marine archaeology diver inspection on priority anomalies; coastal walkover for cable landing by a licensed field archaeologist. Mobilisation lead time 3-4 months; window May-August.

**Phase 2 integration:** mooring footprint overlay against anomaly map, shift pile positions to avoidance zones (iterative with Sunlit Sea / SINTEF mooring design); mitigation plan with avoidance as primary strategy; chance-find procedure in all marine installation contracts.

**20th-century wrecks:** not pre-1850 but still relevant to MKB, and may be protected under foreign nations' legislation if warships (Baltic naval losses 1939-45). A German submarine or minesweeper in the footprint raises munitions-safety and diplomatic-notification issues beyond archaeology. The geophysical survey is designed to detect these targets too.

Key institutional contacts: Länsstyrelsen Gotland kulturmiljöenheten (permitting authority), Länsmuseet Gotland marinarkeologisk avdelning (essential early engagement partner), Riksantikvarieämbetet, SMTM/Vrak (Museum of Wrecks, Stockholm).

### 8.7 Marine logistics workstream

Vessel availability — not site readiness — is the single most constraining item on the critical path. Baltic offshore wind projects pre-book heavy-lift crane barges and anchor handlers for May-September 2029 from mid-2027 onwards. If the project's vessels are not committed by end-2027, the installation slips to 2030.

**Port of staging — the primary decision.** Three on-island candidates (Storugns, Fårösund, Visby) and mainland alternatives (Oxelösund SSAB area, Nynäshamn, Lövsta). Each has trade-offs on depth, lay-down space, tow distance and operational conflict with Nordkalk's bulk shipping. Likely optimal: pre-cast concrete breakwater units on mainland, tow to Storugns for final assembly, install from Storugns or barge at site. Each breakwater unit is approximately 20 m × 5 m × 2.5 m, 120+ tonnes at full scale.

**Vessel market.** Baltic crane-barge operators and anchor handlers (Boskalis, Jan De Nul, Normann & Thomsson, Siem Offshore, Havila Shipping) are typically North Sea-mobilised, 4-6 weeks transit. Pre-booking Q3-Q4 2027. Local Gotland tug capacity: none; dedicated tug mobilised from Göteborg or Malmö for 4-6 weeks. Marine insurance (Marsh Marine, WTW Marine) pre-placed Q3 2027. Classification society review via DNV SE (Göteborg).

**Ice and weather windows.** Installation is only viable May-September. Ice-affected mid-January to mid-March in cold years (2010, 2023 were worst). SMHI hindcast: May-June most favourable sea state; September weather degrades fast (> 1.5 m Hs probability rises to 30-35 %). Target start 1 May 2029; total campaign 6 weeks including weather contingency.

Key tasks on Olle's side: port dialogues on-island (Storugns, Fårösund, Visby), vessel market sounding memo updates for Sunlit Sea, local tug market check, FPV module shipping route confirmation, Sjöfartsverket pre-consultation on marking and construction-phase vessel management.

## 10. Open questions and verification needs (Phase 0 to resolve, before anything else is committed)

Each of these is an assumption in the current brief that must be verified before downstream work hangs on it. In rough priority order:

1. **Which socken and which fastighet.** Confirm Lärbro socken administrative boundary covers the coordinates (section 7.1 task 0.2). Confirm fastighet ownership of the water surface via Lantmäteriet (task 0.3). The whole rest of the brief assumes Lärbro + ambiguous (either Nordkalk fastighet or allmänt vattenområde).
2. **Natura 2000 overlap.** Confirm whether the site intersects a designated area or buffer (task 0.5). If yes, add 12-24 months to the regulatory timeline for an Article 6(3) appropriate assessment; the Gotland site may not fit the project window at all without a redesign.
3. **Storugns port approach line.** Confirm the FPV footprint does not sit on the Nordkalk bulk-carrier approach line (task 0.4). If it does, the site needs to shift or the layout needs to compress — do this before any other geometry work.
4. **Vattenfall Eldistribution hosting capacity at Storugns.** Check the published kapacitetskarta. If below 1 MW at the nearest node, nätförstärkningsanalys is essentially certain (section 8.2).
5. **Nordkalk's industrial substation as a candidate PCC.** Voltage level, transformer capacity, spare busbar capacity are not public. If viable and Nordkalk cooperates, behind-the-meter PPA dramatically simplifies grid connection.
6. **OX2 / Nordkalk e-fuel grid reservation.** If OX2 has already been allocated capacity at Nordkalk's substation or the nearest DSO node, FPV capacity may be constrained (task 0.16).
7. **Fornsök and local museum records near site.** Preliminary Fornsök search (task 0.6) and Länsmuseet Gotland records request before survey contractor is engaged.
8. **MKB threshold at 1 MWp FPV.** No explicit Swedish threshold for floating solar. Full MKB is likely but not certain; Länsstyrelsen behovsbedömning decides. Clarify at 0.8 informal samråd.
9. **Strandskydd seaward extent and FPV applicability.** Does the offshore water-surface installation itself (as opposed to land-based infrastructure) constitute an åtgärd requiring dispens? Case law thin; obtain Länsstyrelsen's position explicitly at 0.8.
10. **Vattenverksamhet-tillstånd vs anmälan at 1 MWp.** Likely full tillstånd from MMD rather than anmälan to Länsstyrelsen (anmälningsgränsen in Förordning 2013:14), but confirm at 0.8.
11. **Nordkalk fastighets­gränser into the water.** May include the seabed under part of the FPV footprint. If so, nyttjanderättsavtal or arrende is required regardless of other permits.
12. **Klinthagen HD appeal timing.** Prövningstillstånd decision from Högsta domstolen pending. The timing changes Nordkalk's communications posture and the window of co-marketing possibility.
13. **Grant start date (T0).** EUPI-PV evaluation 6-12 months after December 2026 submission → T0 is likely H1-H2 2028, not H2 2027 as some sections above assume. Confirms the installation window target: 2029 or 2030?
14. **Paired-demonstrator timing relative to Gotland in the CINEA milestone structure.** Milestone definitions in the Grant Agreement must allow partial payment on the paired site's completion so that Gotland slip does not strand that tranche.

17. **Sunlit Sea manufacturing location for the Gotland array.** Shipping route plan from factory gate to Visby/Storugns depends on this (section 8.7).
