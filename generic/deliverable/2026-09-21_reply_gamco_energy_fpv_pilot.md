Dear Mr. Gmati,

Thank you for reaching out and for the detailed description of the pilot, including the 3D layout and the JA Solar datasheet.

A short framing note before the specifics. Our current product is the Sunlit Sea CONNECT Gen 2 system. The Gen 1 float you may have seen on our website is no longer in production. Gen 2 is close to commercial release but is not yet in commercial deployment, so a firm supply commitment inside the timeline of your 24/09/2026 tender is not something we can offer today.

Gen 2 is engineered around a single fixed configuration. The specific points in your enquiry that this affects:

- Panel size and type. The Gen 2 float carries a standard glass-glass panel in the ≈710 – 740 Wp / 2384 × 1303 mm class as a monofacial module. The JAM66D45-620/LB you propose is 2382 × 1134 × 30 mm — a narrower footprint that does not fit the frame — and it is a bifacial double-glass module. The rear face of each Gen 2 module is closed by cast PU foam, so any bifacial gain would be zero. Customer-supplied modules are not supported; the panel choice is part of the Gen 2 system specification.
- Tilt. The Gen 2 module tilt is 2°, fixed by the CONNECT hinge geometry that couples adjacent modules and by the 6 cm lower-edge freeboard. The 12° tilt in your layout is not achievable with the standard product, and we do not offer custom-tilt variants.
- Anchoring and mooring. Not part of what we supply. Mooring design, anchor points and installation are the developer's or EPC's scope in every project we deliver. The non-perforation requirement for the geomembrane sits naturally in that scope — shore-based anchoring to the basin crown or perimeter is standard practice on lined reservoirs, but it is your engineers who own that design.

Given the three points above, the pilot as currently specified does not match our standard product. Two paths from here:

1. If the tender allows the module choice to be revisited, a monofacial glass-glass panel in the standard 2384 × 1303 mm class (≈710 – 740 Wp) is compatible with Gen 2. That still leaves the 12° tilt and the mooring scope to resolve on your side, but the float itself becomes a fit.
2. If the specification is fixed on the JAM66D45-620/LB and the 12° tilt, the pilot is not a Sunlit Sea system today.

Please let us know whether either of these paths works on your side. We would also be interested in the wider ~10-site pipeline you mentioned if the standard Gen 2 configuration fits the follow-on installations.
