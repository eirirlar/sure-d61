This Power Purchase Agreement (the "**Agreement**") is entered into between

(A) **\[Seller\]**, a company organized under the laws of Norway, with business registration number \[Seller Organization Number\] and business address at \[Seller Address\] (the "**Seller**"); and

(B) **Aaltvedt Betong AS**, a company organized under the laws of Norway with business registration number \[Buyer Organization Number\] and business address at Havnevegen 72, Skien, Norway (the "**Buyer**").

The Buyer and the Seller are individually referred to as a/the "**Party**" and collectively as the "**Parties**".

**WHEREAS**,

(A) The Seller provides technology to the floating solar industry;

(B) The Buyer operates a concrete production facility at Havnevegen 72, Skien, Norway, with a substantial and continuous demand for electric power for its manufacturing operations;

(C) the Buyer wishes to engage the Seller to provide solar power energy to the Buyer's concrete production facility at Havnevegen 72, Skien, Norway; and

(D) the Seller wishes to develop and operate the Facility (as defined below), and deliver solar power energy to the Buyer as specified in Appendix 1 and according to the provisions of this Agreement; and

# Effective Date -- Term of the Agreement

## Effective Date

This Agreement, when signed off by the Parties, will constitute a binding agreement upon the Parties.

Upon fulfillment of the conditions precedent in Clause 3, the Seller shall install the Facility, estimated to be finalized \[120\] days from fulfillment of said conditions precedent.

The Parties assume that the Facility will be put into operation as soon as it has been installed and connected to the Buyer's concrete production facility, save for an initial testing period estimated to \[Test Period\] days, and the Seller's obligation to deliver solar power energy to the Buyer shall commence at the end of the testing period (the "**Effective Date**").

## Term

This Agreement shall remain in effect for and expire automatically two (2) years following the Effective Date, unless earlier terminated in accordance with terms and conditions of this Agreement.

# Conditions Precedent

The commencement of this Agreement and the Seller's installation obligations are subject to each of the following conditions precedent being satisfied (or waived by the Party benefiting therefrom) prior to \[Conditions Precedent Deadline\]:

3.1 **Site rights and facilitation.** The Buyer shall have granted the Seller all necessary rights in order to install and operate the Facility, including access to land and premises, right to place the Facility at sea, right to dig cable trenches, right to install inverters, cabinets and associated infrastructure, and access to the Buyer's electrical meters and infrastructure, all as further illustrated in Appendix 1. For the avoidance of doubt, such rights shall, if necessary, include property and rights held by third parties. No compensation shall be paid by the Seller for rights as set out in this Clause 3.1. The Buyer shall in good faith facilitate a smooth installation, including timely coordination and access. Costs incurred by the Seller due to unreasonable obstruction or lack of cooperation by the Buyer shall be reimbursed by the Buyer.

3.2 **Licenses, permits and Skien harbor authorization.** The Seller/Buyer shall have obtained necessary licenses, permits and authorizations for the construction and operation of the Facility, including production and sale of electricity as further laid out in this Agreement. In particular, the permit issued by Skien harbor (Skien havn) for the placement of the Facility in Vollsfjorden shall be in effect at the time of installation and shall remain in effect throughout the Term. Should the permit be withdrawn or lapse prior to the Effective Date, this Agreement shall be terminated without further cost to either Party. In the event the Seller, making all reasonable endeavors, is unable to obtain such necessary licenses, permits, or authorizations within \[Authorization Date\], the Agreement shall be terminated.

3.3 **Grid connection.** The Buyer shall have entered into a grid connection agreement with the local distribution system operator, or, alternatively, provide the Seller with a written statement in which the Buyer confirms that such agreement will not be required. The Buyer shall at all times be responsible for maintaining grid connectivity for the Buyer's own consumption meter at Havnevegen 72 throughout the Term.

3.4 **Seller financing.** The Seller shall have secured sufficient funding to realize the Facility within the prevailing cost estimate, including such combination of public support, grant funding and private financing as the Seller deems necessary to proceed. Should the Seller not have secured such financing within \[Financing Deadline\], the Seller may, at its sole discretion, terminate this Agreement without further obligation to the Buyer.

3.5 **Grid tariff assumptions.** The commercial basis for this Agreement assumes that (i) the Delivery Point is treated as a low-voltage connection, (ii) the Facility is classified under Lede AS's prosumer regime applicable to installations of this scale, and (iii) no anleggsbidrag (grid connection contribution) or comparable one-off capital charge is levied by Lede AS on connection of the Facility. Should any of these assumptions not be confirmed by Lede AS prior to the Effective Date, the Parties shall enter into good faith renegotiation of the Price Per kWh under Clause 5.3; failing agreement within thirty (30) days of such determination, either Party may terminate this Agreement without further obligation.

3.6 **Buyer electrical installation.** The Buyer's existing electrical installation shall be in compliance with applicable Norwegian regulations, including without limitation NEK 400 and Forskrift om elektriske lavspenningsanlegg (FEL). Compliance shall be verified by an authorized electrician at the time of installation of the Facility. Should upgrades or modifications to the Buyer's installation be required in order for the Facility to be safely connected and operated in accordance with applicable regulations, the Buyer shall bear the cost of such upgrades or modifications.

# Delivery and Maintenance

1.  The Seller shall make solar power energy available under this Agreement at the Delivery Point, and the ownership and risk of loss of solar power energy delivered in accordance with this Agreement shall pass from the Seller to the Buyer at the Delivery Point.

2.  The Seller shall install the Facility Meter at the Delivery Point, at the alternating-current output of the Facility's inverter(s), to measure the amount of Net Electrical Output delivered by the Seller to the Buyer. The Seller shall own, operate and maintain the Facility Meter during the entire term of this Agreement as defined in Clause 2 (*Term*) at its own expense. The Facility Meter shall be a standard revenue quality meter and electronic data acquisition equipment that can be used continuously to measure and record the Net Electrical Output.

3.  The Seller shall read the Facility Meter daily and shall record the Net Electrical Output delivered to the Buyer. Upon written request, the Seller will make available to the Buyer the records from the Facility Meter.

4.  The Seller shall provide standard quality calibration testing of the Facility Meter prior to putting it into operation and at least annually thereafter to ensure the accuracy of the Facility Meter. The Facility Meter shall be sealed and locked after each calibration testing. The Buyer shall be entitled to witness such tests.

In the event of damaged or faulty meters, the Seller shall make output estimates in accordance with good faith based on historical data (if available) or predicted values.

No minimum production of Net Electrical Output shall be guaranteed delivered at the Delivery Point.

The Seller shall ensure operation, reparation and maintenance of the Facility consistent at all times with Prudent Utility Practice to ensure compliance with the requirements of this Agreement, subject to Clauses 4.9, 4.10 and 4.11 below.

## Scheduled and unscheduled outages

If possible, scheduled outages shall take place outside of generation hours and peak generation months. The Buyer shall not be entitled to any compensation upon outages.

## Curtailment, grid downtime and network maintenance

In the event of curtailment, grid downtime and network maintenance, the Buyer shall pay for the energy which could have been produced and delivered at the Delivery Point. The Seller shall specify such amount of energy upon invoicing.

### Operation and maintenance

The Buyer shall keep the solar panels of the Facility clean, in accordance with the operation and maintenance manual, to ensure optimal performance. The Seller shall be responsible for maintenance of the inverter(s), mooring and anchoring and other equipment falling within the Seller's operational domain, subject to Clauses 4.9, 4.10 and 4.11 below.

In the event of power outages or power production being limited due to poor or missing operation and maintenance for which the Buyer is responsible, including without limitation the Buyer's failure to keep the solar panels clean, the Buyer shall pay for the energy which could have been produced and delivered at the Delivery Point. The Seller shall specify such amount of energy upon invoicing, together with a reasoned basis for the estimate.

### Individual panel defects

Should individual solar panels of the Facility become defective or otherwise cease to produce electricity, the Seller shall not be obligated to repair or replace such panels. The estimated energy that such defective panels would otherwise have produced during the period of defect (calculated on the basis of the Facility's expected production profile and the rated output of the affected panels) shall be deducted from the invoiceable Net Electrical Output for the period. The Seller shall notify the Buyer of any such defect and provide a reasoned basis for the deduction.

### Non-critical inverter faults

Non-critical faults on the Facility's inverter(s) fall within the Seller's operational domain. The Seller may, at its discretion, elect not to repair such non-critical faults, provided that the Facility otherwise operates in compliance with applicable safety and regulatory requirements. A fault is "non-critical" for the purposes of this Clause 4.10 where it does not compromise safety, does not constitute a regulatory breach, and does not materially impair the Facility's overall production.

## Billing and Payment

The Seller shall submit to the Buyer latest by the fifteenth (15) day of the following month an original copy of its invoice for the sale of electricity supplied to the Buyer in accordance with this Agreement. Each invoice submitted shall relate to only one-month service provided and shall include:

(a) The amount of Net Electrical Output during the billed month; and

(b) The payment kWh unit rate for the current agreement year pursuant to Clause 5.3.

Each invoice shall be due and payable to the Seller ten (10) days from receipt of the Seller's invoice.

Should the Buyer dispute an invoice, it shall notify the Seller within five (5) business days of receipt of such invoice. The Buyer shall not be entitled to defer payment thereof beyond the date specified for payment but shall pay such an invoice except in instances of manifest error. In the event the Parties resolve a dispute regarding an invoice within fifteen (15) days, each Party shall be entitled to refer the dispute for dispute resolution in accordance with this Agreement for a determination. In the event it is found that an error was made on the invoice, such invoice shall be canceled and thereafter a new invoice shall be issued within ten (10) days from the resolution of such dispute.

Any amounts not paid when due (unless disputed in good faith) shall bear interest according to the Norwegian act relating to interest on overdue payments.

# Event of Force Majeure

## Definition of an Event of Force Majeure

In this Agreement, an "**Event of Force Majeure**" means an event beyond the control of either Party, which prevents the Party from complying with any of its obligations under this Agreement, including but not limited to (i) act of God (such as, but not limited to, fires, explosions, earthquakes, drought, tidal waves and floods), (ii) war, hostilities (whether be declared or not), invasion, act of foreign enemies, mobilisation, requisition, or embargo, (iii) rebellion, revolution, insurrection, military or usurped power, or civil war, (iv) contamination by radioactivity from any nuclear fuel, or from any nuclear waste from the combustion of nuclear fuel, radio-active toxic explosive, or other hazardous properties of any explosive nuclear assembly or nuclear component of such assembly, (v) riot, commotion, strikes, go slows, lock outs or disorder, unless solely restricted to employees of the Contractor or of his subcontractors (vi) acts or threats of terrorism (vii) vandalism (authorities' withdrawal of permission to operate) and ice drift.

## Consequences of an Event of Force Majeure

Neither the Seller nor the Buyer shall be considered in breach of this Agreement to the extent that performance of their respective obligations (excluding payment obligations) is prevented by an Event of Force Majeure.

The Party prevented from carrying out its obligations hereunder (the "**Affected Party**") shall give notice to the other Party of an Event of Force Majeure upon it being foreseen by, or becoming known to, the Affected Party. The Affected Party shall in good faith attempt to rectify the situation.

# End-of-Life Options

Upon expiry of the Agreement according to Clause 2.4, the following shall apply:

8.1 **Seller elects to retain the Facility (extension available).** If the Seller has secured extension of the underlying permits, authorizations and any support arrangements necessary to keep the Facility in operation beyond the initial Term, and the Seller elects to retain the Facility at the Buyer's site, the Buyer shall choose between:

(a) continuing this Agreement on similar terms, save the electricity price, term of the Agreement and other parameters likely to change, to be agreed between the Parties; or

(b) requiring the Facility to be decommissioned prior to the natural end of its extended lifetime, in which case the Buyer shall bear the reasonable cost of such early decommissioning.

8.2 **Seller does not retain the Facility.** If the Seller has not secured such extension, or the Seller elects not to retain the Facility at the Buyer's site, the Seller shall decommission the Facility at the Seller's sole expense, with no cost to the Buyer.

8.3 **Purchase option.** The Parties are additionally free to negotiate a purchase of the installation by the Buyer with a discount on nominal price per kWp according to the measured degradation of the floating solar system, optionally combined with an agreement regarding support and maintenance services provided by the Seller.

## Decommissioning

Upon expiry or termination of the Agreement, and subject to Clause 8 above, the Seller shall ensure decommissioning of the Facility, which includes all necessary activities to leave the site in a similar state as it was prior to the establishment of the Facility.

The decommissioning activities involves cleaning up the site after removing the Facility, including installations such as solar panels, anchoring and mooring, cables, equipment at Buyer's electrical point of contact, inverter(s), any cabinets or sheds/shelters, signs etc.

## Obligation Upon Termination

Upon expiration or termination of this Agreement, the Parties shall have no further obligations or liabilities hereunder except for those obligations and liabilities that arise prior to such termination.

# Liability and Indemnification

This Agreement shall not give grounds for claims for damages or indemnity claims, unless a Party is in material breach of its obligations under this Agreement. In such cases, the defaulting Party must compensate the other Party's economic loss in accordance with Norwegian law of torts. However, the Seller's liability shall in any event be limited to the Seller's right to payment in the year the breach occurs.

## Dispute Resolutions

The Seller and Buyer shall use their best efforts to settle amicably all disputes arising out of or in connection with this Agreement or its interpretation.

If the Parties are unable to reach a settlement as per Clause 10.2.1 above within sixty (60) days of the first written notice on the matter of disagreement, then either Party may give notice to the other Party of its intention to commence arbitration in accordance with Clause 10.2.3.

The arbitration shall be finally settled under the Rules of Arbitration (the "Rules") of the International Chamber of Commerce in Oslo, Norway, and shall be conducted in the Norwegian language. The decision or award of the arbitration shall be in writing and is final and binding on both parties. The arbitration panel shall award the prevailing party its attorneys' fees and costs, arbitration administrative fees, panel member fees and costs, and any other costs associated with the arbitration, the enforcement of any arbitration award and the costs and attorney's fees involved in obtaining specific performance of an award; provided, however, that if the claims or defenses are granted in part and rejected in part, the arbitration panel shall proportionately allocate between the parties those arbitration expenses in accordance with the outcomes; provided, further, that the attorney's fees and costs of enforcing a specific performance arbitral award shall always be paid by the non-enforcing party, unless the applicable action was determined to be without merit by final, non-appealable decision.

The arbitration panel may only award damages as provided for under the terms of this Agreement and in no event may punitive, consequential and special damages (or as otherwise specified in this Agreement) be awarded. In the event of any conflict between the Rules and any provision of this Agreement, the provisions of the Agreement shall prevail.
