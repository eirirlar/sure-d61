Effective Date

This Agreement, when signed off by the Parties, will constitute a binding agreement upon the Parties.

Term

This Agreement shall remain in effect for and expire automatically two (2) years following the Effective
Date, unless earlier terminated in accordance with terms and conditions of this Agreement.

Delivery and Maintenance

   1. The Seller shall make solar power energy available under this Agreement at the Delivery Point, and
      the ownership and risk of loss of solar power energy delivered in accordance with this Agreement
      shall pass from the Seller to the Buyer at the Delivery Point.
   2. The Seller[/Buyer] shall install the Facility Meter at the Delivery Point to measure the amount of
      Net Electrical Output delivered by the Seller to the Buyer. The Seller[/Buyer] shall own, operate
      and maintain the Facility Meter during the entire term of this Agreement as defined in Clause
      2 (Term) at its own expense. The Facility Meter shall be a standard revenue quality meter and
      electronic data acquisition equipment that can be used continuously to measure and record the Net
      Electrical Output.
   3. The Seller shall read the Facility Meter daily and shall record the Net Electrical Output delivered
      to the Buyer. Upon written request, the Seller will make available to the Buyer the records from
      the Facility Meter.
   4. The Seller[/Buyer] shall provide standard quality calibration testing of the Facility Meter prior to
      putting it into operation and at least annually thereafter to ensure the accuracy of the Facility Meter.
      The Facility Meter shall be sealed and locked after each calibration testing. The [Seller/]Buyer
      shall be entitled to witness such tests.

In the event of damaged or faulty meters, the Seller shall make output estimates in accordance with good
faith based on historical data (if available) or predicted values.
No minimum production of Net Electrical Output shall be guaranteed delivered at the Delivery Point.
The Seller/Buyer shall ensure operation, reparation and maintenance of the Facility consistent at all times
with Prudent Utility Practice to ensure compliance with the requirements of this Agreement.


Scheduled and unscheduled outages

If possible, scheduled outages shall take place outside of generation hours and peak generation months.
The Buyer shall not be entitled to any compensation upon outages.


Curtailment, grid downtime and network maintenance

In the event of curtailment, grid downtime and network maintenance, the Buyer shall pay for the energy
which could have been produced and delivered at the Delivery Point. The Seller shall specify such
amount of energy upon invoicing.

Should the Buyer dispute an invoice, it shall notify the Seller within five (5) business days of receipt
of such invoice. The Buyer shall not be entitled to defer payment thereof beyond the date specified for
payment but shall pay such an invoice except in instances of manifest error. In the event the Parties
resolve a dispute regarding an invoice within fifteen (15) days, each Party shall be entitled to refer the

found that an error was made on the invoice, such invoice shall be canceled and thereafter a new invoice
shall be issued within ten (10) days from the resolution of such dispute.
Any amounts not paid when due (unless disputed in good faith) shall bear interest according to the Nor-
wegian act relating to interest on overdue payments.


Event of Force Majeure

Definition of an Event of Force Majeure

In this Agreement, an “Event of Force Majeure” means an event beyond the control of either Party,
which prevents the Party from complying with any of its obligations under this Agreement, including
but not limited to (i) act of God (such as, but not limited to, fires, explosions, earthquakes, drought, tidal
waves and floods), (ii) war, hostilities (whether be declared or not), invasion, act of foreign enemies,
mobilisation, requisition, or embargo, (iii) rebellion, revolution, insurrection, military or usurped power,
or civil war, (iv) contamination by radioactivity from any nuclear fuel, or from any nuclear waste from the
combustion of nuclear fuel, radio-active toxic explosive, or other hazardous properties of any explosive
nuclear assembly or nuclear component of such assembly, (v) riot, commotion, strikes, go slows, lock
outs or disorder, unless solely restricted to employees of the Contractor or of his subcontractors (vi)acts
or threats of terrorism (vii) vandalism (authorities’ withdrawal of permission to operate) and ice drift.


Consequences of an Event of Force Majeure

Neither the Seller nor the Buyer shall be considered in breach of this Agreement to the extent that perfor-
mance of their respective obligations (excluding payment obligations) is prevented by an Event of Force
Majeure.
The Party prevented from carrying out its obligations hereunder (the “Affected Party”) shall give notice
to the other Party of an Event of Force Majeure upon it being foreseen by, or becoming known to, the
Affected Party. The Affected Party shall in good faith attempt to rectify the situation.

End-of-Life Options

Upon expiry of the Agreement according to Clause 2.4, the Seller and the Buyer are free to negotiate:

  (a) an extension of the Agreement on similar terms, save the electricity price, term of the Agreement
      and other parameters likely to change; or
  (b) a purchase of the installation with a discount on nominal price per kWp according to the measured
      degradation of the floating solar system, optionally combined with an agreement regarding support
      and maintenance services provided by the Seller.


Decommissioning

Upon expiry or termination of the Agreement, the Seller shall ensure decommissioning of the Facility,
which includes all necessary activities to leave the site in a similar state as it was prior to the establishment
of the Facility.
The decommissioning activities involves cleaning up the site after removing the Facility, including in-
stallations such as solar panels, anchoring and mooring, cables, equipment at Buyer’s electrical point of
contact, inverter(s), any cabinets or sheds/shelters, signs etc.


Obligation Upon Termination

Upon expiration or termination of this Agreement, the Parties shall have no further obligations or liabil-
ities hereunder except for those obligations and liabilities that arise prior to such termination.


Liability and Indemnification

This Agreement shall not give grounds for claims for damages or indemnity claims, unless a Party is
in material breach of its obligations under this Agreement. In such cases, the defaulting Party must
compensate the other Party’s economic loss in accordance with Norwegian law of torts. However, the
Seller’s liability shall in any event be limited to the Seller’s right to payment in the year the breach
occurs.


Miscellaneous

Governing Law

This Agreement and the rights and obligations hereunder shall in all respects be interpreted, construed
and governed by Norwegian law.

Dispute Resolutions

The Seller and Buyer shall use their best efforts to settle amicably all disputes arising out of or in con-
nection with this Agreement or its interpretation.
If the Parties are unable to reach a settlement as per Clause 10.2.1 above within sixty (60) days of the
first written notice on the matter of disagreement, then either Party may give notice to the other Party of
its intention to commence arbitration in accordance with Clause 10.2.3.
The arbitration shall be finally settled under the Rules of Arbitration (the “Rules”) of the International
Chamber of Commerce in Oslo, Norway, and shall be conducted in the Norwegian language. The deci-
sion or award of the arbitration shall be in writing and is final and binding on both parties. The arbitration
panel shall award the prevailing party its attorneys’ fees and costs, arbitration administrative fees, panel
member fees and costs, and any other costs associated with the arbitration, the enforcement of any arbi-
tration award and the costs and attorney’s fees involved in obtaining specific performance of an award;
provided, however, that if the claims or defenses are granted in part and rejected in part, the arbitration
panel shall proportionately allocate between the parties those arbitration expenses in accordance with the
outcomes; provided, further, that the attorney’s fees and costs of enforcing a specific performance arbi-
tral award shall always be paid by the non-enforcing party, unless the applicable action was determined
to be without merit by final, non-appealable decision.
The arbitration panel may only award damages as provided for under the terms of this Agreement and in
no event may punitive, consequential and special damages (or as otherwise specified in this Agreement)
be awarded. In the event of any conflict between the Rules and any provision of this Agreement, the
provisions of the Agreement shall prevail.


Language

The language of this Agreement shall be English. All documents, notices and all other communication
written or otherwise between the Parties in connection with this Agreement shall be in English.


Confidentiality

The contents of this Agreement including appendices and confidential information about another Party’s
business and operations must not be made known to third parties or other unauthorized persons without
the other Party’s prior written confirmation. This obligation continues to apply for 3 years after the
Agreement has been terminated.
The confidentiality obligation does not include publicly available information, information which must
be presented to courts or public authorities or others pursuant to legislative acts, regulations or decisions
made on grounds of legislative authority. Furthermore, the Parties shall be entitled to inform advisors
about the content of this Agreement. Neither Party shall issue or cause the publication of any press release
or other public announcement in relation to the Facility or this Agreement without the prior approval of
the other Party.
