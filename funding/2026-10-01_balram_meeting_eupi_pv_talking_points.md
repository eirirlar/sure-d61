# Talking points — Balram / SINTEF meeting on HORIZON-CL5-2026-11-D3-14

## Context (one-liner)

Balram has flagged a ~€7.5M floating-PV call with a December deadline. It is HORIZON-CL5-2026-11-D3-14 (EUPI-PV Partnership). Sunlit Sea evaluated this call internally on 2026-08-11 and concluded: good fit as technology-provider partner, not as lead applicant. This meeting is the opening we hoped for.

## What the call requires

- €7.5M lump-sum per project, 4 projects funded (2 offshore/nearshore PV, 2 agriPV).
- Deadline 2026-12-01, single stage.
- Projects must reach TRL 7-8 by end of project.
- At least 5 MW of operational installations across multiple EU climate or sea zones (i.e. at least two distinct EU sea areas, not two sites in one country).
- 8-12 partners recommended, with mandatory roles: SSH expert, permitting expert, utility/grid operator, precision-monitoring SME, PV system integrator, marine/offshore engineer.
- Lump-sum model: payment on milestone acceptance, not cost reimbursement — under-delivery = no release.

## Goals for this meeting

1. Understand SINTEF's intent: coordinating, joining, or just scouting.
2. Learn what consortium shape they see — who is in, who is still open.
3. Explore how this fits alongside EIC Transition SUREWAVE (complementary vs. overlapping scope).
4. Signal Sunlit Sea's interest as FPV-technology partner for one demonstration site, without taking coordination load.

## Questions to ask Balram

- Is SINTEF taking a coordinator role, or joining a consortium led by someone else? If the latter — who?
- Which EU zones are being targeted for the 5 MW? Is there already a Southern-European site anchor (Portugal/Spain/Italy)?
- Which mandatory roles (SSH, permitting, utility, monitoring SME) are already filled, and which are still open?
- How does SINTEF see the relationship to EIC Transition SUREWAVE — complementary pilot (100-300 kWp integrated FPV+breakwater) vs. utility-scale demonstration (≥5 MW standalone FPV)? Can both be defended if both are awarded?
- What role is SINTEF envisioning for Sunlit Sea? Hardware supplier for one site, WP contributor, or something broader?
- Timeline: when does the consortium want partner commitments locked, and when does writing start in earnest?

## Our position (what to signal, what to avoid)

What we offer:
- FPV units for one demonstration site, with Gunneklevfjorden (~3.2 MWp) as the realistic Norwegian nearshore slice. Storavatnet is blocked by HNP-omregulering until ~2028 and should not be put on the table for this project window.
- Contribution to 1-2 work packages on design and factory integration.
- Hardware budget in the €0.5-1.5M range, consortium-negotiated.
- Our EIC Transition SUREWAVE application already lifts gen 2 toward TRL 6 by end 2027 — compatible with entering this project at TRL 5-6 and reaching TRL 7-8 by 2029-2030, provided EIC Transition is awarded and delivers.

What we do not offer:
- Coordinator role. Our capacity does not support it.
- Lead-partner role on the full €7.5M.
- A commitment conditional on anything other than EIC Transition being awarded.

What to flag honestly:
- Our TRL 7-8 reachability depends on EIC Transition SUREWAVE being awarded. If it is not, our contribution would be reduced to a gen 1 reference or a slower gen 2 ramp.
- Writing bandwidth is tight: EIC Transition submission was 2026-09-16; this call deadline is 10-11 weeks later. We can contribute WP text but not carry a coordinator writing load.
- Scope boundary vs. SUREWAVE and EIC Transition SUREWAVE must be explicit in the proposal to avoid double-funding challenges.

## Related: CENER unsolicited approach

Joseba Merino (CENER, Spanish RTO) emailed us 2026-09-30 asking to join a proposal for the same topic. CENER has credible FPV / offshore wind capability and could plausibly fill a BoS, testing, or Spanish demonstration-site slot.

If Balram's consortium has open slots that CENER could fill (particularly a Southern-European anchor), useful to flag CENER to SINTEF rather than respond to CENER directly. If SINTEF's consortium is already set, we can park CENER politely.
