# Sunlit Sea SuRE extension — Task 6.4 pilot at Gunneklevfjorden

Sunlit Sea's Task 6.4 pilot in SuRE is delivered at Gunneklevfjorden — a commercial near-shore FPV installation Sunlit Sea is developing at Herøya Industrial Park in Porsgrunn, Norway. The pilot sits inside the commercial plant as an instrumented demonstrator, with an integrated monitoring campaign that feeds field data into the consortium's model chains through the remainder of the SuRE project term. The additional grant of approximately 470 000 EUR at the SuRE funding rate of 70 % covers this extension of Sunlit Sea's WP6 activities.

## Proposition

Under the extension, Sunlit Sea's WP6 scope covers the site-specific adaptation of the float and mooring design to the Gunneklevfjorden environment, the installation of a research-instrumented demonstrator inside the commercial plant, and a monitoring campaign that runs through the remainder of the SuRE project term. D6.4 is delivered as a quality-controlled field-data package from the Gunneklevfjorden demonstrator, made available to the SuRE monitoring and modelling work packages.

The scope covers the research layer of the installation only. The commercial plant itself — modules, floats, mounting hardware, mooring, inverters, grid-tie switchgear — is financed independently through revenue from the power-purchase agreement, Sunlit Sea equity, and national support instruments available for renewable-energy investment in Norway.

## Feasibility

### Near-shore setting

Gunneklevfjorden is an inner-fjord basin adjacent to the industrial harbour at Herøya. It sits inside the near-shore envelope Sunlit Sea targets for first commercial deployment — sheltered enough for reliable operation at a scale that supports a power-purchase agreement, exposed enough to real environmental variability that laboratory conditions cannot reproduce. Wave conditions are wind-fetch driven: the fetch across the fjord basin is bounded by the surrounding topography rather than by open coast, so wave build-up under strong winds is limited by fetch length rather than by open-sea swell arriving from outside.

The demonstrator sits alongside two validation strands already scoped in the grant: the wave-tank testing named in Task 6.4, which verifies simulated results and the final design, and the WP2 activities under SO4 that validate the design up to SSC 5. The demonstrator delivers what neither wave tank nor model can — a full seasonal cycle of real-water data on parameters lab and model conditions cannot reproduce: mooring behaviour on a capped seabed, brackish-water electrochemistry, thermal cycling in cold-climate operation, wind-driven wave and current loading, biofouling, and long-duration commercial-plant integration. Together the three cover the validation envelope for Sunlit Sea's commercial platform.

### Site rights and permits

- Herøya Industrial Park's project company holds the site rights and has contracted Sunlit Sea as the FPV technology provider under a develop-operate-sell arrangement.
- The Norwegian Environment Agency has confirmed jurisdiction over the marine permit and has accepted subsurface anchoring on the capped seabed left by Hydro's earlier remediation of the fjord.
- The Municipality of Porsgrunn has confirmed willingness to process the building application under the existing regulatory framework.

### Grid and power off-take

- The industrial park's grid operator has confirmed grid capacity and identified the physical connection points on the network.
- Power off-take is arranged through the industrial park, where existing industrial demand on the park provides long-term commercial security for plant operation.

### Engineering and execution

- A Norwegian solar-project engineering firm is contracted for detailed engineering, permit processing, electrical installation, and post-installation operations and maintenance.
- Marine works are contracted to a specialist marine subcontractor familiar with mooring installation on capped seabeds.
- Sunlit Sea has been operating a 105 kWp first-generation FPV installation at Skiftestjørna in Haugaland Industrial Park since autumn 2024. Skiftestjørna is Sunlit Sea's own asset under a commercial power-purchase agreement, and it is the operational reference on which the Gunneklevfjorden design draws.

### Financing of the commercial plant

- The plant itself is financed outside the SuRE budget through PPA revenue, Sunlit Sea equity, and national support instruments available for renewable-energy investment in Norway. The SuRE budget covers only the research layer described in the Activities section.

## Activities enabled by the extension

The extension defines six activities within Sunlit Sea's WP6 scope, all bounded by the SuRE project term.

### Activity A — Site-specific design adaptation

Adaptation of the float and mooring configuration to the Gunneklevfjorden environmental envelope: wave, wind and ice loading, brackish-water chemistry, and the capped-seabed mooring constraint. The mooring engineering is documented as a stand-alone note that can be cited by later capped-seabed FPV deployments — a piece of transferable engineering learning that emerges directly from the site conditions.

Owner: Sunlit Sea CTO, with the engineering subcontractor for geotechnical inputs.  
Output: updated float and mooring design pack for the site.

### Activity B — Monitoring-package specification and procurement

Specification of a research-grade monitoring package covering meteorology (irradiance, wind, wave proxy, water temperature), structural and mooring loads, and additional string-level electrical monitoring beyond the commercial plant's standard instrumentation. Procurement is by twelve-month rental from commercial vendors, keeping the SuRE budget clear of long-lived asset costs and confining instrumentation to the period of research use.

Owner: Sunlit Sea monitoring and data lead.  
Output: monitoring package under rental contract, with vendor calibration certificates.

### Activity C — Demonstrator installation

Physical installation of the SuRE-instrumented demonstrator at Gunneklevfjorden. Marine works — mooring installation on the capped seabed, install vessel, diver time — are executed by the marine subcontractor. Grid tie and low-voltage electrical works are executed by the engineering subcontractor. Sunlit Sea supervises the installation and takes over the completed system.

Owner: Sunlit Sea site and commissioning engineer.  
Output: demonstrator in the water, mechanically and electrically complete.

### Activity D — Commissioning and data-system integration

Commissioning of the instrumentation, on-site calibration, integration into the SuRE data platform, and end-to-end validation of data delivery into the consortium's workflows.

Owner: Sunlit Sea monitoring and data lead, with CTO support.  
Output: commissioning report and validated data pipeline.

### Activity E — Monitoring campaign and data delivery

Operational monitoring generating quality-controlled field data across environmental, mechanical, mooring, and electrical dimensions. Rolling data delivery to the modelling partners in a form directly usable by the WP1, WP2, and WP3 model chains. Monitoring continues beyond the SuRE project period at Sunlit Sea's operational cost on the working plant, with data continuing to be shared with the consortium under the existing data-sharing arrangements.

Owner: Sunlit Sea monitoring and data lead.  
Output: quality-controlled field-data package delivered on a rolling basis into the SuRE data platform, with a consolidated first-period dataset as the closing D6.4 deliverable.

### Activity F — Documentation, dissemination and consortium reporting

Two case-study reports on the topics of highest external interest arising from the demonstrator: FPV performance in cold-climate brackish near-shore conditions, and mooring engineering for capped contaminated seabeds.

Owner: Sunlit Sea CEO with the CTO and monitoring lead.  
Output: two case-study reports, a factsheet, site signage, and one open-day event at the demonstrator.

## Contribution to SuRE work packages and deliverables

The extension delivers and extends the following items already committed in the grant agreement:

- Task 6.4 / D6.4 (extended). *Prototype production and field deployment.* The Gunneklevfjorden demonstrator delivers D6.4 at enlarged scale and with an integrated monitoring campaign not present in the original standalone-pilot scoping.
- WP6 objective O6.2.1. *Optimized selection of new floater generation prototype.* D6.4 at Gunneklevfjorden closes the loop from D6.1 (Sunlit model chain — mechanical load, heat transfer, float production, LCA) and D6.2 (Pareto frontier mapping and prototype candidate selection) into field validation of the second-generation design.
- Sunlit Sea's technology outcome as committed in the grant proposal — *Improved integration of floater and module for FPV application* — is advanced by the demonstrator. Operational evidence supports the KPI target of up to 60 % LCOE reduction, and the SSC 5 design envelope committed under SO4 is validated across the pair of demonstrator and wave-tank programme.

The demonstrator also feeds three consortium work packages beyond WP6:

- WP1 — Environmental monitoring concepts (Deltares). The monitoring package is designed against the concept catalogue delivered in D1.1 and adds a cold-climate near-shore reference to the WP1 dataset.
- WP2 — Stress and degradation modelling (IFE, Fraunhofer, TNO). Structural and mooring load data from the Sunlit Sea FPV platform, in near-shore conditions distinct from the reservoir sites in the consortium, feed the multiscale mechanical models.
- WP3 — Performance-loss quantification (IFE). String-level electrical monitoring combined with the meteorological data allows separation of thermal, soiling, and mismatch losses in cold-climate near-shore operation — a regime under-represented in the current SuRE dataset.

## Team and execution capacity

Sunlit Sea has been developing FPV since 2019, with freedom to operate verified for the current design. The first-generation platform is operational at 105 kWp Skiftestjørna in Haugaland Industrial Park — commissioned in autumn 2024 under Sunlit Sea's own develop-operate-sell arrangement, in continuous operation since — and is the direct operational reference used in the Gunneklevfjorden design work. The Gunneklevfjorden pilot delivers the second-generation design developed under SuRE WP6.

Local execution at Gunneklevfjorden is delivered by a Norwegian engineering subcontractor for detailed engineering, permit processing, electrical works and post-installation operations, and by a specialist marine subcontractor for the mooring installation. The Sunlit Sea team on the extension comprises the CEO, CTO, a site and commissioning engineer, and a monitoring and data lead — approximately 2.6 FTE over the remaining project period, executing Activities A through F.

## Timeline

The schedule is anchored to the date of CINEA's approval of the budget increase (T0). Anchoring to the funding decision, rather than to fixed calendar dates, ensures that activities are not shown scheduled to start before funding is in place.

| Period | Activities |
|---|---|
| T0 to T+3 months | Site-specific design adaptation (A); monitoring-package specification and procurement (B); contracting of marine works |
| T+3 to T+6 months | Design pack completed; permits closed out; monitoring instrumentation delivered; component preparation |
| T+6 to T+9 months | Demonstrator installation (C); commissioning and data-system integration (D) |
| T+9 months through SuRE project end | Monitoring campaign and data delivery (E); dissemination (F) |
| After SuRE project end, on Sunlit Sea's operational responsibility | Continued monitoring on the working plant, with data continuing to be shared with the consortium |

The activities fit within the SuRE project term and no extension of the project term is requested. If T0 lands late enough that the full T+9-month execution window would run past project end, Sunlit Sea coordinates scope compression with IFE, retaining the monitoring campaign as first priority.

## Risks and mitigations

- Approval timing. If CINEA approval arrives late enough that the T+9-month execution window would extend past the SuRE project end, Sunlit Sea coordinates scope compression with IFE, retaining the monitoring campaign as first priority and reducing the duration of the in-project monitoring window as needed.
- Marine installation weather window. Installation on the capped seabed is subject to sea state and vessel availability. Marine subcontracting is scoped with a weather margin appropriate to the site's seasonal sea-state, within the T+6 to T+9-month installation band.
- Commercial-plant dependency. The demonstrator is embedded in the commercial plant. If the commercial plant's build schedule slips beyond the SuRE project term, Sunlit Sea deploys the demonstrator on a stand-alone basis using the same mooring and monitoring design, preserving the D6.4 deliverable independent of the commercial timeline.
